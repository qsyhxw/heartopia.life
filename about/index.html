🎮 About Heartopia

Heartopia is a cozy life simulation game developed and published by XD Entertainment. The game launched globally on January 7, 2026, and is available on PC (Steam), iOS, and Android.

In Heartopia, players can fish, garden, cook, catch insects, photograph birds, care for pets, and build their dream home while befriending charming NPCs in a beautiful world.
