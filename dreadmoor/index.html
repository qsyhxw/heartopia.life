ABOUT THE GAME

A trawler. Murky water.
A world to piece together.

DREADMOOR is Dream Dock's first-person fishing adventure, published by Digital Vortex Entertainment. Explore flooded settlements, catch and trade fish, craft equipment and upgrade your boat while facing threats on land and water.

The Windows PC game entered Steam Early Access on 9 November 2026. Features and quest behavior can change during development.
