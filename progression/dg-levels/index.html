🎖️ What is DG Level?

DG (Developer Grade), also called the D.G. Members Guild, is Heartopia's central progression system. Think of it as your main character level.

DG Level Unlocks:

  • → New Hobbies
  • → Events & Activities
  • → Increased Energy Capacity
  • → Home Plot Expansions
  • → New Furniture & Recipes
  • → Story Content

⚠️ Time-Gated Progression

DG leveling is intentionally time-gated. You can only earn a limited number of Contribution Medals per day, so consistent daily play is more important than grinding sessions.

📈 How to Level Up

Contribution Medals = XP

Contribution Medals are the experience points for your DG level. Earn enough medals to level up!

🔑 Main Source: Resident Requests

  • • 5 requests available per day
  • • Each request gives 10 Contribution Medals
  • • Maximum of 50 medals daily from requests
  • • Resets at 6:00 AM (server time)

Types of Resident Requests:

  • 📸 Taking photos of specific locations or items
  • 🎣 Catching specific fish
  • 🦋 Catching specific insects
  • 📦 Delivering items to NPCs
  • 🌾 Collecting resources

Other Medal Sources

  • 🏆 Event Rankings — Top ranks in minigame events
  • 🏠 Hosting Guests — Invite friends to your home
  • 📖 Town Guide Missions — Complete special objectives
  • 📅 Weekly Requests — Available at DG Level 13+

⏱️ Time to DG6 (First Major Milestone)

Reaching DG Level 6 takes approximately 15-20 days of consistent daily play. This unlocks Cooking, which is the best early-game money maker!

🎫 Hobby Expansion Ticket Summary

You receive tickets at DG Levels 2, 3, 5, 8, and 11. Each hobby requires one ticket to unlock, so plan your unlocks carefully!

💡 Progression Tips

✅ Do

  • • Complete all 5 Resident Requests daily — missing days slows progress significantly
  • • Check requests at 6 AM — get a head start on the day
  • • Save Hobby Tickets for Level 6 — unlock Cooking first for profit
  • • Prepare materials in advance — some requests need specific items
  • • Level hobbies alongside DG — many events require hobby levels

❌ Don't

  • • Skip daily requests — you can't make up for lost medals
  • • Use Hobby Tickets randomly — plan your unlock order
  • • Expect to rush — the system is designed for daily play
  • • Ignore the calendar icon — that's where requests are found!

🔍 How to Access DG Menu

  1. 1. Open your in-game watch/smartwatch
  2. 2. Find the D.G. Members Guild icon
  3. 3. View your current Contribution Medals and progress
  4. 4. When you have enough medals, press the upgrade button (costs some Gold)

📅 Resident Requests: Look for the calendar icon in the top-left of your screen to find daily requests from NPCs.
