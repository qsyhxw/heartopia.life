⭐ The Golden Rule: Process Before Selling

Never sell raw materials!

Cooking raw ingredients into dishes doubles or triples their value. This is the single most important money-making tip in Heartopia.

Example: Blueberry → Jam Profit

Item Value
4× Blueberry (raw) ~64 Gold
1× Blueberry Jam (1-star) 170 Gold
1× Blueberry Jam (2-star) 255 Gold
1× Mixed Jam (3-star) 320 Gold

🍳 Cooking for Profit

Cooking is the most profitable hobby in Heartopia. Unlock it at DG Level 6 by speaking to Massimo at his café.

💰 High-Profit Recipes

Recipe Ingredients Sell Price Profit
Fish & Chips Potato + Fish 310 Gold +170
Fried Crucian Carp Crucian Carp 350 Gold +270
Jam (any fruit) 4× Fruit 170 Gold +100+
Ratatouille Mixed Vegetables High Great
Corn Soup Corn + Milk High Great

💡 Cooking Tips

  • ✓ Use 1-star ingredients — they have better profit margins
  • ✓ Set up 3 cooking pots for efficient batch cooking
  • ✓ Latte Trick: Buy milk + coffee → make latte → sell for profit
  • ✓ Cook low-value fish (100 Gold or less) into dishes
  • ✗ Don't cook high-value fish — sell them raw

🌻 Farming Strategies

Reach Gardening Level 2 to unlock better crops. Focus on high-value seeds for maximum returns.

✅ Do

  • • Plant Potatoes over Tomatoes (+60-100 Gold/harvest)
  • • Water crops consistently
  • • Process crops into dishes before selling
  • • Forage wild fruits for free jam ingredients

❌ Don't

  • • Sell raw crops without cooking
  • • Plant cheap seeds like Tomatoes
  • • Forget to harvest before logging out

🌽 Advanced: Corn-Strawberry Rotation

Plant corn before bed (12-hour growth) → harvest in morning → plant strawberries (6-hour growth) → harvest and replant. This method can potentially earn over 100,000 Gold daily at higher levels.

🎣 Fishing for Gold

Reach Fishing Level 2 to increase high-star fish catches. Know which fish to sell raw vs. cook.

Fish Selling Strategy

💰 Sell Raw (200+ Gold)

High-value and 3-star fish should be sold directly to Albert Jr. for maximum value.

🍳 Cook First (100 Gold or less)

Low-value fish like Crucian Carp should be cooked into dishes (e.g., 80g → 350g).

🌈 Rainbow Fish — 5,000 Gold Each!

During rainbow weather, go to Starlight Sea and use premium bait. Rainbow Fish sell for an incredible 5,000 Gold each!

🛒 Selling to Albert Jr.

Albert Jr. is the wandering merchant who buys all your items. She doesn't have a fixed location — find her on the map!

How to Find Albert Jr.

  1. 1 Press M to open the map
  2. 2 Look for Albert Jr.'s icon (she moves around town)
  3. 3 Walk/drive to her, or take the bus (200 Gold)

📅 Daily Money-Making Routine

  1. 1
    Morning: Harvest & Plant

    Harvest overnight crops, replant high-value seeds

  2. 2
    Forage Fruits

    Collect apples, berries, mushrooms around town

  3. 3
    Fish While Waiting

    Use bait at home pond, collect low and high-value fish

  4. 4
    Cook Everything

    Turn fruits → jam, low-value fish → dishes, crops → meals

  5. 5
    Sell to Albert Jr.

    Find her on map, sell all cooked dishes + high-value raw fish

  6. 6
    Sleep to Restore Energy (Not Food!)

    Log out while in bed for full energy on next login

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • ✗ Eating cooked food for stamina — Sleep instead to save sellable items!
  • ✗ Selling raw materials — Always process into dishes first
  • ✗ Cooking high-value fish — Sell 200+ Gold fish raw
  • ✗ Using expensive ingredients in cheap recipes — Check ingredient values first
  • ✗ Planting cheap seeds — Potatoes beat Tomatoes by 60-100 Gold
