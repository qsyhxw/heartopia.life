Loftia Secret Lake route and preparation

Secret Lake is east of Overgrowth and north of Windmill Island in the Hanging Gardens. The story route uses the Glider Propeller. Players also report an earlier climb with Bounce Shoes and a Glider.

Choose your access route

The Early Access district record ↗ separates the later story Propeller from the reported Overgrowth climb. If you prefer the story route, continue Gridfall progression. The climb below is a community route and may depend on current collision and movement behavior.

Reported Overgrowth climb

  1. From Lower Village, head north into Overgrowth. Clear boulders with Hammer Lv.2 and brambles with Pruners Lv.2.
  2. At the two banana trees, use the mushroom on their right with Bounce Shoes.
  3. Head right toward the NPC wearing a pink bunny hat and climb the platform behind her.
  4. Beyond the log, climb the rocks, turn left and follow grassy mountain edges.
  5. Jump toward the tree-bearing cliff, then run, jump and glide to the lake.

This route is reported by Kirthana K with credited player images. Route walkthrough ↗

Decide whether the trip serves your target

For Nassau Grouper or Giant Sea Bass, use the ordinary lake area. For Moapa Dace, use Overgrowth. The Secret Lake listing instead includes blind cave species and mineral catches. Check the fish-area guide before investing in the journey. Ivy also has other Hanging Gardens locations, as explained in Materials.
