How to get your first pet in Loftia

Continue the Hanging Gardens story to Joanna's Notes From the Fauna quest. Find five dog clues, bring 4 Lettuce and 2 Carrots to the Pet Bowl, adopt the dog and report back.

Prepare before following clues

During Kassim's Field Research, speak with Joanna and accept the missing-dog task. Bring the vegetables before heading toward The Grove. Inspect disturbed-ground dog tracks until the Dog Location Piece counter reaches five, then follow the bowl objective. Dog rescue walkthrough ↗

Outfits and adoption

An outfit that asks you to unlock its pet does not perform the adoption. Complete the rescue first, then choose your companion. Players asking why they had no pet after reaching the Airtram were directed to the Hanging Gardens main quest. Progression question ↗
