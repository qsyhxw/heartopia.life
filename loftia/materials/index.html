Loftia materials chain for ivy, lignin and carbon fiber

Bioluminescent Ivy needs Pruners Lv.2. Refine 5 ivy into Lignin at a Refining Station Lv.2. Carbon Fiber requires a later gathering route, so check your tools before searching.

Ivy and lignin preparation

The level-two refining upgrade comes from completing a page of Central Lake research bundles. Building it needs 10 Screws, 2 Metal Plate and 2 Plank. Owning ivy alone is insufficient for this craft. Station and lignin data ↗

The ivy regional listing ↗ includes Overgrowth, Secret Lake and Bamboo Forest. Try accessible Overgrowth nodes before treating Secret Lake access as mandatory. See our fish-area checklist for research collecting.

Fiber before and after the Locator

With Hammer Lv.3, check Lockbox Hammer nodes or Piles of Cars. Once you have a Locator, Residential Artifacts become another option. The Locator itself consumes 2 Carbon Fiber, so its artifact route cannot supply your first crafting pair. The Art of Letting Go awards fiber after the Locator crafting step. Gathering and quest chain ↗

Budget another 3 Carbon Fiber for Rail Boots. Reserve the Locator pair separately if pursuing both unlocks.

Adobe is the earlier station task

Gather Clay Mounds by hand and refine clay at the basic station. The station recipe comes during Permission to Glide and costs 5 Screws plus 3 Scrap Wire to build. This branch can precede the lignin upgrade. Adobe recipe ↗
