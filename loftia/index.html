BEFORE YOU LOG IN

The essentials

Loftia is Qloud Games’ online life simulation, with farming, decorating, fishing and shared adventures. Early Access opened on November 3, 2026.

PC and Mac are available through Steam and Epic. An internet connection is required, even for solo play. Nintendo Switch is planned for 2027; a separate Switch 2 release is not confirmed.
