What the purchase decision depends on

The same repeated-material loop attracts some players and puts others off. If collecting for another craft sounds satisfying, look at the materials chain to see an example of the progression gates. If that sounds like work, waiting for further development may suit your preferences.

In the parallel buying discussion, peppermintblue described enjoyment after eight Early Access hours, following substantial Demo and beta play. MayaDaBee1250 advised uncertain buyers to wait. These are individual opinions from a self-selected discussion, with no representative satisfaction score implied.
