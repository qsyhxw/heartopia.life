Loftia pineapple seeds, growth time and unlock checks

Buy Pineapple Seed at Hazel’s Seed Shop after its story unlock, then grow it in a Garden Bed. Current community records list 30 Dew per seed and a two-hour growth time.

Seed shop or wrong container?

The Codex pineapple record ↗ names Everything Is Connected as the main-quest unlock and lists Hazel as the seed vendor. If seeds are absent, check story progress before searching the wilderness. Pineapple is a Garden Bed crop.

Plan a small planting

The November 6 Early Access crop guide ↗ lists a 120-minute growth time, 30-Dew seed cost and 60-Dew ordinary harvest value. For four seeds, that means 120 Dew up front and 240 Dew gross harvest value, giving a 120-Dew difference before any other costs. This calculation assumes four ordinary successful harvests and excludes shiny bonuses.

Check the seed price and timer in your client before scaling up. Older beta-oriented guides list six hours and different prices, so their budgets do not fit this record.

Keep enough for your immediate task

Pineapple is used in Pineapple Cake, Pineapple Fried Rice, Pineapple Skewer and Tropical Smoothie, and in Sweet and Spicy research. Check the research-station request before selling your whole harvest. Recipe availability is a separate gate from growing the crop.
