Loftia Rail Boots recipe and unlock checks

Claim the Rail Boots recipe from Electric Dreams at the Museum Research Station. Craft it at Project Station Lv.3 using 5 Magnets and 3 Carbon Fiber.

Separate the quest from the reward

A November 7 player thread first points to Leela's Slip, Zip, Hooray quest in Gridfall. Its follow-up identifies Electric Dreams at the Museum Research Station as the recipe source. If you already did the dance step, check that research reward. Quest discussion ↗

Crafting checklist

  1. Check Electric Dreams and claim its recipe reward.
  2. Confirm Project Station Lv.3.
  3. Set aside 5 Magnets and 3 Carbon Fiber.
  4. Craft the boots and follow the quest instruction to try an air rail.

The recipe record ↗ gives the tier and ingredients. Our materials chain explains the first-fiber gate.
