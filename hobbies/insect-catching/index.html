🔓 How to Unlock Insect Catching

  1. 1
    Reach DG Level 6

    Complete daily Resident Requests to level up your Developer Grade

  2. 2
    Get a Hobby Expansion Ticket

    Earned at DG Levels 2, 3, 5 (save one for Level 6!)

  3. 3
    Visit Naniwa

    Find Naniwa near the river in their garden

  4. ✓
    Receive Bubble Net

    Naniwa gives you the bubble net and teaches the basics

💡 Tip: Consider unlocking Cooking first at DG Level 6 for better profit. Insect Catching is more relaxing but less profitable early on.

🫧 Catching Mechanics

How to Catch Insects

  1. 1. Equip the Bubble Net from your toolbox
  2. 2. Approach slowly — don't startle the insect!
  3. 3. Cast a bubble to capture:

⚡ Quick Bubble

Tap action button for a fast bubble in facing direction. Good for nearby bugs.

🎯 Charged Bubble

Hold action button to enter stealth mode. Walk slowly, charge up, release for precise capture.

Insect Spawn Factors

  • 📍 Location — Forest, Flower Field, Onsen Mountain, etc.
  • ⏰ Time — Day (6AM-6PM), Night (6PM-6AM), or All Day
  • 🌤️ Weather — Sunny, Rainy, or Rainbow
  • 📊 Catching Level — Higher levels = rarer insects

How to Use

  1. 1 Go to a good location — Onsen Mountain, Fishing Village, or areas with insect spawn points
  2. 2 Open inventory and select the Inflatable Insect Attractor
  3. 3 Place the attractor — rare insects will spawn!
  4. 4 Catch Sulkowsky's Morpho with your Bubble Net!

💡 Tips & Tricks

✅ Do

  • • Check weather conditions — Rainbow = best for rare insects!
  • • Hunt at night (6PM-6AM) for Hercules Beetle
  • • Use charged bubbles for tricky insects
  • • Join Bait the Insects Event (DG Level 8+) for rare bugs
  • • Keep one of each insect for encyclopedia completion
  • • Buy Inflatable Insect Attractor from Naniwa for rare spawns

❌ Don't

  • • Run at insects — you'll scare them away!
  • • Sell all rare insects — save for encyclopedia first
  • • Ignore weather — some insects only appear in rain/rainbow
  • • Forget about events — they offer exclusive rare bugs

🎉 Bait the Insects Event

🦗

Special Event for Rare Insects

  • Unlock: DG Level 8 + Insect Catching hobby
  • Location: Onsen Mountain, Crater Lake
  • Challenge: Catch as many insects as possible in time limit
  • Rewards: Rare insects, Contribution Medals, event prizes

Check event schedule in-game for times!
