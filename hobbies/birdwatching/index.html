🔓 How to Unlock Birdwatching

  1. 1
    Reach DG Level 6

    Complete daily Resident Requests to level up

  2. 2
    Get a Hobby Expansion Ticket

    Earned at DG Levels 2, 3, 5

  3. 3
    Visit Bailey J

    Find Bailey J above the Pet Shop in the Central Plaza

  4. ✓
    Receive Bird Scanner

    Bailey J gives you the scanner (camera) to photograph birds

💡 Tip: Birdwatching is more relaxing than profitable. If you want Gold, unlock Cooking first at DG Level 6.

📷 Photography Mechanics

How to Photograph Birds

  1. 1. Equip the Bird Scanner from your tools menu
  2. 2. Find a bird in forests, lakes, villages, etc.
  3. 3. Approach carefully — don't get too close or you'll scare it!
  4. 4. Aim and focus — use the zoom slider, wait for the focus ring
  5. 5. Snap the photo to create an Info Card

⭐ How to Get Higher Star Photos

  • 📸 Capture bird actions — wait for chirping, foraging, or flying
  • 🎯 Center the bird perfectly in frame
  • 🔍 Zoom in close without scaring the bird
  • 😴 Idling birds = 1 star — wait for actions!

🎴 Info Card Recycling

What is Info Card Recycling?

Instead of selling bird photos for minimal Gold, recycle them with Bailey J for much better rewards!

📋 Requirements

  • • Birdwatching Level 2 (200 Proficiency)
  • • Visit Bailey J above Pet Shop

📦 Rewards

  • • Repair Kits
  • • Quality Timber
  • • Other crafting materials

⚠️ Daily Limit

You can recycle maximum 5 Info Cards per day. Higher star cards may give better rewards!

💡 Pro Tip

Don't sell cards before Level 2! Save them for recycling — the rewards are much better than Gold.

💡 Tips & Tricks

✅ Do

  • • Wait for bird actions — chirping/foraging = higher stars
  • • Check weather daily — Rainbow = best for rare birds
  • • Save cards until Level 2 — recycle instead of sell
  • • Visit Mountain Peak at midday for Golden Eagle
  • • Hunt at night in winter for Snowy Owl
  • • Give photos to Bailey J for friendship points

❌ Don't

  • • Run at birds — approach slowly!
  • • Sell Info Cards before unlocking recycling
  • • Photo idle birds — wait for actions for more stars
  • • Ignore weather — some birds require specific conditions
