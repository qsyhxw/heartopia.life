🐕 All Heartopia Dog Breeds List (2026)

Looking for your dream dog? Here is the complete list of all 11 dog breeds available in Heartopia, organized by size and type!

📅 Updated: Feb 2026 | 🐶 Total Breeds: 11

🐾 Small Breeds

🦊

Corgi

Short legs, big personality. Famous for their "sploot" and round bottoms.

🐩

Poodle

Elegant and intelligent. Available in standard white curly coat.

🐶

Beagle

Floppy ears and a great nose. A classic friendly hound.

🐕 Medium & Unique Breeds

🐕

Shiba Inu

Independent and fox-like. The famous "Doge" breed of Heartopia.

🏁

Spotted Dog (Dalmatian)

Iconic black and white spots. High energy and distinct look.

🌾

Rural Dog

Also known as "Gardening Dog". Loyal, hardy, and down-to-earth.

🐺 Large Breeds

🦮

Golden Retriever

The ultimate friendly dog. Long golden coat and always happy.

🐕‍🦺

Labrador Retriever

Short coat, sturdy build. Loyal and great for active players.

🚔

Shepherd Dog

Resembles a German Shepherd. Intelligent, alert, and protective.

🛡️

Rottweiler

Strong and muscular with distinctive black and tan markings.

🐺

Sled Dog (Husky)

Wolf-like appearance. Often comes with the "Energetic" trait.

🧬 Personality Traits Guide

Remember: Breed does NOT determine personality! Each dog gets 2 random traits. Here is what they mean:

  • 📢
    Chatterbox

    Barks frequently. Can help find hidden items but noisy.

  • 🏃
    Energetic

    Needs lots of walks and play. Hunger drops faster.

  • 💕
    Clingy

    Follows you everywhere. Needs frequent petting.

  • 😈
    Naughty

    Prone to damaging furniture if left alone too long.

  • 🌲
    Lone Wolf

    Prefers solitude. Doesn't need as much social interaction.

  • 😴
    Sluggish

    Low energy. Needs less food but walks are slow.

  • 🥣
    Sensitive (Picky Eater)

    Harder to please with food. Requires experimenting.

  • 😐
    Indifferent

    Neutral behavior. Low maintenance.
