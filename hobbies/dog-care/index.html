🔓 How to Unlock Dog Care

⚠️ Prerequisite: You must complete Cat Care first before Dog Care becomes available!

  1. 1
    Reach DG Level 12

    Same requirement as Cat Care

  2. 2
    Unlock Cat Care hobby

    Give Hobby Expansion Ticket to Mrs. Joan

  3. 3
    Complete Cat Care tutorial

    Adopt a cat and learn basic pet care

  4. 4
    Return to Mrs. Joan

    Dog Caring option is now available!

  5. ✓
    Adopt your first dog!

    Choose from 3 daily options

🏠 Adoption Process

How to Adopt a Dog

  1. 1. Go to the Adoption Center (Mrs. Joan's location)
  2. 2. Browse the 3 available dogs (selection rotates daily)
  3. 3. Check dog's breed, gender, and personality
  4. 4. Pay the 500G adoption fee (physical exam)
  5. 5. Your dog is sent to your home!

💡 Tip: Don't like today's dogs? Check back tomorrow for a new selection of 3 dogs!

🎓 Training Commands

Unlike cats, dogs can be trained to perform commands! This is a unique feature of the Dog Caring hobby.

Command Description Benefit
🔍 Seek Treasure Dog searches for hidden items Helps find items!
🐾 Give Paw Dog offers paw to shake Increases bond
🙂 Shake Head Dog shakes head on command Cute trick!
🎾 Fetch Dog retrieves thrown items Great for play

⭐ Best Command: Seek Treasure

Train this command first! Your dog can help you find hidden items around the world — a unique passive benefit!

🧡 Daily Care Activities

Daily care builds your bond and levels up your Dog Caring hobby:

Activity How To Effect
🍽️ Feeding Give dog food or snacks Restores hunger, increases bond
🤚 Petting Interact with your dog Increases happiness & bond
🎾 Playing Fetch, toys, outdoor games Great for energetic dogs
🛁 Bathing Give your dog a bath Maintains cleanliness
🎓 Training Teach new commands Unlocks abilities!

📱 Pet App

Use the Pet app on your in-game watch to monitor your dog's hunger, energy, and happiness levels!

🎁 Rewards & Perks

As You Level Up Dog Caring:

  • 🐕 Adopt more dogs — Unlock additional pet slots
  • 🎓 New training commands — Teach advanced tricks
  • 🧴 Advanced grooming tools — Better care options
  • 🎾 Special play activities — New toys and games
  • 🔍 Passive item finding — Dogs help find hidden items!
  • ❤️ Deeper bond perks — Unlock special interactions

🐕 vs 🐱 Dog Caring Advantage

Dogs have training commands that cats don't! The Seek Treasure ability is especially useful for finding items.

💡 Tips & Tricks

✅ Do

  • • Complete Cat Care first — Required to unlock dogs
  • • Train Seek Treasure early — Most useful command
  • • Check daily — New dogs appear every day
  • • Care consistently — Daily interaction levels up faster
  • • Use the Pet app — Monitor hunger/energy before they drop

❌ Don't

  • • Skip Cat Care — You cannot unlock dogs without it!
  • • Neglect your dog — Happiness drops if ignored
  • • Forget to train — Training unlocks the best perks
  • • Rush adoption — Wait for a dog you really like
