🔓 How to Unlock Cooking

Cooking is one of the most valuable hobbies in Heartopia. Here's how to unlock it:

  1. 1
    Reach DG Level 5-6

    Complete daily quests and main story to level up your Developer Grade.

  2. 2
    Get Hobby Expansion Ticket

    You'll receive this automatically when reaching the required DG level.

  3. 3
    Find Massimo

    He's the cooking mentor, located outside his café in the town center.

  4. ✓
    Give Ticket to Massimo

    Speak with him and use your Hobby Expansion Ticket. You'll also get a free stove for your home!

💡 Why Cooking First? Cooking synergizes perfectly with Fishing and Gardening (your first two hobbies), letting you turn raw ingredients into high-value dishes for massive profit.

👨‍🍳 How to Cook

  1. 1. Approach a stove (there's one at Massimo's café, or use the one at home)
  2. 2. Interact with the stove to open the recipe menu
  3. 3. Select a recipe you've already learned (you can't experiment with random ingredients)
  4. 4. Add the required ingredients from your inventory
  5. 5. Watch for the QTE (Quick Time Event) — a spatula icon with green timer
  6. 6. Tap the spatula before the timer runs out to prevent burning

⚠️ Warning: If you miss the QTE, your dish becomes "Bizarre Food" which has reduced value and effects. Pay attention to the spatula icon!

🌱 Starter Recipes (Unlocked Immediately)

These recipes are available as soon as you unlock the cooking hobby:

🍯 Mixed Jam

Ingredients: 4× Any Fruit

The base jam recipe. Using 4 of the same fruit unlocks that specific jam variant!

🥗 House Salad

Ingredients: 2× Any Vegetable

A simple starter dish. Great for using excess vegetables.

💡 Cooking Tips

✅ Do

  • • Use 1-star ingredients for better profit margins
  • • Set up multiple cooking pots for efficiency (3 is ideal)
  • • Cook low-value fish into dishes (100 Gold or less)
  • • Make jam from foraged fruits — it's free profit!
  • • Watch for the QTE spatula to avoid burning

❌ Don't

  • • Sell raw ingredients — always cook first!
  • • Cook high-value fish (200+ Gold) — sell them raw
  • • Eat cooked food for energy — sleep instead to save profits
  • • Use expensive ingredients in low-tier recipes
