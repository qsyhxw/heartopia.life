The "Secret" Mechanic

There is no single universal favorite food for all cats! Each cat has unique preferences (RNG). A Cat Shark might be one cat's favorite, while another prefers simple Sardines.

🧪 The 3-Step Testing Strategy

1

Stock Up

Go fishing! Catch 5-10 of commonly liked fish (Chub, Sardine, etc.). Don't waste rare fish yet.

2

Observe the Reaction

Feed one item. Look closely at the emoji bubble above your cat's head.

  • ❤️ Hearts / Music Note: LOVES IT! (This is the favorite)
  • 😐 Neutral / Dot Dot Dot: Just okay.
  • 🤢 Confused / Sad: Dislikes it.
3

Remember It!

Once you find the food that triggers the ❤️ reaction, stick to it! This will maximize your daily bond increase.

⚠️ Dealing with "Sensitive" Cats

Does your cat have the Sensitive trait? They are "picky eaters" and harder to please!

Tip: You might need higher quality fish (like Cat Shark or Tuna) or premium store-bought food to satisfy them. Be patient!
