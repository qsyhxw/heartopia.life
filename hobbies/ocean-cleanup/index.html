Heartopia Ocean Cleanup Guide

Unlock the Ocean Cleaner, understand the two separate level systems, handle every pollutant type, and reach Ocean Cleaning Lv.10 without wasting proficiency or tickets.

Three different levels appear in this update: D.G. Member Level controls event access, Whalefall Canyon Exploration Level advances the Adventure Journal, and Ocean Cleaning Level upgrades the hobby itself. Raising one does not automatically raise the others.

Unlock route

How to Unlock Ocean Cleanup

1

Open Call of Whales

Reach D.G. Member Level 7, finish the requirement shown in the live event panel, and enter Whalefall Canyon.

2

Open the Adventure Journal

Use the shell-shaped Whalefall Canyon progression icon. Complete its objectives and daily requests to earn exploration progress.

3

Reach Exploration Level 2

Claim the level reward. Ocean Cleanup and the beginner Ocean Cleaner unlock from this canyon progression, not from Oliver dialogue alone.

4

Equip and complete the first cleanup

Choose the cleaner from the tool wheel, approach black pollution mist, lock on, and hold the cleanup control until the gauge finishes.

Cleaner missing after talking to NPCs? Return to the Adventure Journal and claim Exploration Level 2. The progression reward is the important checkpoint; repeatedly talking to nearby characters will not replace an unclaimed reward.

Cleanup controls

How Each Pollutant Type Works

Start close to the target and keep it centered. If a pollutant flashes red or gains a red energy shield, release the control and resume when it becomes safe.

If your character becomes polluted

Body Pollution increases stamina use. Move to a Clean Anemone in the canyon, use a home bath, or let another player clean the contamination before continuing a long route.

After the pollutant disappears

Collect any revealed seashells instead of swimming away immediately. Seashell discoveries contribute to the Ocean Cleanup collection and its display rewards.

Do not confuse the tickets

Ocean Cleaning uses its dedicated training tickets. Standard hobby tickets in your inventory do not replace them. Raise Whalefall Canyon Exploration Level and claim each Adventure Journal reward.

Efficient progression

A Better Daily Cleanup Loop

01

Claim daily requests

Prioritize Whalefall Canyon tasks because they advance exploration and ticket rewards alongside the hobby.

02

Clear clustered areas

Sweep nearby targets before moving zones. Pollution respawns, so a cleared route is not permanently exhausted.

03

Collect while moving

Gather sea plants and revealed shells on the same circuit to progress canyon objectives and recipes.

04

Upgrade before the next lap

Open the hobby screen when proficiency is capped, then claim Adventure Journal ticket rewards before more cleaning.

Troubleshooting

Ocean Cleanup Not Working?

Cleaner or hobby is still locked

Open the Whalefall Canyon Adventure Journal, reach Exploration Level 2, and claim the reward. Event access at D.G. Level 7 is only the first gate.

Black mist is missing

Install the latest update, leave the canyon, and reload it. A July 15 flash update addressed pollutants appearing without their black mist.

Swirling Pollutant cannot be targeted

Update and reload first because the same flash update fixed this targeting bug. If the target glows red after locking on, wait for the shield phase to end.

Proficiency is not increasing

Check whether the current level bar is full and waiting for an upgrade. Also confirm that you have claimed the dedicated cleanup tickets needed for the next level.

No pollution remains nearby

Move between the expedition camp, cave, whale-bone, and coral routes or return after the area refreshes. Cleared pollution can respawn.

Stamina drains unusually fast

Look for the Body Pollution effect. Clean it at a Clean Anemone, use a home bath, or ask another player to clean your character.

Ocean Cleanup FAQ

Is Ocean Cleanup limited to Call of Whales?

The hobby is permanent. Fashionwave missions, charity activities, and some rewards can still follow event dates.

Where do Ocean Cleanup tickets come from?

Raise Whalefall Canyon Exploration Level and claim the Adventure Journal rewards. They are separate from ordinary hobby tickets.

Should I sell every seashell?

No. Keep collection and display goals in mind before selling duplicates, especially while your Ocean Cleanup collection is incomplete.

When does the underwater Seamount unlock?

The Seamount management terminal is tied to Whalefall Canyon Exploration Level 5, not Ocean Cleaning Level 5.
