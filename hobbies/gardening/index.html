Heartopia Gardening Guide

Buy seeds, plant on a schedule you can return to, water through the in-game care flow, and harvest ingredients for cooking.

1. Choose seeds

Use Blanc's gardening shop and compare the seed budget before buying several planters' worth.

2. Match your return

Quick crops fit active play; long crops are better before work, school, or sleep.

3. Water and harvest

Use the standard gardening tools, clear weeds from plots, and replant after harvesting.

Gardening FAQ

Where do I buy seeds?
Check Blanc's gardening shop and the seed list currently available to your character.

Which crop should I plant first?
Pick a growth time that matches your next return, then consider the recipes you want to prepare.

How can I plan a seed budget?
Use the crop planner to multiply each verified seed price by the number of seeds you want to buy.
