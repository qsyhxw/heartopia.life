Heartopia Gardening Guide

Gardening is easier when you plan by growth time. Use this guide to buy seeds, keep planters active, water crops, clear weeds, and track all 17 crop entries.

Quick Start

  1. Find Blanc or the gardening shop from the map.
  2. Buy seeds that match your next return time.
  3. Place seeds in planters and water through the normal tool flow.
  4. Clear weeds when they appear around crop plots.
  5. Harvest, then mark new crops in your collection.

Gardening Basics

Buying seeds
Use Blanc's shop as your seed source. If a seed is not available yet, keep progressing and recheck the shop list.
Watering
Water crops after planting and during normal crop care. If watering behaves oddly, replace the planter and try again.
Weeds
Remove weeds from crop plots as part of your daily garden loop.
Cooking prep
Check recipes before buying a large seed batch, especially for Wheat and Grape.

Common Mistakes

  • Buying more seeds than your planters can handle.
  • Planting a long crop before a short play session.
  • Forgetting to replant after harvesting a quick crop.
  • Using old profit charts as permanent game data.

Gardening FAQ

Where do I buy seeds?
Buy seeds from Blanc or the gardening shop when the seed appears in your available shop list.

How many crops are listed?
The current crop database lists 17 crop entries.

What should I plant first?
For short sessions, plant quick crops. For long breaks, plant crops that take 8 hours or longer.
