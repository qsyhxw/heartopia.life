  1. 1

    Travel to Onsen Mountain

    Head to the Winter Frost Season event area

⛄ How to Create Snow Sculptures

📦 Get Snowballs

  • • Snowball Machine: 20 per hour (passive)
  • • Resource harvesting: Random drops
  • • First sculpture bonus: +10 snowballs from Azure

🎮 Sculpting Minigame

  • • Interact with Sculpting Bench
  • • 20 seconds to complete
  • • Tap snowflakes as they light up
  • • Better timing = higher star rating

⭐ Star Rating System

Each sculpture receives a star rating based on your minigame performance. Higher stars = more gold when selling!

⭐ 1-Star ⭐⭐ 2-Star ⭐⭐⭐ 3-Star ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4-Star (Lv3+) ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5-Star (Lv5)

📈 Upgrade Costs & Benefits

⚠️ Cost Warning: Fully maxing Snow Sculpting costs approximately 600 Wishing Stars. Plan your spending carefully!
Level Cost Unlocks
Level 1 100 ⭐ Basic sculpting, starter designs, 1-3 star sculptures
Level 2 50 ⭐ New designs, improved quality chance
Level 3 50+ ⭐ 4-star sculptures unlocked!
Level 4 50+ ⭐ More designs, better odds
Level 5 (MAX) 50+ ⭐ 5-star sculptures, Vanya Bear design, Achievement!
💡 Is it worth maxing? Level 3 gives you 4-star sculptures which is a good stopping point. Level 5 is only for completionists and collectors who want the Vanya Bear.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I unlock Snow Sculpting?

Visit Azure at Onsen Mountain and purchase the hobby for 100 Wishing Stars from the Fashionwave Hobby Store.

How long is the Winter Frost Season?

The event runs from January 31 to March 13, 2026 - approximately 6 weeks.

How much does it cost to max Snow Sculpting?

Approximately 600 Wishing Stars total (100 to unlock + 50 per level upgrade with increasing ticket costs).

Will my sculptures disappear after the event?

Most players believe sculptures will remain in your inventory, but this hasn't been officially confirmed by the developers.

How do I get the Vanya Bear sculpture?

Reach Level 5 Snow Sculpting AND sculpt during rainy or rainbow weather.
