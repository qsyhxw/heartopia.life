🔓 How to Unlock Fishing

Fishing is one of your first hobbies, unlocked during the tutorial:

  1. 1
    Meet Vanya

    Progress through the tutorial until you encounter Vanya, the fishing mentor.

  2. 2
    Craft a Repair Kit

    Vanya needs a repair kit for their fishing rod. Craft and give it to them.

  3. ✓
    Receive Hobby Expansion Ticket

    Vanya gives you a ticket to officially unlock the Fishing hobby. You'll also get a fishing rod!

🎣 How to Fish

  1. 1
    Equip Your Fishing Rod

    Open your toolbox/inventory and select the fishing rod.

  2. 2
    Find Fish Shadows

    Look for dark shadows in the water — these indicate fish. Bigger shadows = rarer fish!

  3. 3
    Cast Your Line

    Face the fish and tap the fishing rod icon. Hold and release for precise casting.

  4. 4
    Wait for a Bite

    When the bobber disappears and an exclamation mark (!) appears, you've hooked a fish!

  5. 5
    Reel In Carefully

    Hold the reel icon to pull the fish. Watch the line color!

🎯 Line Tension Guide

⚪
White
Safe to reel
🟡
Yellow
Be cautious
🔴
Red
RELEASE NOW!

If the line stays red, it will snap and the fish escapes!

🪱 Bait Types

Bait makes fishing easier and increases rare fish chances. Unlock it after completing the "Cat on the Roof" quest with Vanya.

🪱 Basic Fish Bait

  • • Craft from apples (recipe from Vanya)
  • • Or buy for 200 Gold from Vanya
  • • Spawns a fish instantly in front of you
  • • Reduces wait time between bites

✨ Premium Bait

  • • Unlocked at Fishing Level 4
  • • Increases rare fish chances
  • • Essential for Rainbow Fish hunting

🧪 Specialized Baits (from Bill)

  • • Mermaid Perfume — targets specific rarity
  • • Fish Attractor — skip the minigame!
  • • Available from advanced mentor Bill

🏆 Legendary Bait

  • • Unlocked at Fishing Level 10
  • • Vanya offers daily quests for this reward
  • • Best for catching legendary fish

How to Use Bait

Stand at water's edge → Open inventory → Select bait → Press "Chum" to drop it in water → Fish spawns instantly!

🧜Mermaid Fish Attractor

A special tool unlocked at Fishing Level 4 that creates blue flashing fish shadows when used with other players!

✨ How It Works

  1. 1. Get from Vanya (Fishing Level 4)
  2. 2. Use near water with other players nearby
  3. 3. Blue flashing fish shadows appear
  4. 4. Catch spawns specific rare fish by water type!

💡 Fishing Tips

✅ Do

  • • Level up to Fishing Level 2 ASAP — unlocks higher-star fish chances
  • • Watch for rainbow weather — it's your chance for rare catches
  • • Carry Repair Kits — don't let your rod break mid-session
  • • Use bait at home pond — efficient gold farming loop
  • • Sell high-value fish (200+ Gold) raw — cooking them isn't worth it
  • • Look for bigger shadows — they indicate rarer fish

❌ Don't

  • • Ignore line tension — red = release immediately!
  • • Cook high-value fish — sell them raw for better profit
  • • Fish without bait when hunting rare species
  • • Sell low-value fish raw — cook them first!
