📋 Recommended Unlock Order

1-2

Tutorial: Fishing & Gardening

These unlock automatically. Focus on leveling both to Level 2 for better yields.

3

DG Level 6: Choose Cooking First! ⭐

Cooking synergizes perfectly with Fishing and Gardening. Turn raw materials into high-profit dishes — best early-game income!

4-5

DG Level 6+: Insect Catching & Birdwatching

Unlock these as you earn more Hobby Expansion Tickets. Both are great for collection-focused players.

6-7

DG Level 12: Cat Care → Dog Care

Late-game unlocks. You must unlock Cat Care before Dog Care becomes available.

🎫 Hobby Expansion Tickets

Each hobby requires a Hobby Expansion Ticket to unlock. These are scarce, so choose wisely!

How to Get Tickets:

  • 🎯 Complete tutorial quests (first 2 hobbies)
  • 📈 Increase your DG Members Guild rank
  • 🏆 Progress through the main story

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How many hobbies are in Heartopia.Life?

There are 8 hobbies in Heartopia.Life: Fishing, Gardening, Cooking, Insect Catching, Birdwatching, Cat Care, Dog Care, and Snow Sculpting (seasonal).

What is the best hobby to unlock first in Heartopia?

Cooking is the best hobby to unlock first at DG Level 6. It synergizes perfectly with Fishing and Gardening, allowing you to turn raw materials into high-profit dishes for 2-3x Gold.

How do I get Hobby Expansion Tickets?

Hobby Expansion Tickets are obtained by completing tutorial quests (first 2 hobbies), increasing your DG Members Guild rank, and progressing through the main story.

Can I unlock Dog Care before Cat Care?

No, you must unlock Cat Care first before Dog Care becomes available. Both require DG Level 12.

Which hobbies are unlocked automatically?

Fishing and Gardening are unlocked automatically during the tutorial. All other hobbies require Hobby Expansion Tickets and specific DG Level requirements.
