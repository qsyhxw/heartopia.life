📋 Recommended Unlock Order

1-2

Tutorial: Fishing & Gardening

These unlock automatically. Focus on leveling both to Level 2 for better yields.

3

DG Level 6: Choose Cooking First! ⭐

Cooking synergizes perfectly with Fishing and Gardening. Turn raw materials into high-profit dishes — best early-game income!

4-5

DG Level 6+: Insect Catching & Birdwatching

Unlock these as you earn more Hobby Expansion Tickets. Both are great for collection-focused players.

6-7

DG Level 12: Cat Care → Dog Care

Late-game unlocks. You must unlock Cat Care before Dog Care becomes available.

🎫 Hobby Expansion Tickets

Each hobby requires a Hobby Expansion Ticket to unlock. These are scarce, so choose wisely!

How to Get Tickets:

  • 🎯 Complete tutorial quests (first 2 hobbies)
  • 📈 Increase your DG Members Guild rank
  • 🏆 Progress through the main story
