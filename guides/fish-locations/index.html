Heartopia Fishing Basics

Fishing is one of the foundational pillars of the Heartopia gold economy. If you haven't unlocked it yet, simply speak with Vanya (the Fishing Mentor) during your early-game quests.

🎮 The Mini-Game

Cast your line directly in front of a fish's shadow. When it bites, hold the reel icon, but release it immediately if the line flashes red to prevent snapping!

📈 Hobby Progression

Consistently fishing raises your Hobby Level. This increases rod durability and directly affects the chance of 4-Star or 5-Star fish shadows spawning in your world.

Weather & Time Conditions for Rare Fish

Just like the insect catching mechanics, the most legendary fish will absolutely ignore your bait unless the stars (and weather) align perfectly.

🌈

The Rainbow Jackpot

Rainbow weather is the most highly sought-after event in Heartopia. If you see a rainbow, stop whatever you are doing and run to the nearest body of water!

Exclusives & Boosted Spawns:
  • Golden King Crab
  • Butterfly Koi
  • Swordfish
  • Smooth Hammerhead
  • Goldfish

🌧️❄️ Rainy & Snowy Days

Rainy and snowy days share the exact same spawn tables. Don't worry about missing summer fish in the winter.

  • Butterfly Koi (Meadow Lake)
  • Large Pearl Mussel
  • Northern Pike

🕒 Night Owls & Early Birds

Certain fish are strictly tied to specific 6-hour chunks of the real-world server time.

  • Morning (6-12): European Smelt, Mottled Sculpin
  • Night (18-24): European Eel, Blackspot Seabream
  • Midnight Secret: The legendary Koi only appears at the Secret Pond during late-night hours.

Fish Location FAQ

Where should I start if I only know the fish name?

Start with the quick finder at the top of this page. Match the fish to lake, river, or sea first, then check whether it needs weather, time, level, or Mermaid Attractor.

Why is a fish not appearing at the listed location?

Check four things in order: wrong water type, missing Fishing Level, wrong weather/time, or missing special attractor. If the fish has a single page, open it for the exact condition list.

Which rare fish should I check first?

Butterfly Koi is a good first rare lake target because its location is clear: Meadow Lake during Rain or Rainbow. For sea collection, start with Sardine and Seahorse before chasing higher-level rare fish.

Is the fish database different from this location guide?

Yes. This guide is for choosing routes quickly. The fish database is better when you need a longer list, prices, and links to individual fish pages.

Need the exact route or map border?

If you are unsure where a named water area begins, open the map guide first, then return here to pick the fish target.
