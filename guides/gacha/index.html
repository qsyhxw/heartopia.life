Common questions

Heartopia gacha FAQ

Does Heartopia have pity?

Do not assume one universal pity. The disclosed SANRIO Special Treasure reaches 100% on draw 10 while unowned, but another pool can use different rules.

Where do I find current gacha rates?

Open the exact exhibition and use its question-mark or rules control to open Probability Display. Match the pool name and your current draw column.

Does pity carry over to another banner?

Do not assume carryover between exhibitions or recolors. Only rely on a carryover rule if the current in-game panel explicitly states it.

How much money should I budget?

Enter the remaining costs shown in your pool, subtract currency you own, then compare the shortage with the current pack size and checkout price.
