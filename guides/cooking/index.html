🔓 How to Unlock Cooking

Unlock Requirements

1
Reach DG Level 5
Complete Resident Requests to earn Contribution Medals
2
Talk to Massimo
Find him at his café in the Town Square (Residential Street)
3
Use Hobby Expansion Ticket
You'll receive your first stove and starter recipes!

💡 Recommendation: Choose Cooking first among available hobbies (Cooking, Insect Catching, Birdwatching) — it synergizes perfectly with Gardening and Fishing!

🍳 Cooking Basics

📍 How to Cook

  1. Interact with a stove in your home
  2. Select a recipe you've learned
  3. Add the required ingredients
  4. Complete the QTE mini-game to regulate heat
  5. Collect your finished dish!

⚠️ Watch the Heat!

If you miss the QTE timing, your dish will burn!

  • 🔥 Burned food = Bizarre Food
  • 🥤 Burned drink = Bizarre Drink
  • These sell for much less, so focus!

⭐ Star Quality

Dishes can be 1-5 stars. Higher star = much higher sell price!

★★★★★ = Maximum profit

🔥 Multiple Stoves

Craft additional stoves at workbench (timber + ore) for mass production!

Pro Tip: 2-4 stoves = cooking empire 💰

🌱 Early Game Strategy

  1. Forage mushrooms everywhere
  2. Cook Grilled Mushrooms (free!)
  3. Sell 5-star dishes for 1,440g
  4. Reinvest in more stoves

🍓 Mid-Late Game Strategy

  1. Grow Corn + Strawberries (rotation)
  2. Make Corn Soup + Strawberry Jam
  3. 5-star Strawberry Jam = 12,640g!
  4. Use multiple stoves for mass production

💡 Pro Tip: Always cook ingredients before selling! Raw items sell for much less than cooked dishes. Even "failed" Bizarre Food is sometimes better than raw ingredients.

📈 Fast Leveling Tips

🔥

Use Multiple Stoves

Craft 2-4 stoves to cook in parallel. More cooking = faster XP!

🆕

Cook New Recipes

First time cooking any recipe/variant = huge XP bonus!

🍎

Free Ingredients

Jams from free fruits (apples, berries) = easy XP with no cost!

☕

Lattes at Level 3

No harvesting needed! Buy coffee beans from Massimo and cook.

💡 Pro Tips

🌾 Gardening + Cooking Combo

Grown ingredients boost recipe profits. Plant wheat, tomatoes, and strawberries for best synergy.

🎣 Fishing + Cooking Combo

Fish and Chips needs 2 fish + 2 potatoes. Catch fish, grow potatoes, profit!

⏰ Daily Cooking Quests

Complete daily cooking quests for bonus XP on top of regular cooking XP!

⛽ Cooking Fuel

Use cooking fuel to reduce wait times when you're in a hurry!
