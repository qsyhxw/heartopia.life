🍰 How to Make Green Roll Cake
(Also known as "Grass Cake")

Searching for the elusive "Grass Cake"? You likely mean the Green Roll Cake! Here is the complete recipe and guide to ingredients.

👩‍🍳 Chef: Doris | ⭐ Cost: 50 Stars

🥣 Ingredients

  • 🥚
    1x Egg Common Ingredient
  • 🥛
    1x Milk Common Ingredient
  • 🌿
    2x Green Sugar ⚠️ Must buy from Doris!

📝 How to Unlock Recipe

1

Wait for Special Weather

Doris only appears during Rain, Snow, Rainbow, or Meteor Shower.

2

Find Doris

Look for her at Art Street, Forest Island Bridge, or Onsen Mountain. She is the traveling merchant.

3

Buy Key Items

Buy the "Original Roll Cake Recipe" (50 Stars) AND stock up on Green Sugar.

4

Cook!

Go to any stove. Select Roll Cake. Change sugar type to Green Sugar. Done!

💡 Pro Tip

Don't see a "Green Roll Cake" recipe in the shop? You need to buy the "Original Roll Cake" recipe first! The colored versions are just variations using different sugars.
