🏠 1. Main Areas Overview

Heartopia features six main explorable regions. Most areas appear to be accessible from the start of the game — DG Level requirements primarily apply to hobbies and events rather than geographical access.

Area Description Key Features
🏡 Home Your personal starting plot Gardening, tree chopping, mining, decoration
🏙️ Central Town Social hub & shops Clothing store (Dorothy), Furniture store (Bob), Pet store
🎣 Fishing Village Coastal fishing area Sea Fishing event (Bill), Shiitake mushrooms, Sea Otter
🌲 Forest Large exploration area Bamboo, Jump Puzzle, Forest Lake, Panda, Sika Deer
🌻 Flower Field Beautiful meadow area Meadow Lake, windmills, Alpaca, Fox
♨️ Onsen Mountains Hot spring region Geysers, Capybara, Oyster mushrooms, Crater Lake
💡 Tip: Use Bus Stops scattered throughout the map for fast travel between areas. Check the in-game map (upper-left corner) for all available locations.

🐼 2. Wild Animal Trough Locations

Befriend wild animals by finding their feeding troughs! Feed them daily to build friendship and unlock interactions.

🦙 Alpaca

  • Location: Flower Fields / Tranquil River
  • Landmark: Near the giant yellow duck in southern Flower Fields

🐰 Bunny

  • Location: Suburbs
  • Landmark: West of Art Street, between bus stop and town

🦫 Capybara

  • Location: Onsen Mountain
  • Landmark: West of Onsen Mountain Lake, near ruins area

🦨 Ferret

  • Location: Rosie River area
  • Landmark: South of Rosie River, near northernmost windmill

🦊 Fox

  • Location: Flower Field
  • Landmark: Near Meadow Lake, base of southern windmill hill

🐼 Panda

  • Location: Forest
  • Landmark: South area with bamboo, near Jump Puzzle stairs

🦦 Sea Otter

  • Location: Fishing Village
  • Landmark: Between the two piers in the village square

🦌 Sika Deer

  • Location: Forest Lake
  • Landmark: Beside Forest Lake, close to bus stop

👤 3. Key NPC Locations

📍 Static NPCs

NPC Location Function
Dorothy Central Town Clothing store (unlock via poster quest)
Bob Central Town Furniture store (unlock via joinery tea table quest)
Bailey J Pet Store (upstairs) Birdwatching hobby unlock (DG Level 6)
Ka Ching Near city center Inventory expansion
Bill Fishing Village Sea Fishing event host
Naughty Onsen Mountain (north walkway) Quest giver ("Gossip: Treasure Hunt")

🔄 Dynamic NPCs

💰 Albert Jr. (Gold Merchant)

Wandering merchant for item sales. Changes location throughout the day — check your in-game map for his icon.

🌳 Roaming Oak

Special tree NPC that moves daily. Often appears near home plots. Provides rare Roaming Oak Timber.

🌧️ 4. Doris (Traveling Merchant)

Doris is a special weather-dependent merchant who sells exclusive recipes, ingredients, cosmetics, and furniture. She only appears during specific weather conditions:

Weather Condition Location Notes
🌧️ Rain / ❄️ Snowfall Art Street district Near the street marketplace or bridges
🌈 Rainbow (after rain) Base of the rainbow Location varies by rainbow spawn point
☄️ Meteor Shower Near meteor impact sites Often in Onsen Mountain or highland areas
💡 Pro Tip: Rainbows typically appear at 6 PM server time after rain ends. Check the weather forecast in your Watch app!

🏝️

🌲

🚌 Navigation Tips

  • • Bus Stops: Fast travel points at Meadow Lake, Forest Lake, and other key locations
  • • In-game Map: Access via upper-left corner to view all locations and NPC icons
  • • Teleport Home: Use your Watch menu for quick return to your home plot
  • • Compass: Follow the mini-map compass for navigation during quests
