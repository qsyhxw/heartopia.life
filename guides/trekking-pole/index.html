Heartopia Trekking Pole Quest: Vernie and Naughty

For the Trekking Pole gossip, talk to Vernie at the Flower Fields, then report back to Naughty. Track the active quest and NPC if an event has moved her.

Follow the active gossip · If Vernie is missing · If Treasure Hunt has not appeared

Follow the active gossip

  1. Accept Naughty’s Treasure Hunt gossip and read the current objective.
  2. Track Vernie and check the Flower Fields around the windmills.
  3. Ask her about the trekking pole, then return to Naughty and finish his dialogue.
  4. Inspect the quest completion and reward screen before leaving.

The guide records a Retro Desk Lamp reward. Quest walkthrough

If Vernie is missing

Use the current NPC marker. Players in the original question reported finding her at a concert during an event. Follow her marker before repeatedly searching the usual field position. If her dialogue has no quest option, confirm the gossip is active and reread its current objective. Original question and event-location reports

If Treasure Hunt has not appeared

AllThingsHow describes earlier story deliveries to Cassie and Naughty, followed by an orange gossip marker. That prerequisite sequence is community documentation from January 2026. Continue available story requests and watch the map. A fixed waiting time cannot be established from that guide.

Once the gossip is active, the useful next action is Vernie’s dialogue. Compare the objective after each conversation if your client differs.
