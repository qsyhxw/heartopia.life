🏠 1. What Are Home Plots?

Home Plots are individual sections of land that make up your residential property in Heartopia. Each plot provides space where you can place furniture, decorations, gardens, and expand your dream home.

📊 Plot Specifications

  • • Starting plots: 2 plots
  • • Maximum plots: 8 plots (at DG Level 14)
  • • Plot size: 8×5 tiles each
  • • Total area (8 plots): Up to 320 tiles

✨ What You Can Do

  • • Place furniture and decorations
  • • Create gardens with planters
  • • Build outdoor workshop areas
  • • Increase your load limit for items
💡 Good to Know: Unlocking a new plot not only gives you more space, but also increases your total load limit — the number of furniture pieces you can place in and around your home.

🎖️ 2. DG Level Requirements

Your ability to purchase more Home Plots is tied directly to your D.G. Member's Guild level. As your rank increases, more plots become available for purchase.

Some sources mention a maximum of 18 home plots through continued progression beyond Level 14. However, most current guides confirm 8 plots at DG Level 14 as the verified maximum. This may change in future updates.

🛒 3. How to Purchase Plots

Once you've reached the required DG level, follow these steps to buy new plots:

📱 Step-by-Step Purchase Guide

  1. 1 Open your Watch menu (tap the watch icon or phone icon in the top-right corner)
  2. 2 Navigate to D.G. Member's Guild app
  3. 3 Tap the four squares icon in the top-right corner (Plot tab)
  4. 4 Select an available plot (look for shaking lock icons — these are unlocked!)
  5. 5 Tap the yellow acorn icon to pay Gold and confirm purchase

✅ After Purchase

  • • Construction starts immediately
  • • No waiting timer required
  • • New plot is instantly accessible
  • • Load limit increases automatically

🔒 Lock Icon Guide

  • • Static lock: DG level not reached
  • • Shaking lock: Ready to purchase!
  • • No lock: Already owned

⚡ 5. Fast Unlock Strategy

Want to expand your property as quickly as possible? Focus on these key strategies:

🎯 4-Plot Speedrun (DG Level 4 in 3-4 Days)

Goal: Reach DG Level 4 to unlock 2 additional plots

Method: Complete all 5 Daily Resident Requests every day

Medals needed: ~200-250 medals per level (varies)

Timeline: 3-4 days of consistent daily play

✅ Do This

  • • Complete all 5 requests daily
  • • Check requests right after 6 AM reset
  • • Stock common items: Wheat, Tomatoes, Fish, Eggs, Milk
  • • Earn Gold while waiting (fishing, cooking)
  • • Save Gold for plot purchases first

❌ Avoid This

  • • Skipping days (no catch-up mechanism)
  • • Spending Gold on cosmetics before plots
  • • Abandoning requests too quickly
  • • Expecting to rush (system is time-gated)

💰 Gold Farming While You Wait

Since DG progression is time-gated, use waiting time to earn Gold:

  • • Best: Complete shop orders (100-300g each)
  • • Good: Sell cooked dishes to Albert Jr.
  • • Decent: Fish and sell high-value catches
  • • Extra: Use Redeem Codes for free Gold

📐 6. Layout Planning Tips

Smart plot selection can make a big difference in your home design. Plan ahead before purchasing!

🥇 First Expansion (Plots 3-4)

  • • Buy plots adjacent to starting plots
  • • Create a compact 2×2 block
  • • Avoid diagonal plots first
  • • Best for: Large house + connected garden

🥈 Later Expansions (Plots 5-8)

  • • Extend your block into a 2×3 or 2×4 shape
  • • Consider a "courtyard" design
  • • Dedicate one plot to production/storage
  • • Keep aesthetic plots clean and separate

⚠️ Common Layout Mistakes

  • • Checkerboard pattern: Creates isolated islands, wastes pathways
  • • Linear only (1×8): Limits building shape options
  • • Diagonal first: Makes connections awkward
💡 Pro Tip: Plan your final 8-plot layout before buying Plot #3. Each plot is 8×5 tiles, so sketch your design first to avoid rebuilding later!

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How many home plots can I have in Heartopia?

You can own up to 8 home plots. You start with 2 and unlock more through DG progression up to Level 14.

Why can't I buy a plot even though I see it?

Check two things: (1) Have you reached the required DG level? (2) Do you have enough Gold? Both conditions must be met.

Do plots unlock automatically when I level up?

No. Leveling up only increases your plot limit. You must manually purchase each plot with Gold in the D.G. Member's Guild app.

When do daily requests reset?

Daily Resident Requests reset at 6:00 AM server time. Make sure to complete all 5 before the reset!
