Unlock route

Tidal Stove and Massimo Quest

1

Enter Whalefall Canyon

Reach D.G. Member Level 7 and finish the event entry requirement.

2

Find Massimo at camp

Track his event cooking objective to learn the new ingredient route.

3

Learn every reward

Use the Tidal Stove crafting book and each recipe card from your bag.

4

Craft or borrow a stove

Craft yours at a regular workbench or use the station beside Massimo.

5

Buy permanent cards

Massimo sells the three permanent sea-ingredient recipe cards.

6

Cook bases first

Cook the base dish before substituting the named fruit or jam.

Reward missing? Check your bag, mail, home storage, and warehouse. Switch to the correct stove and relog once after learning a card.

Gathering route

One Ingredient Route for All 19

Whale Sea

Catch Scallop for pearl cakes and Japanese Flying Squid for grilled squid.

Whalefall Canyon

Forage Sea Grape, Wakame, and Glasswort while exploring.

Massimo's shop

Get Spirulina Powder, Pasteurized Egg, Meat, and three permanent cards.

Blanc and pantry

Grow Starfruit and Paddy. Keep Milk, Wheat, Tomato, Shiitake, Common Prawn, fruit, and jams.

Batch order: grow Starfruit and Paddy while gathering sea plants; catch six Japanese Flying Squid and six Scallops; prepare the named jams on a normal stove; then cook bases and variants on the Tidal Stove.

Variant method

Discover the Variants

Pearl Cake family

Start with Scallop + Milk + Wheat. Add Apple, Blueberry, Mandarin, Raspberry, or Starfruit.

Grilled Squid family

Use Japanese Flying Squid + Sea Grape + Wakame, then Fruit Jam or the exact named jam.

Tip: avoid Universal Ingredient. If discovery fails, confirm the base card, reload the zone, and retry.

Troubleshooting

Recipe or Tidal Stove Missing?

No Tidal Stove blueprint

Complete Massimo event cooking and use the crafting book in your bag.

Stove says no recipes

Learn each card and confirm that you are using the correct stove.

Massimo quest missing

Finish earlier canyon objectives and track the cooking task in the event panel.

Variant not unlocking

Cook the base first, avoid Universal Ingredient, and retry after reloading.

No Spirulina Powder

Progress Massimo route, then check his Whalefall Canyon shop.

No sea plants

Search canyon routes and return after the gathering nodes refresh.

FAQ

Quick Answers

Are all 19 event-limited?

No. Fifteen use the Tidal Stove. Four use normal stoves.

Can I use the camp stove?

Yes. The event cooking station near Massimo works for seasonal recipes.

Which five recipes come from the route?

Tomato Seafood Soup, Grilled Squid w/ Fruit Jam, Ocean Iced Drink, Ocean Feast, and Seashell Pearl Cake.
