🧊 Heartopia Frostspire Infection Guide

Use this page when the Frostspire Infection or Frostspore Infection task appears during Winter Frost: how to trigger the cold-weather buff, keep the 6-day streak, and check why progress may not count.

Quick Answer

Frostspire Infection is the cold-weather infection route tied to the Winter Frost event. Stand in cold weather until the snowflake-style buff appears, keep it active for at least 1 minute, and repeat that on 6 consecutive event days. Missing a day resets the streak, and the main reward path is the Frostbitten Skin cosmetic.

Important: This is event-tied progress. If Winter Frost is not active on your server, focus on other winter pages and return when the event systems are available again.

What Counts as the Infection Buff?

Visible sign

Look for the cold-weather snowflake icon or infection-style status after standing in a cold area.

Daily timer

Do not leave immediately. Keep the buff active for about a minute before switching areas.

Streak state

The task is about daily consistency, not standing in cold weather for a very long session once.

Requirements Before You Start

Requirement What to Check Why It Matters
Winter Frost access Event quests and winter areas are available. The infection route is tied to this event season.
Cold weather Your character is in a cold-weather zone long enough for the icon. The buff starts from cold exposure.
Daily reset awareness Do one clear trigger per event day. The 6-day chain fails if a day is skipped.

6-Day Walkthrough

  1. 1

    Pick a repeatable cold-weather spot

    Use the same winter route each day so you are not guessing whether you reached the right condition.

  2. 2

    Wait for the snowflake-style status

    Do not start counting until the cold or infection indicator appears.

  3. 3

    Stay for at least 1 minute

    A short buffer is safer than leaving immediately after the icon appears.

  4. 4

    Repeat after the next daily reset

    Do one clean trigger on each of 6 consecutive event days.

  5. 5

    Check the Frostbitten Skin unlock

    After Day 6, check your cosmetic options and winter event progress panels.

Progress Not Counting: Fixes First

The buff icon appears, but the streak did not advance.

Stay longer the next day and use a clear 60-90 second count after the icon appears. Leaving too quickly is the easiest mistake to make.

The streak reset.

A missed event day resets the chain. Restart from Day 1 and use the same cold-weather spot after each daily reset.

I cannot trigger the infection at all.

Check whether Winter Frost systems are active, whether you are in a cold-weather area, and whether the relevant event introduction quests are available on your account.

I already did six days but do not see the skin.

Reopen the character customization and winter event panels, then relog once. If it still is not there, count the next daily reset as your next clean trigger instead of spending more time in the same day.

Common Mistakes

Doing multiple triggers in one day

The route depends on consecutive days. Repeating it on the same day does not replace tomorrow's trigger.

Leaving before the hold time

Wait at least a minute after the status appears before changing zones or opening another activity.

Mixing up Frostspire and Frostspore terms

Players use both spellings when searching. In-game winter content often points to Frostspore activities.

Waiting until the last event days

Start early during the event window because one skipped day can force a full restart.

FAQ

Is it Frostspire Infection or Frostspore Infection?

Players search both names. The winter event pages use Frostspore language for many event activities, while the search phrase often appears as Frostspire Infection.

Can I do all 6 days in one session?

No. Treat it as one successful cold-weather trigger per event day across six consecutive days.

What happens if I miss a day?

The winter guide states that missing a day resets the streak, so restart the chain and use a daily reminder while the event is active.
