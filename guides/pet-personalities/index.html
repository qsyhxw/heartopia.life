🔓 How to Unlock Pets

Unlock Requirements

1
Reach DG Level 12
Complete Resident Requests to earn Contribution Medals
2
Talk to Mrs. Joan
Visit the Pet Adoption Center when prompted by quest
3
Use Hobby Expansion Ticket
Unlock "Cat Caring" first, then "Dog Caring"

💡 Tip: The pet shop resets daily! Check back every day for new pets with different breeds and trait combinations.

🎭 All Personality Traits (Shared)

Each pet has 2 random traits that are NOT determined by breed. These traits affect daily behavior and care needs.

🗣️

Chatterbox

Makes noise more often. Dogs bark frequently, cats meow a lot.

✓ Can help find hidden items ✗ May be distracting
🤗

Clingy

Follows you around on home plot. May damage furniture.

✓ Always nearby ✗ Furniture repairs needed
⚡

Energetic

Prefers to play more often. Very active and lively!

✓ Fun to play with ⚠ Needs more playtime
😐

Indifferent

Not bothered by much. Neutral, chill behavior.

✓ Low maintenance ✓ Easy to care for
😈

Naughty

More likely to misbehave, chew furniture, or dig holes.

✓ Adds personality ✗ Needs "Reprimand" training
🤫

Quiet

Doesn't make much noise. Perfect for peaceful gameplay.

✓ Peaceful ✓ Not distracting
🥺

Sensitive

Picky eater. Requires specific or premium food.

⚠ Needs special food ✗ Higher maintenance
💕

Sociable

Prefers more interactions. Craves attention and affection!

✓ Great bond potential ⚠ Needs frequent petting
🐈‍⬛

Unsociable

Doesn't need many interactions. Prefers independence.

✓ Great for busy players ✓ Low maintenance

🐱 Cat-Only Traits

🐌

Slow

Hunger depletes slowly. You don't have to feed them as often.

✓ Great for busy players
😴

Sluggish

Energy depletes slowly. Stays active longer between rests.

✓ More time to play

🐕 Dog-Only Traits

🐺

Lone Wolf

RARE

Requires very little social interaction to remain happy.

✓ Perfect for explorers
😴

Sluggish

Hunger depletes slowly. Easier to care for, but less active on walks.

✓ Low food cost

⭐ Best Trait Combinations

🏆 Best for Busy Players

  • • Unsociable + Slow/Sluggish
  • • Indifferent + Quiet
  • • Lone Wolf + Sluggish (Dog)

💕 Best for Bonding

  • • Sociable + Energetic
  • • Clingy + Quiet
  • • Chatterbox + Sociable

⚠️ Challenging Combos

  • • Naughty + Clingy - Lots of repairs!
  • • Sensitive + Energetic - High maintenance
  • • Chatterbox + Sociable - Very demanding

💡 Pro Tip

Traits don't prevent progress! Every pet can learn poses, be trained, and reach max bond level. Choose a pet you love, not just "perfect" traits!

💖 Bond & Training System

📈 Increasing Bond Level

Bond level goes up to Level 10 through daily care:

  • 🍖 Feeding - Favorite foods give more affection
  • ✋ Petting - Daily interaction
  • 🎾 Playing - Use toys and games
  • 🎓 Training - Teach tricks and poses

🎭 Pet Collections

  • Kitty Time (Cats)
    Train with teaser toy, unlock poses, take photos for rewards
  • Dog Moment (Dogs)
    Teach tricks like Seek Treasure, Give Paw, Shake Head

💡 Pet Care Tips

Daily Routine

  • • Check pet happiness daily
  • • Feed before hunger drops too low
  • • Play with Energetic/Sociable pets
  • • Train for collection progress

Handling Naughty Pets

  • • Use "Reprimand" action when misbehaving
  • • Repair furniture damage at workbench
  • • Don't ignore bad behavior
  • • Training helps long-term

For Sensitive Pets

  • • Use premium/specific food types
  • • Check food preferences
  • • May reject regular pet food
  • • Cook special meals if needed

Choosing Your Pet

  • • Check traits before adopting
  • • Pet shop resets daily
  • • All pets can reach max bond
  • • Pick a pet you love!
