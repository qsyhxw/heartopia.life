💰 Heartopia Trading Guide

Learn how to sell items, find merchants, share with friends, and maximize your profits in Heartopia!

📢 Current Trading System

Heartopia currently focuses on NPC merchants for buying and selling. Direct player-to-player item trading is not available, but you can share food and collaborate with friends on co-building projects!

💵 1. Selling to Albert Jr.

Albert Jr. is the wandering Gold Merchant — your primary way to sell fish, crops, crafted goods, and more for Gold!

🗺️ How to Find Albert Jr.

  1. 1. Open your Map (press M on PC)
  2. 2. Look for Albert Jr.'s icon — it moves throughout the day!
  3. 3. Travel to his location
  4. 4. Interact and select "Sell Items"
  5. 5. Select items from inventory — Gold is instant!

✅ What You Can Sell

  • • Fish (all types)
  • • Crops and produce
  • • Cooked dishes
  • • Caught insects
  • • Bird info cards
  • • Gathered materials

💡 Selling Tips

  • • Sell multiple items in one transaction
  • • Total earnings calculated in real-time
  • • Cooked dishes sell for MORE than raw ingredients
  • • Higher ★ items = higher prices

🛍️ 2. Special Merchants

Beyond Albert Jr., there are special merchants with unique inventories!

🎪

Doris — Secret Traveling Vendor

A mysterious merchant who appears at random locations with exclusive items!

  • • Rare baking recipes
  • • Event-only furniture
  • • Limited-time items
  • • Check map regularly for her location
🪑

Bob — Furniture Store

Your main source for furniture blueprints and home decoration items.

  • • Furniture blueprints
  • • Daily inventory refresh
  • • Rare items appear randomly
🐱

Mrs. Joan — Pet Shop

Specializes in pet-related items and supplies.

  • • Pet furniture
  • • Pet care items
  • • Daily inventory refresh

🏪 3. NPC Shops

Shop Items Sold Refresh
🛒 General Store Seeds, tools, paint cans, basic supplies Daily
👗 Clothing Store Outfits, accessories, customization Rotates regularly
🪑 Furniture Store Blueprints, furniture, decorations Daily
🌰 Acorn Store Special items, premium goods Varies
💡 Tip: Check shops daily! Stock rotates and rare items can appear. Other players can grab limited items before you!

👥 4. Social Features

While direct item trading isn't available, you can interact with friends in other ways!

🍲 Share Cooked Food

You can share cooked dishes with friends! Great for helping each other with energy and buffs.

📮 Send Postcards

Send postcards to friends as gifts and to strengthen your friendship.

🏠 Co-Build Together

Team up with friends on house construction projects. Doesn't affect your main home!

🎣 Multiplayer Events

Join friends for fishing and bug-catching excursions together!

🎁 Limited Gifting (High Level)

At DG Level 15+ with Friendship Level 3, you may be able to gift certain items like books and CDs to friends. This feature is limited and criteria may vary.

📈 5. Maximize Profits

🍳
Process Before Selling

Cooked dishes sell for much more than raw ingredients. Cook crops and fish before selling!

⭐
Higher Quality = Higher Prices

5-star fish/crops sell for significantly more than 1-star. Focus on quality!

🐟
Fish for Consistent Income

Fishing provides steady income. Rare fish during events are especially valuable!

🏆
Gilded Acorn Membership

Full membership provides 1.1x sell price bonus when selling items!

🎪
Event Currencies

Special event currencies (Wish Stars, Star Fragments) can be traded for exclusive items!

🌟 6. Pro Tips

🗺️
Check Map Regularly

Albert Jr. and Doris change locations. Check map to find them!

📦
Batch Selling

Sell multiple items at once for efficiency. Select all before confirming.

🏪
Shop Early

Rare shop items are competitive. Check daily and buy fast!

💾
Keep Favorites

Don't sell everything — keep items for recipes, gifts, and collections.

👥
Invest in NPCs

Investing in NPC shops can unlock rewards and improve your area!

🎁
Build Friendships

Higher friendship levels may unlock gifting options in the future!
