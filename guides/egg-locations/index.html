Quick Answer

Heartopia egg locations usually refer to the 16 cracked eggs from the Onsen Egg Promise event. Go to Onsen Mountain, start the event chain, then photograph each cracked egg. If you need the cooking dish instead, open the Onsen Egg recipe page and use the Onsen Baths wooden baskets.

Cracked Egg Locations

These are the collectible-looking eggs used by the Onsen Egg Promise hunt. They appear around Onsen Mountain as event checkpoints, and the important action is to photograph the egg with the in-game camera.

  • • Start through the Winter Frost event chain.
  • • Check Onsen Mountain landmarks such as the overlook, rink, domed building, outdoor kitchen and plaza areas.
  • • After each photo, look nearby for the furniture bubble tied to that step.

Onsen Egg Cooking Location

The cooked Onsen Egg is made at the Onsen Baths, not at a normal kitchen stove. Use the small wooden baskets beside the hot spring water and place Pasteurised Eggs inside.

  • • Buy Pasteurised Eggs from Massimo.
  • • Go to the Onsen Baths area.
  • • Interact with the wooden basket, then cook the eggs there.

Egg Hunt Flow

1. UnlockReach the Winter Frost event flow and meet the quest requirements.
2. StartSpeak to the Onsen Mountain NPC tied to the Onsen Egg Promise chain.
3. PhotoFind the cracked egg for the step and take an in-game photo.
4. RewardCheck the nearby bubble and continue through the full 16-egg route.

FAQ

Are egg locations and the Onsen Egg recipe the same thing?

No. Egg locations usually mean the cracked eggs in the Onsen Egg Promise hunt. The recipe page explains how to cook the food item after you unlock it.

Do I need to collect or photograph the eggs?

Use the in-game camera and take a photo of the cracked egg. The photo step is the important part of the hunt flow.

Where do I cook Onsen Eggs?

Cook them at the Onsen Baths using the wooden baskets beside the hot spring water.

Where do Pasteurised Eggs come from?

The Onsen Egg recipe page points players to Massimo for Pasteurised Eggs and shows the cooking-profit details.
