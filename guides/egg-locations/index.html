Quick Answer

The Onsen Egg Promise used one numbered egg for each day of the Winter Frost route. Select a day below, travel to the listed Winter Utopia landmark, and photograph the cracked egg with the in-game camera. The date directory records the original 2026 sequence; use the day number shown by your event progress rather than assuming the calendar date is current.

Fast Route for Days 8–14

  1. Main building cluster: Days 8 and 9 are both on the right side of Winter Utopia's main building.
  2. Vendor cluster: Day 10 is at Blanc's stall; Days 13 and 14 use the Vanya and Massimo stall areas.
  3. Azure cluster: Days 11 and 12 are both left of Azure, first at the palm trunks and then by the rock and red-petal plant.
  4. Camera check: For rooftop eggs, move back until the egg and stall are both inside the photo frame.

Egg Hunt Flow

  1. 1. Check progress: match the event's active day number.
  2. 2. Travel: use the Winter Utopia landmark in the directory.
  3. 3. Photograph: take an in-game camera photo of the cracked egg.
  4. 4. Continue: collect the related bubble before moving on.

Cooking Location Is Different

The hunt eggs are photo targets. The cooked Onsen Egg is made separately at the Onsen Baths using the wooden baskets beside the hot spring water and Pasteurized Eggs.

Egg Location FAQ

Where is the Heartopia Day 8 egg?

It is on top of the pop-up coffee stand to the right of Winter Utopia's main building.

Where are the Day 11 and Day 12 eggs?

Both are left of Azure. Day 11 rests between two palm trunks; Day 12 is beside the red-petal plant on the left of a nearby rock.

Where is the Heartopia Day 13 egg?

Look on top of the fishing stall near Vanya in Winter Utopia.

Do I collect the egg or photograph it?

Use the in-game camera to photograph the cracked egg. The furniture bubble is a separate follow-up reward step.
