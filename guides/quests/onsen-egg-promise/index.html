🥚 Heartopia Egg Locations & Onsen Egg Promise Guide

Find all 16 hidden egg locations on Onsen Mountain to unlock the exclusive Onsen Egg Recipe! Complete walkthrough for daily locations.

📅 Jan 31 - Mar 13 🥘 Recipe Reward 📍 Onsen Mountain

1 Getting Started

Prerequisite DG Level 7 + Main Story Tutorial
Start NPC Naughty (Onsen Mountain Overlook)

Make sure you have completed the prerequisite quests with Eric and Atara first. Then, find Naughty at the overlook to begin the 16-day egg hunt.

⚠️
Important: You must take a photo 📷 of each egg with your in-game camera to "collect" it. Simply clicking it might not work! Be sure to also check for a nearby Furniture Bubble after finding each egg.

2 Daily Egg Locations

Day 1: Beige Cracked Egg

Available Now

Location Hint: Found directly behind Naughty, right where you accept the quest.

Day 2: White Cracked Egg

Feb 1

Location Hint: At the entrance to the Ice Skating Rink (North Beach). Look for it perched on the head of a small moose snow sculpture to the right of the stairs.

Day 3: Green Cracked Egg

Feb 2

Location Hint: Hidden under the staircase that leads up to the roof of the Domed Building (Winter Frost Park).

Day 4: Cyan Cracked Egg

Feb 3

Location Hint: Behind Blanc's Caravan Shop. Check near the rocks close to the lower-ground entrance of the Domed Building.

Day 5: Lavender Cracked Egg

Feb 4

Location Hint: On the awning above Massimo's Outdoor Kitchen by the ice rink. It blends in, so look up!

Day 6: Azure Cracked Egg

Feb 5

Location Hint: Rooftop of the Domed Building. Look for it on a railing post behind the snowman project.

Day 7: Black Cracked Egg

Feb 6

Location Hint: Inside the Domed Building (Lower Floor). It's on a table in the reception seating area near the stove.

Day 8-10 Locations

  • Day 9 (Orange Egg): To the right of the Winter Utopia main building, on a rock under the walkway.
  • Day 10: On a shelf at Blanc's Pop-up Shop (right of blue flowers).

Day 11: Emerald Egg

Feb 10

Location Hint: On a tree near Annie at the Winter Event area, or check the trees at Stone Cliff.

Day 12: Aquamarine Egg

Feb 11

Location Hint: Hidden behind a bush near a palm tree (walking away from Ice Rink stairs), or near Blanc's stall.

Day 13: Blue Egg

Feb 12

Location Hint: On top of Vanya's Fishing Stall on the shoreline. Look for the Blue Cracked Egg facing the sea.

Day 14: Purple Egg

Feb 13

Location Hint: At the very top of Massimo's Cooking Stall. You'll need to climb up to find this Purple Cracked Egg.

📍 Upcoming (Day 15-16)

Unlocks Feb 14 & 15. Check back for final locations!

  • Day 15 (Feb 14): Check Coffee Umbrella behind Igloo / East Pier.
  • Day 16 (Feb 15): Final Egg! Likely near Winter Utopia main area.
