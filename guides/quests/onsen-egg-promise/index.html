🥚 [Walkthrough] How to Get All Eggs in Heartopia

Find all 16 hidden egg locations on Onsen Mountain to unlock the exclusive Onsen Egg Recipe! Complete walkthrough for daily locations.

📅 Jan 31 - Mar 13 🥘 Recipe Reward 📍 Onsen Mountain

1 Getting Started

Prerequisite DG Level 7 + Main Story Tutorial
Start NPC Naughty (Onsen Mountain Overlook)

Make sure you have completed the prerequisite quests with Eric and Atara first. Then, find Naughty at the overlook to begin the 16-day egg hunt.

⚠️
Important: You must take a photo 📷 of each egg with your in-game camera to "collect" it. Simply clicking it might not work! Be sure to also check for a nearby Furniture Bubble after finding each egg.

📍 Onsen Mountain Egg Checkpoints

2 Step-by-Step Egg Walkthrough

Step 1: Beige Cracked Egg

Day 1

Location Coordinate: Found directly behind Naughty, right where you accept the quest.

Step 2: White Cracked Egg

Day 2

Location Coordinate: At the entrance to the Ice Skating Rink (North Beach). Look for it perched on the head of a small moose snow sculpture to the right of the stairs.

Step 3: Green Cracked Egg

Day 3

Location Coordinate: Hidden under the staircase that leads up to the roof of the Domed Building (Winter Frost Park).

Step 4: Cyan Cracked Egg

Day 4

Location Coordinate: Behind Blanc's Caravan Shop. Check near the rocks close to the lower-ground entrance of the Domed Building.

Step 5: Lavender Cracked Egg

Day 5

Location Coordinate: On the awning above Massimo's Outdoor Kitchen by the ice rink. It blends in, so look up!

Step 6: Azure Cracked Egg

Day 6

Location Coordinate: Rooftop of the Domed Building. Look for it on a railing post behind the snowman project.

Step 7: Black Cracked Egg

Day 7

Location Coordinate: Inside the Domed Building (Lower Floor). It's on a table in the reception seating area near the stove.

Steps 8-10 (Red, Orange & Yellow Eggs)

  • Step 8: On top of the pop-up coffee stand to the right of the Winter Utopia main building.
  • Step 9: On the orange rock to the right of the main building, below the wooden walkway.
  • Step 10: On a shelf at Blanc's Pop-up Shop, to the right of the blue flowers.

Step 11: Emerald Egg

Day 11

Location Coordinate: Between two palm trunks to the left of Azure.

Step 12: Aquamarine Egg

Day 12

Location Coordinate: Beside the red-petal plant on the left side of the rock near Azure.

Step 13: Blue Egg

Day 13

Location Coordinate: On top of Vanya's Fishing Stall on the shoreline. Look for the Blue Cracked Egg facing the sea.

Step 14: Purple Egg

Day 14

Location Coordinate: At the very top of Massimo's Cooking Stall. You'll need to climb up to find this Purple Cracked Egg.

📍 Final Steps 15-16

  • Step 15: Check Coffee Umbrella behind Igloo / East Pier.
  • Step 16: The highly anticipated Final Egg! Located right by the center plaza fountain. You're done!
