Heartopia Rare Timber Location

When a blueprint asks for Rare Timber, look for large, thick-trunk trees along the outer roads around residential home plots. Do not spend the route chopping every forest tree, and do not substitute Roaming Oak Timber.

Quick answer: Travel to the residential area, ride one full loop along the outside road, check large trees near corners and slopes, chop them with your axe, and confirm the pickup is named Rare Timber.

Match the exact recipe name

Four wood materials that are easy to confuse

These materials occupy separate inventory entries. A recipe that asks for one cannot be completed with another.

Timber

Basic wood from common trees for early building work.

Quality Timber

A separate higher-grade wood that does not replace Rare Timber.

Rare Timber

The target here: gather it from large, thick trees near residential roads.

Roaming Oak Timber

A different item tied to Roaming Oak and its own unlock route.

A repeatable search pattern

Rare Timber farming route

Start near the home plots

Use a residential waypoint, then move toward the outside road rather than the town center or a random forest path.

Ride one outer-road loop

Follow the road around the residential plots in one direction. Scan corners, small rises, and the edges of each home zone so you do not double back.

Target thick-trunk trees

The useful trees are noticeably larger than ordinary trees. If a tree only gives Timber or Quality Timber, move on instead of repeatedly farming the same tree type.

Verify every pickup

Read the item name in the pickup message or inventory. The wood icons look similar at a glance, but the recipe requires the exact Rare Timber entry.

Heartopia Rare Timber FAQ

Where do you find Rare Timber?

Travel to the residential area and follow the outer roads around the home plots. Look for large, thick-trunk trees near corners, slopes, and zone edges, then confirm the item name after chopping.

Is Rare Timber the same as Roaming Oak Timber?

No. They are separate inventory items. Rare Timber comes from large trees; Roaming Oak Timber is connected to Roaming Oak and has a different acquisition route.

Why do I keep getting Quality Timber?

You are probably chopping another tree type. Leave the general forest route, follow the roads around the residential plots, and prioritize the noticeably larger thick-trunk trees.

When does Rare Timber respawn?

If a previously useful node is empty, check it again after the server reset. Resource timing can change between versions, so a repeatable area loop is more useful than relying on one remembered tree.
