🖌️ 1. Drawing Board & Custom Patterns

📋 Unlock Requirements

  • • DG Member Level 14 required
  • • Complete Dorothee's quest chain
  • • Purchase from General Store

The Drawing Board lets you create custom patterns for both clothing and furniture! Here's how it works:

🎨 Drawing Tools

  • • Adjustable brushes - Multiple sizes
  • • Paint bucket - Fill large areas
  • • Eraser - Fix mistakes
  • • Stamp tool - Pre-made designs (flowers, animals)
  • • Basic + Advanced color palettes

📐 Canvas Options

  • • Various canvas sizes available
  • • Clothing templates for custom outfits
  • • Furniture templates for decor
  • • Designs will have pixelated appearance

💰 Applying Custom Designs

👗 For Clothing:

  1. 1. Select clothing template
  2. 2. Create your design
  3. 3. Use Tailoring Ticket at workbench

🪑 For Furniture:

  1. 1. Select furniture template
  2. 2. Create your design
  3. 3. Use Paint Can at workbench (50 💎)

💎 = Moonlight Crystals. Paint cans available from General Store or quests.

🌈 2. Dyeing System

Many furniture pieces can be dyed to match your design vision. Here's what you need to know:

✨ How Dyeing Works

  • • Enter Build Mode
  • • Select furniture piece
  • • Check if dyeing option is available
  • • Preview colors before applying
  • • Apply your chosen color

✅ Key Benefits

  • • Set bonuses are NOT affected by color changes!
  • • Create cohesive color schemes
  • • Match themes across rooms
  • • Personalize without losing buffs
💡 Tip: Not all furniture can be dyed. Check Build Mode preview first! Joinery-based furniture supports the most customization options.

🏠 3. Room Design Tips

🎨
Color Coordination

Stick to 2-3 main colors. Use accent colors sparingly for visual interest.

⚖️
Symmetry & Balance

Balance large items with comparable visual weight on opposite sides.

💡
Lighting Layers

Use multiple light sources: overhead, table lamps, and floor lights for depth.

🖼️
Wall Decoration

Fill vertical space with paintings, shelves, and wall decorations.

📏
Negative Space

Don't overcrowd! Leave breathing room for a clean, organized look.

🌿
Add Plants

Place plants on tables, in corners, and on shelves for natural beauty.

✨ 4. Furniture Set Bonuses

Furniture belongs to themed sets. Completing sets provides gameplay buffs! Pieces must be placed (not just owned) to activate bonuses.

Room Type Typical Bonuses
🛋️ Living Room Social bonuses, relaxation, reduced stamina costs
🛏️ Bedroom Energy recovery, stamina regeneration
🍳 Kitchen Cooking speed, reduced failure rate, quality boost
🌳 Outdoor Gardening efficiency, gathering bonuses
🔧 Workshop Crafting speed, material efficiency

💡 Strategic Tips

  • • Hybrid Rooms: Mix complementary sets (kitchen + dining) to maximize space
  • • Rotating Displays: Store and swap sets based on current gameplay focus
  • • Permanent Sets: Keep bedroom furniture placed for constant benefits

🏰 5. Design Theme Ideas

🌲 Enchanted Forest Retreat

Natural wood furniture, plants everywhere, earthy tones, fairy lights.

🏰 Fantasy Castle

Grand furniture, stone walls, royal colors (purple, gold), chandeliers.

❄️ Winter Wonderland

White & blue palette, cozy blankets, fireplaces, frost-themed decor.

🌿 Greenhouse

Floor-to-ceiling windows, gardening plots, lots of plants, natural light.

🍰 Bakery / Restaurant

Kitchen equipment, dining tables, warm colors, display cases.

🐾 Animal Sanctuary

Pet furniture, outdoor spaces, natural elements, cozy pet beds.
