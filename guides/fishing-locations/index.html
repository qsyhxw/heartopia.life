💡 Rainbow Weather Tips

  • • Use Mermaid Perfume to increase fish spawn rate during limited rainbow time
  • • Check weather indicator regularly — rainbows can appear after rain
  • • Prioritize Sea Fishing if you have boat access (highest value fish)
  • • If no boat, head to Giantwood River for Huchen or Forest Lake for Pearl Mussel

💡 Location Tips

🏆 Best for Beginners

  • • Meadow Lake - Easy access, starter fish
  • • Suburban Lake - Most variety (12 species)
  • • Tranquil River - Simple fish, quick catches

💰 Best for Money

  • • Sea Fishing (Boat) - Golden King Crab, Bluefin Tuna
  • • Giantwood River - Huchen during rainbow
  • • Meadow Lake - Goldfish sells well

🌙 Night Fishing Spots

  • • Forest Lake - Blue Ero Crayfish (Lv8)
  • • Onsen Mountain Lake - Pumpkinseed, Bluegill
  • • Suburban Lake - Mediterranean Killifish

🌈 Rainbow Priority

  • • Sea Fishing - Golden King Crab, Swordfish
  • • Giantwood River - Huchen
  • • Forest Lake - Large Pearl Mussel
