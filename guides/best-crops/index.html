How to choose

  1. Use the growth time as your main schedule constraint.
  2. Multiply seed price by the number of planters before buying a batch.
  3. Search the recipe-use column when preparing a cooking ingredient.
  4. Do not rely on old raw-crop profit charts: sell values and recipes can change.
