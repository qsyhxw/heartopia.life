Heartopia Best Crops by Playtime

The safest way to choose crops is to match growth time to your next login. This guide uses the current 17-crop database and avoids guessing raw sell prices or profit rates.

Open crops databaseUse crop planner

Active play

Pick 15-60 minute crops when you are staying online and can harvest quickly.

Tomato, Lemon Verbena, Romaine Lettuce, Paddy, Pineapple, Tea Leaf, Potatoes

Evening session

Use 2-6 hour crops when you can check back later the same day.

Carrot, Wheat, Cacao Bean, Eggplant, Strawberry

Workday or sleep

Use 8+ hour crops before school, work, or sleep so plots are not idle.

Lettuce, Grape, Corn, Avocado

How to Choose the Best Crop

For collection progress
Grow every crop once, then mark it in My Progress.
For cooking prep
Plant ingredients you are missing, then check recipe requirements before buying a large seed batch.
For short play sessions
Do not start a 10-hour crop if you plan to harvest in 30 minutes.
For long breaks
Long crops keep planters busy while you are away.

  • Do not treat old sell-price screenshots as permanent.

  • Do not leave planters empty when a quick crop would finish during your session.

Best Crops FAQ

What crop should I plant while actively playing?
Pick a crop that finishes in 15-60 minutes so you can harvest and replant before logging out.

What crop should I plant before sleep?
Use an 8+ hour crop such as Lettuce, Grape, Corn, or Avocado so your planters keep working while you are away.

Does this page rank crops by profit?
No. It ranks crops by practical use: growth time, seed cost, and recipe planning.
