💰 Gold: Main Gameplay Currency

💰 What is Gold? Gold is Heartopia's main free-to-play currency. You'll earn it naturally through gameplay activities and use it for most everyday purchases. No top-up required!

💰 How to Earn Gold

  • • Selling Items: Crops, dishes, crafted goods
  • • Completing Quests: Main story and side quests
  • • Event Tasks: Participate in limited-time events
  • • Daily Activities: Login rewards and daily missions
  • • Fishing & Hobbies: Sell catches and hobby products

🛍️ What to Buy with Gold

  • • Clothing: Outfits and accessories
  • • Recipes: Cooking and crafting recipes
  • • Furniture: Home decorations and upgrades
  • • Seeds: Farming supplies and materials
  • • Upgrades: Tool and facility improvements

🌙 Moonlight Crystals: Premium Special Currency

🌙 What are Moonlight Crystals? Moonlight Crystals are a special premium currency that can be earned for free through redeem codes and events, or purchased with Heart Diamonds. They're perfect for players who want premium benefits without spending real money.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is Heartopia pay-to-win?

No, Heartopia is designed as a cozy life simulation where premium currency provides convenience and cosmetic benefits, not competitive advantages. All core gameplay features are accessible to free players through Gold and event currencies.

Can I earn premium currency for free?

Yes! Moonlight Crystals and Wishing Stars can be earned through redeem codes, events, achievements, and social connections. While Heart Diamonds are primarily purchased, small amounts can be earned through special events and achievements.

What's the best first purchase?

The battle pass or seasonal pass typically offers the best value for your first purchase, providing a steady stream of rewards as you play. First-time purchase bonuses also make smaller packages more valuable initially.

Do currencies expire?

No, all currencies in Heartopia are permanent and do not expire. You can save them as long as you want for future purchases or limited-time items.
