Before you pay

Six checks that prevent mistakes

  1. 1. Confirm the account

    Use the Heartopia account you intend to fund.

  2. 2. Match region and server

    A mismatch can send delivery to the wrong profile.

  3. 3. Check the exact product

    Memberships, packs, and Heart Diamonds differ.

  4. 4. Read the final price

    Currency conversion and offers can change.

  5. 5. Inspect the login domain

    Social sign-in should use the genuine provider domain.

  6. 6. Save the receipt

    Keep the order number until delivery is complete.

Stop before paying if...

  • A password is requested outside the genuine provider domain.
  • You cannot identify the correct server.
  • Product, quantity, currency, or total changed.
  • Refund support is unavailable.
