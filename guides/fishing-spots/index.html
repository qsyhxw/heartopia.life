🔓 How to Unlock Fishing

Fishing unlocks early in the game through the main story:

  1. Reach DG Level 2 by completing story quests
  2. Meet Vanya outside your home — he gives you a broken fishing rod
  3. Craft a Repair Kit (5x Branch) to fix the rod
  4. Catch 3 fish at the nearest pond to complete the tutorial

After that, you can fish at any water body with visible fish shadows.

🌈 Rainbow Weather Fishing Guide

Rainbow weather is rare and temporary. When it appears, prioritize these locations for the highest-value catches.

🎯 Rainbow Priority Route

1
Deep Sea (Boat)
Golden King Crab (Lv8), Bluefin Tuna (Lv9), Swordfish (Lv10)
2
Starlight Sea
Rainbow Fish (Lv10) — 5,000G, the rarest catch
3
Giantwood River
Huchen (Lv9) — exclusive to this location
4
Forest Lake / Meadow Lake
Large Pearl Mussel (Lv6) / Wels Catfish (Lv10)

💡 Rainbow Weather Tips

  • • Rainbows often appear after rain — check the weather indicator regularly
  • • Use Mermaid Perfume to increase fish spawn rate during limited rainbow time
  • • If you don't have boat access, head to Giantwood River or Forest Lake
  • • Rainbow weather affects all locations simultaneously

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How many fishing locations are in Heartopia? ▼
Heartopia has 14+ fishing locations: 4 lakes (Meadow, Forest, Suburban, Onsen Mountain), 3 rivers (Tranquil, Giantwood, Rosy), 4 sea areas (Zephyr, East, Old, Whale), deep sea fishing via boat, plus special spots like Starlight Sea and Secret Pond.
Where is the best fishing location in Heartopia? ▼
It depends on your goal. For beginners, Suburban Lake has the most variety (12 species). For profit, Deep Sea Fishing gives access to Swordfish, Bluefin Tuna, and Golden King Crab. For rare fish, Giantwood River during rainbow weather is the only spot for Huchen.
How do I unlock deep sea fishing? ▼
Reach DG Level 7, then talk to Bill in the Fishing Village to rent a boat. This unlocks deep sea fishing with exclusive high-value fish like Swordfish (Lv10), Bluefin Tuna (Lv9), and Golden King Crab (Lv8).
What fish can I catch during rainbow weather? ▼
Rainbow weather unlocks the rarest fish: Rainbow Fish (Starlight Sea, 5,000G), Swordfish (Deep Sea), Bluefin Tuna (Deep Sea), Golden King Crab (Deep Sea), Huchen (Giantwood River), Wels Catfish (Meadow Lake), and Large Pearl Mussel (Forest Lake).
Where can I find the Rainbow Fish? ▼
The Rainbow Fish can only be caught at Starlight Sea during rainbow weather. It requires Fishing Level 10 and sells for 5,000G — the most expensive fish in the game.
