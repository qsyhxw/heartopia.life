Location Guide

Heartopia Best Fishing Spots & Rare Fish Locations Map

Fishing is one of the most profitable and relaxing hobbies in Heartopia. Whether you are trying to complete your encyclopedia, catch legendary fish, or maximize your Gold income, knowing the exact fishing spots is essential.

🗺️

Looking for the Interactive Map?

If you want a live view of all fishing locations, gathering nodes, and NPC locations, be sure to check out our complete Heartopia Interactive Map tool!

🎣 Best General Fishing Locations

If you are just starting out and need a reliable place to level up your fishing proficiency while earning steady gold, these are the top spots:

1. Meadow Lake (Beginner Friendly)

Meadow Lake is easily accessible and features mostly common and uncommon freshwater fish. It’s the perfect place to safely grind your Fishing Level from 1 to 5 without wasting expensive baits or time.

2. Fishing Village Docks

Once you unlock the coastal area, the docks at the Fishing Village provide a great mix of saltwater fish. This area is also near the bait shop, making long fishing sessions incredibly convenient for inventory management.

🦈 Where to Find Specific Rare Fish

Certain fish in Heartopia are notoriously difficult to track down. Here are the locations for some of the most highly-searched rare catches:

⭐ Legendary Wels Catfish Location

The Wels Catfish is a massive, highly sought-after legendary catch. It has very strict spawn conditions.

  • 📍 Location: Meadow Lake
  • 🕒 Time of Day: Evening and Night (18:00 - 05:00 in-game time)
  • 🌤️ Weather: Sunny or Rainbow weather.
  • 🎣 Requirement: You must be at least Fishing Level 10 to reel it in.

The "European Mudwinnow" Mystery

Many players have been searching the map for the European Mudwinnow, often encountering difficulties finding it. Currently, in the global version of Heartopia, this fish’s exact designated spawn pool remains mostly unconfirmed by official drop tables (it may also appear under translated spelling variations like Mudminnow or general Loach species in certain regions).

If you are hunting for generic loaches or mudminnows to complete a quest, we recommend sweeping the shallow river banks connecting to Meadow Lake during rainy weather, as similar species tend to spawn there. (We will update this exact coordinate once the official encyclopedia logs the precise spawn logic).

🌊 The "Under the Sea" Location Explained

A huge question in the community is: “Where is the Under the Sea location?”

The truth is, there is no official explorable underwater biome for walking around in Heartopia right now. When players talk about being "under the sea," they are usually referring to one of two things:

  1. Sea Fishing Event: To catch deep-sea shadows, you need to talk to the NPC Bill at the Fishing Village. He will allow you to participate in the "Sea Fishing" instanced event from a boat.
  2. The "Submarine" Terrain Glitch: You may have seen screenshots on social media of characters walking on the ocean floor. This is a known map glitch (often triggered by using a vehicle to clip through the coastal borders). It does not offer any secret fish, and developers occasionally patch these boundary breaks.
