💡 Rainbow Weather Tips

  • • Use Mermaid Perfume to boost fish spawn rate during limited rainbow time
  • • Check the weather indicator regularly — rainbows can appear shortly after rain
  • • If you have boat access, prioritise Deep Sea for Golden King Crab, Bluefin Tuna, and Swordfish
  • • No boat? Go to Giantwood River (Huchen) or Forest Lake (Large Pearl Mussel)

🦈 Rare Fish Spotlights

⭐ Legendary Wels Catfish Location

The Wels Catfish is the most sought-after legendary freshwater catch. It has strict conditions — rainbow weather is mandatory.

  • 📍 Location: Meadow Lake
  • 🕒 Time of Day: All Day (catches confirmed across multiple times)
  • 🌈 Weather: Rainbow weather only
  • 🎣 Requirement: Fishing Level 10

🔍 European Mudminnow Location

The European Mudminnow (sometimes searched as "Mudwinnow") is confirmed at Suburban Lake — Lv8, available in sunny and rainbow weather, all day long. It does not require rain or night conditions.

  • 📍 Location: Suburban Lake
  • 🕒 Time: All Day
  • ☀️ Weather: Sunny or Rainbow
  • 🎣 Requirement: Fishing Level 8

🌊 The "Under the Sea" Location — Explained

A common question in the community: "Where is the Under the Sea location in Heartopia?"

The truth is, there is no official explorable underwater biome for walking around in Heartopia. When players talk about being "under the sea," they usually mean one of two things:

  1. Sea Fishing Event (Bill's Boat): To access deep-sea shadows, talk to the NPC Bill at the Fishing Village. You need DG Level 7 to unlock his boat rental, which takes you to the deep sea fishing event area.
  2. The "Submarine" Terrain Glitch: Screenshots of characters walking on the ocean floor circulate on social media. This is a known map boundary glitch (triggered by clipping through coastal borders with a vehicle). It does not offer secret fish and is occasionally patched by the developers.

💡 Location Tips & Best Spots

🏆 Best for Beginners

  • • Meadow Lake — Easy access, starter fish, level 1–10 scaling
  • • Suburban Lake — Most variety (12 species), great for EXP grinding
  • • Tranquil River — Simple fish, quick catches, no travel required

💰 Best for Gold Farming

  • • Deep Sea (Boat) — Golden King Crab, Bluefin Tuna (all require 🌈)
  • • Giantwood River — Huchen during rainbow
  • • Meadow Lake — Goldfish (Lv8) sells very well, no rainbow needed

🌙 Best Night Fishing Spots

  • • Forest Lake — Blue Ero Crayfish (Lv8, night only)
  • • Onsen Mountain Lake — Pumpkinseed + Bluegill (Eve/Night)
  • • Suburban Lake — Mediterranean Killifish (Eve/Night)

🌈 Rainbow Priority Rotation

  • 1. If Level 8+: Deep Sea → Golden King Crab / Swordfish
  • 2. If Level 9: Giantwood River → Huchen
  • 3. If Level 6+: Forest Lake → Large Pearl Mussel
  • 4. If Level 10: Meadow Lake → Wels Catfish
