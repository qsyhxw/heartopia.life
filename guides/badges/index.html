🔓 How Badges Work in Heartopia

Achievement badges are built-in goals tied to hobbies, progression, events, and social play. They unlock at D.G. Member Level 15 and are accessed through the Watch app on your in-game phone.

📊 Unlock Requirement

Reach D.G. Level 15 to access the Achievement system. You'll see "Feature Unlocked: Achievement" in your D.G. Member rewards.

🎖️ Profile Display

Display up to 3 badges on your player profile. Each badge also grants a unique profile title visible to other players.

🔍 Hidden Badges

Some badges are hidden until you complete them. Keep experimenting with different activities to discover secret achievements.

💡 How to Reach D.G. Level 15: Complete daily Resident Requests (5 per day) to earn Contribution Medals. These medals are the only way to level up your D.G. membership. Focus on daily requests consistently — it's the fastest path to unlocking badges.

⭐ Easiest Badges to Earn First

New to badges? Start with these — they're the most straightforward to unlock through normal gameplay.

🎣

Never Empty-Handed

Reach Fishing Level 10 → Title: Skilled Angler

Fishing is the first hobby (D.G. Level 2). Just keep fishing daily!

🐱

Meow-Meow Canteen

Feed 5 Cats their favorite food → Title: Cat Food

Find cats around town and feed them. Check animal troughs for locations.

🐕

Doggie Canteen

Feed 3 Dogs their favorite food → Title: Dog Food

Only 3 dogs needed — one of the quickest badges to complete.

🌱

Green Touch

Reach Gardening Level 10 → Title: Horticulturist

Gardening unlocks at D.G. Level 3. Water and harvest daily for steady progress.

🍳

Town Cooking Legend

Reach Cooking Level 10 → Title: Chef

Cooking unlocks at D.G. Level 5. Cook recipes daily to level up.

💡 Badge Earning Tips

📋

Do Daily Requests First

Complete 5 Resident Requests daily to earn Contribution Medals and level up your D.G. membership toward Level 15.

🎣

Focus on One Hobby at a Time

Reaching Level 10 in any hobby earns a mastery badge. Fishing is the first available (D.G. Level 2) — start there.

🐾

Feed Animals Daily

Pet care badges like Meow-Meow Canteen and Doggie Canteen are quick wins. Feed cats and dogs their favorite food.

🌈

Watch for Special Weather

Rainbow weather triggers Plentiful Harvest and Rainbow Luck badges. Meteor Showers are needed for Stardust Collector.

🎪

Join Hobby Events

Many challenge badges (Shark Frenzy, Starlight Fisher, etc.) require completing tasks within a single hobby event.

📅

Don't Miss Seasonal Hobbies

Sand Sculpting (Summer), Pumpkin Carving (Fall), and Snow Sculpting (Winter) are only available during their season.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I unlock badges in Heartopia? ▼
Achievement badges unlock at D.G. Member Level 15. Once unlocked, open the Watch app on your in-game phone to view all available badges. Complete specific tasks tied to hobbies, progression, events, and social play to earn each badge and its profile title.
How many badges can I display on my profile? ▼
You can display up to 3 achievement badges on your player profile at a time. Each badge also grants a unique profile title that other players can see when they visit your profile.
What are the easiest badges to get? ▼
The easiest badges are hobby mastery badges earned by reaching Level 10. Fishing is the first hobby (D.G. Level 2), so Never Empty-Handed is typically the first badge most players earn. Pet care badges like Meow-Meow Canteen (feed 5 cats) and Doggie Canteen (feed 3 dogs) are also quick.
Are there hidden badges in Heartopia? ▼
Yes, some badges are hidden until you complete them. They won't appear in your Watch app badge list until the requirement is met. Keep experimenting with different activities and hobby events to discover secret achievements.
Can I earn seasonal badges after the season ends? ▼
No. Seasonal badges (Sand Sculpture Artist, Pumpkinarchy, Snow King) can only be earned during their respective season. If you miss one, you'll need to wait until the same season returns the following year.
