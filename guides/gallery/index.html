🖼️ Heartopia Gallery Guide

Learn how to view Gallery, check Exhibition Passes, unlock the Drawing Board, complete Brushstroke Whimsy, and use custom clothing or furniture designs.

⚡ Quick Answer: How to View Gallery

  • 📷 Photo Album: Open Watch Menu → Album → View/Download photos
  • 🎨 Painting Gallery: Requires DG Level 14 → Finish "Brushstroke Whimsy" quest → Use Drawing Board
  • 🖌️ Design Gallery: Interact with Drawing Board → Browse community designs
  • 🎟️ Exhibition Pass: Check Gallery or Exhibition prompts, event rewards, mail, and claim buttons after unlocking the Drawing Board

📷 1. Photo Album (Screenshots)

Heartopia has a built-in photo mode that lets you capture screenshots during gameplay. All photos are saved to your in-game album.

📸 How to Take Photos

  1. 1. Open your Quick Tools Menu (backpack icon)
  2. 2. Select the Camera tool
  3. 3. Use the grid to align your shot
  4. 4. Press the shutter button to capture
  5. 5. Photo is automatically saved to your Album!

🖼️ How to View Your Photo Album

  1. 1 Open your Watch Menu (watch icon in top-right corner)
  2. 2 Select "Album" from the menu
  3. 3 Browse your saved photos
  4. 4 Click "Download" to save a photo to your device

💾 Where Are Downloaded Photos Saved?

⚠️ Important: You must download photos from the Album first before they appear on your device!

🖥️ PC (Steam)

Check your Downloads folder or Steam screenshot directory

📱 Mobile

Photos save to your device's Gallery/Photos app

🖌️ 3. Brushstroke Whimsy Quest

The "Brushstroke Whimsy" quest is where many players get stuck because it asks you to "View Gallery". Here's exactly what to do:

❓ Stuck on "View Gallery"?

The quest asks you to "View Gallery" but doesn't explain where it is. The Gallery is accessed through the Drawing Board after you place it!

📋 Quest Walkthrough

  1. 1
    Ensure you have the Drawing Board

    Purchase from Ka Ching for 2,000 Gold if you haven't

  2. 2
    Place the Drawing Board anywhere

    Open inventory → Select Drawing Board → Place on ground

  3. 3
    Interact with the Drawing Board

    Tap/click on it to open the painting interface

  4. 4
    Select "Gallery" or browse designs

    This completes the "View Gallery" objective!

  5. 5
    Create a painting (optional for quest)

    Finish the painting to finish the quest

✅ After completing Brushstroke Whimsy: The "Painted Furniture" quest becomes available from Ka Ching, letting you customize furniture with your designs!

🎟️ 5. Exhibition Passes & Design Sharing

Exhibition Pass searches usually come from the Gallery or design-sharing flow. Treat the pass as a limited Gallery-related item: check the Gallery screen first, then event and mail rewards, before spending paid design items such as Paint Cans or Tailoring Tickets.

How to check for passes

Place the Drawing Board, open Gallery or Exhibition, then look for your pass count, claim buttons, or prompts on the design screen.

Where more passes may appear

Check event rewards, mailbox gifts, login rewards, Gallery tasks, and any limited exhibition banner shown in your version.

Before spending one

Confirm the design type first. Clothes need compatible clothing and Tailoring Tickets; furniture needs compatible furniture and Paint Cans.

👗 6. Custom Clothes & Furniture

After completing the painting quests, you can apply your designs to clothes and furniture!

👕 Customizing Clothes

Requirements:

  • • Tailoring Ticket: 80 Moonlight Crystals
  • • A completed custom design
  • • Compatible clothing item

Steps:

  1. 1. Create design on Drawing Board
  2. 2. Select clothing base
  3. 3. Apply design with Tailoring Ticket

🪑 Customizing Furniture

Requirements:

  • • Paint Can: 50 Moonlight Crystals
  • • Joinery-based furniture only
  • • Finish "Painted Furniture" quest

Steps:

  1. 1. Go to your Workbench
  2. 2. Select the brush icon 🖌️
  3. 3. Choose furniture to customize
  4. 4. Select design & apply paint

💡 7. Tips & Troubleshooting

❌ "I can't find the Gallery option"

Make sure you've placed the Drawing Board on the ground first. The Gallery is accessed by interacting with the placed board, not from your inventory.

❌ "Drawing Board not in Ka Ching's shop"

You need to be DG Level 14 AND have started the quest from Dorothee first. Check your quest log!

❌ "Can't download photos from Album"

On PC, check your Downloads folder. On mobile, check your Photos/Gallery app. You must click the download button in-game first.

❌ "Furniture won't accept my design"

Only joinery-based (wooden) furniture can be customized with patterns. Metal or stone furniture cannot be painted.

FAQ

How do I get Exhibition Passes in Heartopia?

Unlock the Drawing Board, open Gallery or Exhibition, then check the pass count, claim buttons, event rewards, and mailbox. If no reward or claim prompt is visible, more passes may not be available from normal Gallery actions at that moment.

How do I use an Exhibition Pass?

Use it only when the Gallery or Exhibition screen clearly asks you to spend an Exhibition Pass. Check the design type first so you do not spend a pass on a design you cannot apply yet.

How do I view Gallery in Heartopia?

For photo Gallery, open Watch Menu and Album. For design Gallery, place the Drawing Board, interact with it, and choose Gallery or browse designs.

Can I share clothing patterns?

Use Gallery or design screenshots when your version supports sharing. To apply a clothing design, you still need a compatible clothing item and a Tailoring Ticket.
