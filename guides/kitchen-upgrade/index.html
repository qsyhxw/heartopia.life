🔓 Unlocking the Cooking Hobby

Requirements

1
Reach Developer Level 5
At DG5, you receive a Hobby Expansion Ticket
2
Find Massimo at his Café
Located in the town center, outside the café
3
Use Your Hobby Expansion Ticket
Choose Cooking to unlock the hobby

⭐ Why Choose Cooking First?

  • • Best early-game profit — dishes sell for more than raw ingredients
  • • Synergizes with Fishing & Gardening — uses ingredients you already collect
  • • Passive income — dishes cook while you do other activities

💡 Missed DG5? Don't worry! You can also get Hobby Expansion Tickets at DG Level 8 and DG Level 11.

🔥 Getting Your First Stove

🔥

Free Stove from Massimo!

When you unlock the Cooking hobby, Massimo will gift you:

🔥 1 Stove
Place in your home
📜 Stove Blueprint
Craft more stoves

Placing Your Stove

  1. 1. Go to your home/farmhouse
  2. 2. Enter Build Mode (furniture icon)
  3. 3. Find the Stove in your inventory
  4. 4. Place it in your kitchen area

🏠 Expanding Your Kitchen

There's no limit to how many stoves you can have! More stoves = cook more dishes simultaneously.

Crafting Additional Stoves

🔥 Stove
Blueprint from: Massimo (free with cooking unlock)
Craft at: Crafting Table in your home

Use the Stove Blueprint Massimo gave you to craft as many stoves as you need at your Crafting Table!

📜 Recipe Unlocks by Level

As you level up Cooking, Massimo unlocks more recipes for purchase:

Level Sample Recipes Unlocked
Lv.1 Basic recipes (Toast, Scrambled Eggs, Mixed Jam)
Lv.2 ☕ Coffee, 🥯 Smoked Fish Bagel
Lv.3 🍝 Seafood Risotto, 🥘 Rustic Ratatouille, 🍝 Black Truffle Cream Pasta
Lv.4 More main dishes, desserts start appearing
Lv.5 Advanced soups and stews
Lv.6-8 Premium dishes with higher profits
Lv.9-10 🍽️ Gourmet meals — highest profit recipes!

Other Ways to Get Recipes

  • • Massimo's Gossip Quests — Complete for special recipes (e.g., Mushroom Pie)
  • • Doris — Sells unique recipes like Original Roll Cake (costs Wish Stars)
  • • Recipe Variants — Using different ingredients can unlock new variations

📈 Leveling Up Cooking

How Cooking XP Works

  1. 1 Cook dishes to fill your proficiency meter
  2. 2 When meter is full, you need Hobby Upgrade Tickets
  3. 3 Use tickets to level up and unlock new recipes

🎫 Getting Hobby Upgrade Tickets

  • → Collection Point Rewards — Collect items for bonus tickets
  • → DG Level Rewards — Earned at certain Developer levels
  • → Events & Quests — Special rewards from activities

🚀 Fast Leveling Tips

  • • Cook recipe variants — Different ingredients give bonus XP
  • • Use multiple stoves — More dishes = more XP per session
  • • Cook while AFK — Let dishes cook while doing other activities
  • • Prioritize new recipes — First-time cooking gives extra XP

💡 Kitchen Tips & Tricks

✅ Do

  • • Build 4-6 stoves for efficient batch cooking
  • • Place stoves close together for easy switching
  • • Stock up on ingredients from Garden and Fishing
  • • Cook high-profit dishes once unlocked
  • • Complete Massimo's quests for exclusive recipes
  • • Try recipe variants for more XP and unlocks

❌ Don't

  • • Sell raw ingredients — cook them for more profit!
  • • Ignore cooking timers — collect finished dishes promptly
  • • Skip low-level recipes — you need XP from everything
  • • Forget about Doris — she sells unique recipes

💰 Kitchen Profit Guide

Best Profit Strategy

🥇
Focus on dishes using your own ingredients
Garden crops + caught fish = free ingredients = pure profit
🥈
Cook in bulk with multiple stoves
4-6 stoves running = 4-6x the gold per cooking session
🥉
Prioritize high-level recipes once unlocked
Gourmet dishes (Lv.9-10) have the best profit margins
