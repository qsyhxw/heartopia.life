🏠 Heartopia Building Guide

Learn how to expand your home plots, gather building materials, unlock blueprints, and create your dream home in Heartopia!

🔨 1. Building Basics

Building is one of the core features in Heartopia! You can construct anything from cozy cottages to elaborate castles. The game encourages creativity with flexible building tools.

🎮 Build Mode Controls

  • • Use the Builder Tablet to enter Build Mode
  • • Move, rotate, and store furniture freely
  • • Take down walls and modify layouts
  • • Paint walls and customize colors

🛠️ Workbench (DG Level 1)

  • • Craft furniture from blueprints + materials
  • • Unlocked after intro quests
  • • More recipes unlock as you progress
  • • Use gathered resources for crafting
💡 Tip: You can completely demolish your home and start fresh at any time! Don't be afraid to experiment.

🏗️ 2. Expanding Home Plots

You start with 3 housing plots. To build bigger, you need to unlock additional Home Plots through the Developer's Guild.

📈 How to Unlock More Plots

  1. 1. Increase your DG Member Level by earning Contribution Medals
  2. 2. Complete daily resident requests for medals
  3. 3. New plots unlock as DG level-up rewards
  4. 4. Open Developer's Guild app → Click the "four squares" icon
  5. 5. Select and purchase available plots
DG Level Plots Available Total Plots
Level 1 Starting plots 3
Level 5+ +2 plots unlocked 5
Level 10+ +2 more plots 7+
Higher Levels Additional expansions 12+

🪵 3. Gathering Materials

Building requires various materials. Here's where to find them:

🪵 Wood Types

  • Basic Wood: Chop trees & bushes (everywhere)
  • Quality Timber: Palm trees
  • Rare Timber: Large trees in Suburban areas
  • Roaming Oak: Daily-moving NPC Oak tree
  • Bamboo: Forest areas only

⛏️ Stone & Ore

  • Stone: Mining rock deposits
  • Iron Ore: Regular rock deposits
  • Silver Ore: Less common deposits
  • Gold Ore: Rare deposits
  • Meteor Ore: Special/event locations

🌸 Special Materials

  • Fluorite: Specific locations
  • Mushrooms: Forest foraging
  • Flowers: Various biomes
  • Event Materials: Limited-time events

💡 Gathering Tips

  • • Check starting plot area for stone
  • • Look for the moving NPC Oak daily
  • • Forest has best bamboo supply
  • • Stock up during events for rare materials

📐 4. Blueprints & Furniture

Get blueprints to craft and place furniture in your home:

🏪 Shops (Daily Refresh)

  • • Bob's Furniture Store — Main furniture
  • • Mrs. Joan's Shop — Pet-related items
  • • Acorn Store — Special furniture
  • • General Store — Basic items

⚠️ Rare blueprints are competitive — other players can grab them!

🎈 Prize Bubbles

  • • Spawn at specific locations
  • • Town Square, Fishing Village, Amazing Forest
  • • Contain furniture blueprints
  • • Check regularly for new spawns

🔬 Research Room (DG Level 27)

Unlock at Developer Level 27 to upgrade interactive furniture and access exclusive set items not found in regular shops!

🎨 Custom Design: Purchase a Drawing Board from General Store to design your own furniture patterns, then apply them at the workbench!

✨ 5. Furniture Set Bonuses

Completing furniture sets provides real gameplay benefits! This is why strategic furniture choices matter.

Bonus Type Effect Example Sets
⚡ Energy Reduction Less energy used for actions Various comfort sets
⏱️ Crafting Speed Faster workbench crafting Workshop sets
💕 NPC Relationship Improved friendship gains Living room sets
🎣 Hobby Boosts Better hobby performance Themed hobby sets
💡 Note: Changing furniture color does NOT affect set bonuses! Feel free to customize colors.

🌟 6. Pro Building Tips

🏠
