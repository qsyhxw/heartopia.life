💡 Beetle Catching Tips

✅ Do

  • • Approach slowly — beetles scare easily!
  • • Hold bubble button for precise aiming on rare beetles
  • • Check weather daily — many beetles need specific weather
  • • Hunt at night (6PM-6AM) — most rare beetles are nocturnal
  • • Use Inflatable Insect Attractor to lure more beetles
  • • Focus on Forest areas — highest beetle density

❌ Don't

  • • Run near beetles — they will fly away
  • • Miss Rainbow weather — many rare beetles only spawn then
  • • Forget to level up Netting Skill — increases range
  • • Sell without checking Collection — keep one of each!
