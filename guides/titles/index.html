❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I unlock achievements?

Reach D.G. Level 15 to access the Achievement system via the Watch app.

How many titles can I display?

Up to 3 badges on your profile. Go to Watch → Profile → Edit Title.

What are hidden achievements?

They show as "???" until unlocked. Often involve social actions like sharing items or visiting friends.
