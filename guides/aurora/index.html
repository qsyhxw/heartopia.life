🌌 Heartopia Aurora Guide: Schedule & Locations

A practical Heartopia aurora guide for checking the weather forecast, finding open viewing spots, taking photos, and avoiding confusion with meteor showers.

Quick Answer

How to Find Aurora

Open the Smartwatch weather forecast and look for aurora weather. It is easiest to notice after 6 PM server time when the sky is dark.

Best Locations

Use open views like Onsen Mountain, Crater Lake, bridges, residential streets, or lakeside paths where buildings and trees do not block the sky.

Main Use

Aurora is best for screenshots, social photos, and cozy night scenes. For controls, use the photo mode guide.

Not a Meteor Shower

Aurora does not use the wish emote. For shooting stars and friend achievements, use the meteor shower guide.

✨ What is the Aurora Effect?

The Aurora Borealis (also known as Northern Lights) is a special weather phenomenon in Heartopia that lights up the night sky with shifting bands of green, pink, and purple light.

ℹ️ Important: Unlike the Meteor Shower, the Aurora is a purely cosmetic event. It does not drop special items (like Star Fragments) and does not spawn unique fish or insects. It is designed for atmosphere and photography.

📅 When Does It Appear?

The Aurora is a night-time weather event. While it can appear randomly, your best tool for predicting it is your Smartwatch.

  • ⌚

    Check the Weather App

    Open your in-game watch and check the weather forecast. Look for a distinct "Aurora" icon (different from clear night or rain).

  • 🌙

    Time Window

    The effect typically becomes visible after 6:00 PM (18:00) server time and lasts until early morning.

📸 Best Viewing Locations

While the sky changes across the map, higher elevations and open areas provide the best backdrop for photos.

🏔️ Onsen Mountain

The high elevation of the Onsen Mountain area makes it the premier spot for viewing the lights. Try positioning yourself near the Crater Lake or the hot springs.

🏡 Residential Streets

Many players report beautiful views right from the street outside their homes. It's a perfect opportunity for a neighborhood photo shoot!

🌠 Aurora, Meteor Shower & Rainbow Blessing

Meteor Shower Shooting stars across the sky. Use the wish emote with friends.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Does the Aurora give any items? ▼

No. Currently, the Aurora does not drop items or provide resources.

Are there special Aurora fish? ▼

No exclusive fish have been confirmed for the Aurora weather condition. For rare fish, check out our Fishing Guide.

Player Notes

  • Aurora is mainly a visual weather effect, so plan it for screenshots and cozy night scenes.
  • For wish emotes, shooting stars, and friend achievements, use the meteor shower guide.
  • Check your Smartwatch forecast before traveling to Onsen Mountain or other scenic spots.
