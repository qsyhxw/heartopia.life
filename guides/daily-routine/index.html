📋 Resident Requests

Resident Requests are your main source of Contribution Medals for DG leveling!

📱 How to Access

  • • Calendar icon near your map
  • • OR "Memo" app on your Watch
  • • Simple tasks: deliver items, gather materials

🎁 Rewards Per Request

  • • 10 Contribution Medals
  • • 5 Wishing Stars
  • • 1,000 Gold
  • • Total daily: 50 medals, 25 stars, 5000G
💡 Tip: Contribution Medals are time-gated — the only way to earn them is Resident Requests. Don't skip these!

⚡ Energy Management

Energy is consumed by tool actions (fishing, chopping, mining). Plan wisely!

😴 Restore Energy (Free)

  • • Sleep in bed — 1 energy per 5 seconds
  • • Free but slow
  • • Great for AFK breaks

🍲 Restore Energy (Fast)

  • • Eat food — Instant restore
  • • Eat low-quality dishes yourself
  • • Sell high-quality dishes for profit
🎮 Good News: Walking, building, and socializing do NOT consume energy. Only tool-based actions use energy!
