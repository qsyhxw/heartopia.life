🎨 1. Popular Design Themes

Choose a cohesive design theme to make your home feel polished and intentional. Here are the most popular styles in the Heartopia community:

🏠 Cozy Cottage

Warm, inviting spaces with soft textures, warm lighting, and comfortable furniture.

Wooden furniture Warm rugs Candles Soft pillows

🏢 Modern Minimalist

Clean lines, neutral colors, and functional pieces. Less is more!

Simple furniture Neutral colors Open space Sleek decor

🌿 Nature Haven

Bring the outdoors in with plants, natural materials, and earthy tones.

Indoor plants Roaming Oak items Natural wood Green accents

🎀 Pastel Dream

Soft, dreamy colors create a whimsical and playful atmosphere.

Pink furniture Lavender accents Cute decor Fairy lights

🪵 Rustic Farmhouse

Country charm with distressed wood, vintage pieces, and warm colors.

Barn wood Vintage items Mason jars Checkered patterns

🏖️ Coastal Beach

Ocean-inspired decor with blues, whites, and sandy neutrals.

Blue & white Shell decor Light fabrics Ocean vibes
💡 Theme Tip: Pick 2-3 main colors and stick to them throughout your home. Use accent colors sparingly for pops of interest!

🛋️ 2. Room-by-Room Ideas

Each room serves a different purpose. Here are design suggestions for different spaces:

🛋️ Living Room

  • • Center seating around a focal point (fireplace, TV)
  • • Add rugs and side tables for warmth
  • • Include bookshelves for personality
  • • Layer lighting: overhead + table lamps

🛏️ Bedroom

  • • Position bed against a wall
  • • Add bedside tables on both sides
  • • Use soft lighting for restful atmosphere
  • • Tip: Beds restore energy!

🍳 Kitchen

  • • Group cooking appliances together
  • • Add a dining table nearby
  • • Display your favorite recipes!
  • • Tip: Place up to 3 stoves for efficiency

📚 Study/Office

  • • Include a desk and good lighting
  • • Add bookshelves for storage
  • • Personal touches: awards, photos
  • • Keep it organized and functional

🎵 Music Hall

  • • Create a stage area with seating
  • • Note: Instruments are playable!
  • • Add multiple instruments for friends
  • • Great for parties and social events

🌻 Garden Room / Sunroom

  • • Lots of plants and natural light
  • • Garden-themed furniture
  • • Bird perches and waterfowl ponds
  • • Display vivariums for bugs/fish

🛒 4. Getting Furniture & Blueprints

Building your dream home requires furniture! Here's how to acquire blueprints and items:

🫧 Blueprint Bubbles

  • • Floating bubbles around town
  • • Spawn in random locations daily
  • • Pop them for free blueprints!
  • • Warning: Shared spawns — other players can grab them

🏪 Shops (Daily Rotation)

  • • Bob's Furniture Store — Main furniture
  • • Mrs. Joan's Shop — Blueprint scrolls
  • • Blanc's Gardening Store — Outdoor items
  • • Check right after daily reset!

❤️ NPC Friendship

  • • Bob Lv.4 → Farmhouse Bed blueprint
  • • Massimo Lv.6 → Oak Dining Table
  • • Give gifts to increase friendship!
  • • Prioritize Bob, Blanc, Massimo

🎉 Events & Rewards

  • • Seasonal events have limited furniture
  • • Quest rewards unlock unique items
  • • House upgrades unlock new options
  • • Never skip events!

🔬 Research Room (DG Level 27)

Unlock at DG Level 27 to discover new blueprints and upgrade interactive furniture like Trampolines and Pressure Plates!

🌈 5. Dyeing & Customization

Don't settle for default colors! Heartopia lets you customize furniture to match your vision.

🎨 Furniture Dyeing

  • Requirements:
  • • Workbench (unlocked early)
  • • Dye (100 Gold from Ka Ching)
  • • The furniture item to dye

⚠️ Not all furniture is dyeable — check item preview first!

🖌️ Drawing Board (DG Lv.14)

  • Unlock: Complete Dorothee's quest
  • Buy: 2,000 Gold from Ka Ching
  • • Create custom patterns
  • • Design clothing & furniture

💰 Customization Costs

🪑 For Furniture:

  • • Paint Can: 50 Moonlight Crystals
  • • From General Store or quests

👗 For Clothing:

  • • Tailoring Ticket: 80 Moonlight Crystals
  • • Or from quest rewards
💡 Pro Tip: Dye can also be found by popping blue Prize Bubbles around the map — save your Gold!

✨ 6. Pro Design Tips

Master these principles to create beautiful, cohesive spaces:

🎨

Color Coordination

Choose 2-3 main colors and stick to them. Use accent colors sparingly.

📐

Symmetry & Balance

Balance large items with smaller pieces on the opposite side.

💡

Lighting Layers

Use multiple light sources at different heights for depth.

🖼️

Wall Decoration

Don't leave walls bare! Add paintings, shelves, or decorative items.

🧹

Negative Space

Don't overcrowd! Leave space between furniture for a breathable look.

🪴

Add Life

Plants bring any room to life. Place them on tables, corners, shelves.

📸 Screenshot Before Redecorating!

Before making big changes, take a screenshot of your current layout. This way you can always go back if you prefer the original design!

✅ Do

  • • Enable grid snapping for symmetry
  • • Layer items: rug → table → decor
  • • Create gallery walls with paintings
  • • Visit friends' homes for ideas
  • • Save layout presets (House Lv.2)

❌ Avoid

  • • Mismatched color schemes
  • • Overcrowding rooms
  • • Blocking pathways
  • • Ignoring vertical space
  • • Random furniture placement
