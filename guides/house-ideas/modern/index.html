🎨 Modern Design Principles

Modern design is all about clean geometry, open space, and letting materials speak for themselves. In Heartopia, you can nail this look using the building system's walls, paint, and carefully curated furniture.

📐 Clean Lines

Everything should feel geometric and intentional. Use straight walls, flat roofs, and rectangular furniture. Avoid curves and ornate details.

🪟 Open Concept

Minimize interior walls. Let rooms flow into each other with wide openings. Use furniture placement and floor color changes to define zones instead of walls.

⬛ Less Is More

Every item should serve a purpose — functional or as a deliberate statement piece. One bold artwork beats ten small decorations. Keep surfaces clear.

💡 Statement Lighting

Lighting is decor in modern design. Use bold lamps and fixtures as focal points. Warm ambient lighting creates atmosphere without clutter.

💡 Key Difference from Other Styles: Modern design prioritizes function and form equally. Where cozy or cottage styles layer textures and objects, modern style strips back to essentials and lets architecture do the talking.

📐 Room Layout & Floor Plan

Modern homes use open floor plans where living, dining, and kitchen areas merge into one large space. Use Heartopia's Blueprint mode to experiment freely before committing.

💡 Blueprint Mode: Access via your phone menu. You get unlimited furniture and decor to test layouts. Use multiple slots — one for your modern build, another for experimenting.

🏡 Exterior & Outdoor Design

A modern exterior is just as important as the interior. Focus on clean geometry, a flat roof, and a curated outdoor space.

🏗️ Building Exterior

  • • Flat roof — use God Mode + flooring on walls
  • • Paint exterior walls white or charcoal
  • • Use contrasting trim (black on white)
  • • Large window openings (remove wall sections)
  • • Geometric, boxy shape — avoid curves

🌿 Modern Outdoor Space

  • • Paved patio area (dark floor tiles)
  • • Geometric planters with single plants
  • • Outdoor seating (bench or chairs)
  • • Minimalist fencing or low walls
  • • One statement tree or sculpture

📋 Step-by-Step Build Guide

1

Plan in Blueprint Mode

Open your phone menu and enter Blueprints. Select an empty slot. Sketch a large open rectangle for the main living/kitchen area, then add enclosed rooms for bedroom and bathroom.

2

Build the Boxy Shell

Place walls in clean, straight lines. Keep the shape geometric — rectangles and squares only. Use half-walls or open doorways between the living and kitchen zones. Remove wall sections for large window openings.

3

Create a Flat Roof

Switch to God Mode and place flooring on top of your walls. Paint it charcoal or black. Extend slightly beyond the walls for a modern overhang effect.

4

Apply the Color Palette

Paint exterior walls white or charcoal. Interior walls in white with one accent wall per room. Floors in dark wood or concrete gray. Use the drag-to-expand trick for efficiency.

5

Place Furniture Sparingly

Add only essential pieces per room. One sofa, one table, one lamp in the living area. Keep surfaces clear. Use the "less is more" rule — if it doesn't serve a purpose, remove it.

6

Add Statement Pieces

One large painting or custom artwork per room. One bold plant. One accent-colored item (cushion, rug, or vase) in your chosen accent color. These create visual interest without clutter.

7

Design the Outdoor Area

Pave a patio with dark tiles, add geometric planters and a single statement tree. Keep the garden minimal and structured — no wild, overgrown looks.

8

Import & Submit for Evaluation

Import your Blueprint to the actual lot. Submit for the weekly Home Evaluation — points are awarded for design, decorations, and overall look. Earn exclusive rewards!

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I build a modern house in Heartopia?

Use Blueprint mode to plan an open-concept layout with minimal walls. Apply a monochrome palette (white, gray, charcoal) using the paint system. Choose sleek, geometric furniture and build a flat roof using God Mode + flooring on walls.

Is there a modern furniture set in Heartopia?

No dedicated "modern" furniture set has been confirmed. Achieve the look by selecting sleek, neutral-toned pieces from Bob's daily shop rotation, crafting custom items with the Drawing Board, and using build hacks like painted panels for shelving and islands.

What colors should I use for a modern house?

Base your palette on white, light gray, and charcoal. Pick one bold accent color (deep blue, emerald, mustard, or red) and use it sparingly — one accent wall, one colored cushion, one piece of art. The contrast creates impact.

How do I make a flat roof in Heartopia?

Build your walls first, then switch to God Mode and place flooring tiles on top of the walls. Paint the flooring in charcoal or black. Extend it slightly beyond the walls for a modern overhang effect.

Does the Home Evaluation reward modern builds?

The weekly Home Evaluation scores your house based on design, decorations, and overall appearance. Any well-executed style can score well — a clean, cohesive modern build with matching furniture and thoughtful layout will earn strong points.
