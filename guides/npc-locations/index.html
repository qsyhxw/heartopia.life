✨ Doris — The Secret Traveling Vendor

Doris is a special NPC who only appears during certain weather conditions! She sells exclusive items you can't get anywhere else.

🌧️❄️

During Rain or Snow

📍 Location
Art Street (central town, near archways/bridges)
🛒 Sells
Original Roll Cake Recipe, Colored Sugar, Exclusive Umbrellas, Postcards
🌠

During Meteor Shower (6PM-12AM)

📍 Location
Onsen Mountain (near meteor landing sites)
🛒 Sells
Meteor Furniture (trade Star Fragments), Pray Emote
🌈

During Rainbow

📍 Location
Foot of the rainbow (Onsen Mountain or Forest areas)
🛒 Sells
Roll Cake Recipe, Colored Sugar, Postcards, Rainbow Emote ✨

💡 Pro Tip: Check your in-house TV for weather forecasts! When you see special weather coming, prepare to hunt for Doris and her exclusive items.

🌟 Special NPCs

🌳

Roaming Oak

Daily Wanderer

A chubby tree with a face that changes location daily! Often found near home plots but can appear anywhere in Heartopia.

Tip: Talk to Roaming Oak for daily rewards — you'll need to explore to find it each day!

🌸

Vernie

Flower Fields Resident

A red-haired NPC typically found around the Flower Fields (left side of the map).

😼

Naughty

Quest NPC

Found at Onsen Mountain during Treasure Hunt quests. Use the NPC tracking feature to locate.

🔧

Eric

Construction NPC

Involved in construction tasks at Onsen Mountain Park, especially during seasonal events.

📍 How to Track NPCs

Using the Smartwatch

  1. 1 Open your Smartwatch (tap the watch icon)
  2. 2 Go to the Map
  3. 3 Tap on an NPC's profile
  4. 4 Select "Track" to get direction indicators

💡 Useful for: Finding quest NPCs, locating hobby mentors, and tracking down Roaming Oak!
