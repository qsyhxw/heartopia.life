🌠 Heartopia Meteor Shower Guide

Use the Smartwatch forecast to catch Heartopia meteor showers, pick a clear viewing spot, take night-sky photos, and use the wish emote with friends.

Time Window 6 PM - 12 AM
Timezone Server Time
Forecast Icon 🌙 + ✨
Action Make a Wish

Quick Answer

When to Check

Open the Smartwatch weather forecast and look for the meteor shower icon. The useful window is usually 6 PM - 12 AM server time.

Where to Stand

Choose open sky areas such as Onsen Mountain, Crater Lake, Meadow Lake, bridges, rooftops, or wide roads without tall trees blocking the view.

What to Do

Use the wish emote while meteors are falling. For the friend achievement, gather together first, then trigger the emote during the shower.

Photo Tip

Switch to photo mode after the sky is active. For camera controls and scenic quests, see the photo guide.

1 How to Predict a Meteor Shower

You don't need to guess! Heartopia has a built-in forecast system on your wrist.

  1. Open your in-game Smartwatch.
  2. Tap the small Weather Icon (top right corner).
  3. Look at the forecast for the upcoming week.
  4. Goal: Find the icon showing a Moon surrounded by Stars.
🕒
Important Note on Time: The event happens 6:00 PM - 12:00 AM Server Time. If your server is in a different timezone than you, check the Server Clock in your settings!

2 How to Wish Upon a Star

There is a special achievement called "Beneath the Meteor Shower". Here is how to unlock it:

🙏

Use the Emote

Open your Emote menu and select the "Wish Upon Shooting Star" action. Your character will clasp their hands and look up.

👥

Do it with Friends

The achievement triggers when you wish together with friends while the meteors are falling.

3 Meteor Shower Locations & Photo Spots

Meteor showers are easiest to enjoy in places where the skyline is not blocked. Move away from dense trees, tall walls, and busy interiors before the event starts.

Onsen Mountain / Crater Lake

High terrain gives a wider sky view, which makes it good for meteor shower photos and aurora screenshots.

Meadow Lake / Bridges

Water reflections and open paths help you frame the sky without losing your character in the shot.

Home Rooftops / Wide Roads

Useful when you want a quick wish with friends and do not want to travel across the map.

Scenic Photo Quests

If a quest asks for a scenic night photo, wait until the meteor streaks are visible, then use the camera from photo mode.

4 Meteor Shower, Aurora & Rainbow Blessing

Sky Event Main Use Best Next Step
Meteor Shower Wish emote, friend achievement, night photos. Check forecast, gather friends, stand in an open area.
Aurora Colorful sky effect and photography. Use the aurora guide for timing and photo spots.
Rainbow Blessing Weather-related searches often appear alongside rare sky events. Check your Smartwatch forecast and related event pages before planning a route.

5 Don't Confuse with "Wishing Stars"

🌟

Wishing Stars are also an in-game currency used to buy rare furniture and clothes.

While the Meteor Shower event is fun, you primarily get the currency "Wishing Stars" from:

  • Daily Requests
  • NPC Gifts
  • Events (like Winter Frost)
