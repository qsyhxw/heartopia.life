🌠 Heartopia Meteor Shower Guide

Witness the most beautiful night sky in Heartopia! Learn exactly when to look up and how to make a wish with your friends.

Time Window 6 PM - 12 AM
Timezone Server Time
Forecast Icon 🌙 + ✨
Action Make a Wish

1 How to Predict a Meteor Shower

You don't need to guess! Heartopia has a built-in forecast system on your wrist.

  1. Open your in-game Smartwatch.
  2. Tap the small Weather Icon (top right corner).
  3. Look at the forecast for the upcoming week.
  4. Goal: Find the icon showing a Moon surrounded by Stars.
🕒
Important Note on Time: The event happens 6:00 PM - 12:00 AM Server Time. If your server is in a different timezone than you, check the Server Clock in your settings!

2 How to Wish Upon a Star

There is a special achievement called "Beneath the Meteor Shower". Here is how to unlock it:

🙏

Use the Emote

Open your Emote menu and select the "Wish Upon Shooting Star" action. Your character will clasp their hands and look up.

👥

Do it with Friends

The achievement triggers when you wish together with friends while the meteors are falling.

3 Don't Confuse with "Wishing Stars"

🌟

Wishing Stars are also an in-game currency used to buy rare furniture and clothes.

While the Meteor Shower event is fun, you primarily get the currency "Wishing Stars" from:

  • Daily Requests
  • NPC Gifts
  • Events (like Winter Frost)
