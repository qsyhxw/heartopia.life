Heartopia Cooking Profit Guide: Best Prices & Recipes

Cooking in Heartopia isn't just about restoring energy—it is arguably the most lucrative way to make Gold in the mid-to-late game. Master ingredient processing and start generating massive wealth!

💰 How Cooking Profits Work

The core economic loop of Heartopia rewards players who process raw materials rather than shipping them immediately. The single biggest factor in maximizing profit is understanding the star rating system.

The Ultimate Secret to Wealth:

Using cheap, 1-star raw ingredients to randomly proc a 4-star or 5-star finished dish! Never use premium raw ingredients for cooking unless required; standard ingredients still have a chance to craft a culinary masterpiece.

🏆 Tier List: Best Food to Sell

Depending on your progression level and available materials, certain recipes yield much higher Returns on Investment (ROI) than others.

S-TIER (MID-GAME)

Grass Cake (青草蛋糕)

The Grass Cake is legendary within the community due to its virtually zero-cost main ingredient, creating pure profit loops.

  • 🛒 Ingredients: Wheat, Milk, Matcha Powder, and Weeds.
  • 📈 Why it's S-Tier: Weeds can be gathered completely for free while farming or walking around. This drastically reduces the overhead cost to almost nothing!
1-Star Effect
Restores 50 Energy
5-Star Selling Price
~ 5,520 Gold

* Note: This recipe was popularized during event collaborations and remains a staple for economy-focused players.

S-TIER (LATE-GAME)

Steamed King Crab

Unlocked at Cooking Level 10 from NPC Massimo, this recipe generates the highest raw profit per dish in the game.

  • 🛒 Ingredients: 3x King Crab (Frostspore King Crabs) and Butter.
  • 🧠 The Strategy: Never sell your raw Frostspore King Crabs to the shipping bin! Process them in this recipe to exploit the star multiplier.

Steamed King Crab 1-5 Star Price Breakdown:

⭐ (1-Star) ~ 1,990 Gold
⭐⭐⭐⭐ & ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ +15,000 Gold Net Profit

Hitting a 4 or 5-star proc creates a massive premium, netting over 15k pure profit compared to selling the raw crabs directly.

A-Tier Alternatives (For Beginners)

Don't have access to Crabs or Matcha Powder yet? Always process raw berries into Apple Jam or craft basic Corn Soup before using the shipping bin! Even basic processing yields a 20-30% higher return than raw ingredients.

📈 Tips for Maximizing Yields

  • Appliance Upgrades: Always use kitchen appliances that match or exceed your DG Level. Using basic stoves late-game reduces your chance of getting a 5-star profit proc.
