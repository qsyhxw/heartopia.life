🌺 Highland Poppy Guide

Unlock, breed, and grow the rare Highland Poppy during the limited-time Winter Frost event.

⏳

Limited Time Only!

Highland Poppies are exclusive to the Winter Frost Event. You must unlock them before March 14, 2026.

🔓 How to Unlock Seeds

1

Wait for Week 2

The seeds are NOT available at the start of the event. They unlock during the second week of Winter Frost.

2

Talk to Blanc

Navigate to the event area and find the NPC named Blanc (the white cat). Accept his special gardening quest.

3

Unlock the Shop

Complete the tutorial quest to plant your first poppy. This will permanently unlock Highland Poppy seeds in Blanc's event shop.

🎨 Colors & Crossbreeding

Highland Poppies come in 3 base colors and 2 hybrid colors. Use crossbreeding to obtain the rare 2-star variants.

Type Color How to Get
⭐ 1-Star (Base) Red Buy from Blanc's Shop
⭐ 1-Star (Base) Yellow Buy from Blanc's Shop
⭐ 1-Star (Base) White Buy from Blanc's Shop
⭐⭐ 2-Star (Rare) Orange Crossbreed: Red + Yellow
⭐⭐ 2-Star (Rare) Pink Crossbreed: Red + White

💡 Breeding Tips

💧 Don't Forget Water

Flowers must be watered daily to crossbreed. If you skip a day, the timer resets.

🌈 Use Rainbow Powder

Unlock Gardening Level 6 to get "Rainbow Breeding Powder". Using this guarantees a successful hybrid mutation!

Content Optimization Notes

This event repeats annually, but dates may shift slightly. We will update for Winter 2027 when announced.
