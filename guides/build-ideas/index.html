🛠️ Blueprints & Layouts

Heartopia Build Ideas & House Layout Gallery

Creating your dream estate goes far beyond dropping a pre-made house on a plot. Learn the secrets of custom furniture scaling, unlock the smartwatch Blueprint app, and draw inspiration from our community gallery.

📘

Unlocking the Blueprint App (Level 8)

Before you tear down your starter home, you must reach D.G. (Developer Guild) Level 8. This unlocks the internal Blueprint App within your in-game smartwatch.

✔
Save Your House:

Always save your current iteration to a blueprint slot before doing major renovations.

✔
Co-Building:

Invite friends to your plot and grant them permission to help you build in real-time!

🪑

Custom Furniture scaling

Heartopia allows you to clip and scale objects in ways other farming sims do not. Don't rely solely on what you find inside Bob's Furniture Shop!

  • Custom Tables: Take a standard Column, use the scaling tool to shrink it down, and place a colored Floor Panel on top to construct a custom coffee table.
  • Wall Shelving: Stack floor panels horizontally against a low wall to create built-in library bookshelves.
💡 Pro-tip: Look for floating "Recipe Bubbles" around the map to unlock free rustic crafting furniture recipes!

Community Build Ideas Gallery

Browse some of the most popular layout aesthetics spreading across the Heartopia community to inspire your next plot overhaul.

🌸 Zen Aesthetic

The Japanese Sakura Courtyard

Perfect for those who love serene, traditional aesthetics. Utilizing low wooden walls, bamboo accents, and placing your housing plot near coastal cherry blossom trees to create an isolated, peaceful retreat.

Key Materials Needed:
Wooden Low Walls Shoji Screens Stepping Stones
🍄 Witchy Core

Enchanted Forest Cabin

For players who prefer the witchy, foraging lifestyle. This build heavily utilizes the Hedges (unlocked via D.G. levels) and glowing mushroom decorations around a dark wooden cabin exterior.

Key Materials Needed:
Hedge Fences Dark Timber Walls Mushroom Lamps
🌿 Botanist Vibe

The Sunlit Botanist Greenhouse

A glass-heavy layout utilizing massive window panes instead of solid walls. Combine dozens of indoor potted plants from Bob's shop and custom floor-panel shelving to display rare farmed flowers.

Key Materials Needed:
Large Window Panes Brick Accents Hanging Planters
