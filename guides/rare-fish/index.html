How To Catch Rare Fish Reliably

Check conditions in order

Confirm the exact location, Fishing Level, weather, time window, shadow size, and whether the fish belongs to a special event category.

Do not mix normal sea and event fish

Bluefin Tuna and Swordfish are normal sea-zone fish, while Golden King Crab, Moonfish, and Shortfin Mako Shark are listed under Sea Fishing.

Priority Route

During Rainbow weather, check your highest unlocked targets first: Golden King Crab or Shortfin Mako Shark in Sea Fishing, Swordfish in Whale Sea, Bluefin Tuna in Zephyr Sea, Smooth Hammerhead in Old Sea, and Wels Catfish in Meadow Lake at Dusk/Night.
