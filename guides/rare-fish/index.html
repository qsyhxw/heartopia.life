These rare fish can only be caught using the Mermaid Fish Attractor in multiplayer mode!

🧜‍♀️ How Mermaid Attractor Works

  1. 1. Buy Mermaid Fish Attractor from Vanya (unlocks at Fishing Lv.4)
  2. 2. Play with friends nearby (multiplayer required)
  3. 3. Both players use Attractors simultaneously
  4. 4. Look for blue flashing fish shadow — only the attractor user can catch it

🎣 How to Catch Rare Fish

Basic Steps

  1. 1 Equip your Fishing Rod from your toolbox
  2. 2 Find fish shadows in the water — bigger = rarer fish
  3. 3 Cast in front of the shadow — hold to aim precisely
  4. 4 Reel in when fish bites — hold the reel icon
  5. ! If line turns RED — release immediately to prevent snap!

💡 Rare Fish Signs

  • • Larger shadows = higher chance of rare fish
  • • Shimmering/sparkling shadows may indicate special fish
  • • Blue flashing shadow = Mermaid Attractor exclusive

💡 Pro Tips for Rare Fish

✅ Do

  • • Check weather forecast daily — prepare for rainbow days!
  • • Upgrade Rod Maintenance skill first to prevent rod breaking
  • • Use Mermaid Perfume during rainbow weather for more spawns
  • • Rent a boat for sea fishing — larger shadows spawn in deep water
  • • Fish at night (6PM-6AM) for many rare fish
  • • Play with friends for Mermaid Attractor exclusive fish

❌ Don't

  • • Miss rainbow weather — it only lasts a few hours!
  • • Force the reel when line is red — instant snap
  • • Sell all rare fish — keep one of each for encyclopedia
  • • Ignore fishing level — some fish won't spawn until higher levels
