🏪 Ka-Ching - General Store Guide

Ka-Ching runs the General Store in Heartopia.Life! Find her location, shop inventory, puzzle sizes, and everything you need to know about this essential NPC vendor.

🧩 Puzzles 🎆 Fireworks 🔧 Repair Kits 🔄 Daily Refresh

📍

Location

Suburban Lake

🏪

Store Type

General Store

🔄

Stock Refresh

Daily

📍Where to Find Ka-Ching

Ka-Ching's General Store is located outside her house, near Suburban Lake on the edge of Residential Street.

🗺️ How to Find Her

1. Open your in-game map

2. Look for Ka-Ching's icon near Suburban Lake

3. Use the "Track" feature to navigate directly to her store

⚠️ Event Relocations

During special in-game events (winter festival, concerts, etc.), Ka-Ching may temporarily relocate to event areas. If you can't find her at her usual location, check event zones or restart the game!

🛒What Ka-Ching Sells

Ka-Ching's General Store offers a wide variety of useful items, with stock refreshing daily!

🧩 Puzzles

Primary source for puzzles in Heartopia! Available in 4 sizes:

  • • Small (S) - 16 pieces
  • • Medium (M) - 32 pieces
  • • Large (L) - 64 pieces
  • • Extra Large (XL) - 100 pieces

💰 S/M/L cost Gold, XL may require Wishing Stars

🎆 Fireworks

Colorful Skyrockets and other fireworks

💡 Money Tip: Buy with Wishing Stars, sell to Albert Jr. for ~1,100 Gold each!

🔧 Upgrades & Tools

  • • Repair Kits
  • • Backpack Expansion
  • • Warehouse Expansion
  • • Crafting Materials

🎨 Decorative Items

  • • Dyes & Paint
  • • Photo Frames (various sizes)
  • • Book Writing Desk
  • • Simplistic Phonograph
  • • Furniture

🧩Puzzles & Dreams System

Puzzles from Ka-Ching are essential for the Puzzle Dream progression system!

🎯 Why Buy Puzzles?

Puzzles aren't just decorative - they're integral to the Dreams progression system!

  • ✅ Unlock at DG Level 16 - Dreams feature becomes available
  • ✅ Earn Dream Points - Complete puzzles to gain points
  • ✅ Upgrade Puzzle Dream - Use points to unlock rewards
  • ✅ Solo or Co-op - Complete alone or with friends
  • ✅ Recommended First Dream - Can be completed solo!
Puzzle Size Pieces Difficulty Currency
Small (S) 16 Easy Gold
Medium (M) 32 Medium Gold
Large (L) 64 Hard Gold
Extra Large (XL) 100 Very Hard Gold/Wishing Stars

Ka-Ching's stock refreshes every day! Visit daily to see new puzzles and fireworks.

Buy Colorful Skyrockets with Wishing Stars, sell to Albert Jr. for ~1,100 Gold profit!

❓Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Ka-Ching located in Heartopia.Life?

Ka-Ching's General Store is located outside her house, near Suburban Lake on the edge of Residential Street. Use your in-game map to track her location!

What does Ka-Ching sell?

Ka-Ching sells puzzles (S/M/L/XL), fireworks, repair kits, dyes, paint, photo frames, book writing desk, phonograph, furniture, and various upgrades. Her stock refreshes daily!

How often does Ka-Ching's stock refresh?

Ka-Ching's stock, especially puzzles and fireworks, refreshes daily. Check back every day for new items!

What are puzzle sizes and how many pieces?

Puzzles come in 4 sizes: Small (16 pieces), Medium (32 pieces), Large (64 pieces), and Extra Large (100 pieces). Larger puzzles are more challenging!

Can I make money from Ka-Ching's items?

Yes! Buy Colorful Skyrockets with Wishing Stars and sell them to Albert Jr. for approximately 1,100 Gold each. Note: Wishing Stars are valuable, so use this strategy wisely!
