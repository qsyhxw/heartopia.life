Daily Tasks FAQ

Does this page upload my checklist?

No. It stores checked tasks in your browser only.

When does Heartopia daily reset happen?

Players commonly refer to 6:00 AM server time. Your local time depends on your server region and daylight-saving rules, so confirm with the in-game clock when timing matters.

Will my tasks clear automatically?

The page separates saved checklist progress by server reset day. When a new reset day starts, you get a fresh checklist.
