💡 Friendship Tips

⏰ Best time to gift: Morning (6 AM - 12 PM) when NPCs are most receptive
🎂 Birthday bonus: Gifts on birthdays give 3× normal friendship points
🔄 Rotate gifts: Use 3-4 different favorites to avoid diminishing returns
📅 Daily visits: Talk to NPCs daily even without gifting for small gains
⏳ Timeline: Close Friend takes ~20-30 days, Best Friend ~40-60 days
😊 Watch mood: Avoid gifting when NPC shows negative mood indicators
