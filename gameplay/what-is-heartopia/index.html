What is Heartopia?

The cozy life simulation MMO where you can build, farm, fish, and hang out with friends in a seamless open world.

🎮 Cross-Platform 💸 Free to Play 🤝 Multiplayer

Imagine if Animal Crossing met a massive online world. That's Heartopia. Developed by XD Entertainment, this game focuses on slow living, creativity, and social interaction without the pressure of combat or frantic leveling.

🏡

Build Your Dream Home

Complete creative freedom. Build mansions, cottages, or modern villas. Place furniture precisely where you want it—no grid restrictions like other games.

🎣

Relaxing Hobbies

Master Fishing, Gardening, Cooking, and more. No rush, no stamina limits—play at your own pace.

👯‍♀️

Social Multiplayer

Invite friends to your house parties, play mini-games, or just sit on a bench and chat. It's a true MMO designed for hanging out.

👗

Endless Fashion

Thousands of clothing items. Mix and match styles to create your unique avatar. Cute, cool, or elegant—it’s up to you.

🤔 Frequently Asked Questions

Is Heartopia Pay-to-Win? ⬇️

No. The monetization is focused on Cosmetics (Gacha) and Furniture. There are no "energy bars" you have to pay to refill, and you can enjoy all hobbies and gameplay features for free.

Can I play offline? ⬇️

**No.** Heartopia is an online MMO. You need an internet connection to play, save your progress, and interact with the world.

How is it different from Animal Crossing? ⬇️

While similar in vibes, Heartopia removes the "real-time clock" pressure (shops are open 24/7) and adds true MMO features like seeing other players running around your town in real-time.
