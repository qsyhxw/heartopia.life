📈 Progression System: D.G. Member's Guild

What is D.G.?

The D.G. Member's Guild (Developer's Guild) is Heartopia's main progression system. Your D.G. level represents your contribution to Heartopia Town and unlocks new features, hobbies, and content.

How to Level Up: Earn Contribution Medals

🎯 Primary Method: Daily Resident Requests

  • • Complete up to 5 Resident Requests daily
  • • Each request rewards 10 Contribution Medals
  • • Maximum: 50 medals per day from daily quests
  • • Requests refresh every 24 hours

Additional Methods:

  • • Mini-game Events: Top ranks earn 5-10 medals
  • • Hosting Guests: Invite friends (2 medals per guest)
  • • Town Guide Missions: 3-5 medals each
  • • Weekly Quests: Bonus medals

D.G. Level Unlocks

D.G. Level Unlocks
Level 3 Gardening Hobby
Level 4 Friendship Journal, Home Life features
Level 5 Cooking, Bug Catching, Birdwatching Hobbies
Level 12 Cat Care, Dog Care Hobbies
Level 16 Dreams feature (Puzzle, Writing, Music)

*Higher levels also unlock additional home plots, advanced shop items, and new game features.

⚡ Energy & Stamina System

How Energy Works

  • • Maximum Energy: 100 points (base capacity)
  • • Energy Consumption: Chopping trees, fishing, digging, bug catching
  • • No Energy Cost: Walking, building, socializing, decorating

Energy Recovery Methods

💤 Sleeping (Fastest Passive)

Rate: +1 energy every 5 seconds

Best Practice: Log out while sleeping = full energy on return

🍽️ Eating Food (Instant)

Effect: Instant energy refill

Best Foods: Cooked dishes > raw ingredients

🛁 Bathing (Passive)

Rate: +1 energy every 10 seconds

Locations: Home bathtub or Onsen Mountain pools

🎁 Gift Bubbles & Buffs

Green Arrow Bubbles: Energy refill buffs

Friend High-Five: Energy restore buff (once per day)

⚠️ Low Energy Warning

Below 20 Energy: Cannot run (slow walk only). Solution: Eat food, sleep, or use a vehicle.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is Heartopia multiplayer or single-player?

Heartopia supports multiplayer features including visiting friends' homes, co-building, and social interactions. However, the core experience is primarily single-player focused on your personal island and progression.

What platforms can I play Heartopia on?

Heartopia is available on PC (TapTap client and Steam), iOS, and Android. The game supports cross-platform play, allowing you to access your account across devices.

Is Heartopia free to play?

Yes, Heartopia is completely free-to-play with optional in-app purchases for cosmetic items and premium currency (Moonlight Crystals).

How many hobbies are in Heartopia?

There are 7 main hobbies: Fishing, Gardening, Cooking, Insect Catching, Bird Watching, Cat Care, and Dog Care.

What is the fastest way to level up D.G.?

Complete 5 Daily Resident Requests every day for 50 Contribution Medals. Also participate in mini-game events and weekly quests for bonus medals.

What's the best hobby for making money?

Cooking combined with Gardening and Fishing provides the best profit. Processed goods (cooked dishes) sell for significantly more than raw materials.

Can I play Heartopia offline?

Most features work offline, but you'll need an internet connection for multiplayer features, daily quest updates, and accessing the shop.

How do I get more Hobby Expansion Tickets?

Hobby Expansion Tickets are earned through tutorial quests and by increasing your D.G. Member Level. They are limited, so choose hobbies strategically.
