🔒 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 23, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Heartopia.Life ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe online experience. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website heartopia.life.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

2.1 Automatically Collected Information

  • IP address and approximate location
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type and operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent on pages
  • Referring website or source

2.2 Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience, analyze site traffic, and understand where our visitors come from. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain our website
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve content and user experience
  • Monitor and prevent technical issues
  • Display relevant advertisements

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our website. Google Analytics collects information such as how often users visit the site, what pages they visit, and what other sites they used prior to coming to our site. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4.2 Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our website. Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4.3 Other Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites and encourage you to read their privacy policies.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing of your data
  • Opt out of cookies and tracking

7. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

📧 Email: contact@heartopia.life

10. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) provides you with specific rights regarding your personal information.

10.1 Information We Collect and Share

We collect information that identifies, relates to, or could reasonably be linked with you, such as identifiers (e.g., IP address, cookies) and internet network activity. We share this information with third-party advertising partners (such as NitroPay and its demand partners) to serve personalized ads on our website. Under the CCPA, this sharing of information for targeted advertising may be considered a "sale" of personal information.

10.2 Right to Opt-Out of Sale

You have the right to direct us not to sell your personal information. You can exercise this right at any time by clicking the "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" link located in the footer of our website.

10.3 Right to Know and Delete

You have the right to request that we disclose certain information to you about our collection and use of your personal information over the past 12 months. You also have the right to request the deletion of your personal information, subject to certain exceptions.

To exercise your rights to know or delete, please contact us or use the privacy preference links provided in the footer of this website. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your CCPA rights.

11. European Territory Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you are accessing this website from the European Union (EU) or European Economic Area (EEA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies to our processing of your personal data.

11.1 Consent Management Platform (CMP)

We use an IAB-registered Consent Management Platform (CMP) provided by NitroPay to manage your data processing preferences. When you first visit our site from a GDPR-regulated region, you will be presented with a consent modal. This tool allows you to grant or deny permission for various data processing purposes, such as personalized advertising, content measurement, and audience research.

11.2 Legal Basis for Processing

We and our advertising partners (up to 1,100+ IAB-authorized vendors) process your personal data based on your explicit consent or legitimate interest, as defined within the CMP. This data may include your IP address, device identifiers, and browsing activity.

11.3 Managing Your Preferences

You have the right to change your mind at any time. You can resurface the consent settings and modify your choices by clicking the "Update Privacy Preferences" link located in the footer of every page on this website.

11.4 Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, or erase your personal data, as well as the right to data portability and the restriction of processing. For any inquiries regarding your data, please contact us through our provided contact channels.
