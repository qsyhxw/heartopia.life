🔓 How to Unlock the Winter Frost Season

1

Wait for Atara's Call

After January 24, log in and you'll receive a phone call from Atara.

2

Head to Town Hall

Atara asks you to find important documents left on a desk at Town Hall.

3

Follow the quest chain to investigate the Frostspore outbreak and help build the Ice Rink.

  • 🔬 Investigate the Frostspore outbreak
  • 🐾 Wildlife check-in with Vanya

🎭 Fashionwave Event & Rewards

The Fashionwave event is the main reward system for the Winter Frost Season, launching January 31. Earn two types of currency to unlock exclusive items.

🌰 Fashionwave Points (Acorns)

  • ☃️ Access via snowman icon (top-right)
  • 🎁 Unlock tiered pass rewards

🪙 Fashionwave Tokens

  • 🐟 Earned by selling Frostspore fish & seasonal food
  • 📋 Earned via seasonal challenges
  • 🛍️ Spend at the Fashionwave shop

🐟 Frostspore Fish

Frostspore Fish are limited-time infected fish that only appear during the Winter Frost Season. They earn Fashionwave Points and can be sold for Fashionwave Tokens.

📍 How to Unlock Frostspore Fishing

1. Find Vanya at the northern beach of Onsen Mountain (she relocates during the event)

2. Complete her short introductory quest about the Winter Frost Season

3. Frostspore Fish will start spawning in the Old Sea

⚠️ Important: All Frostspore Fish spawn exclusively at Old Sea, off the coast of Onsen Mountain. They do NOT appear in rivers, lakes, or other sea areas.

🦋 Frostspore Insects

Infected insects also appear during the event, earning you additional Fashionwave Points.

  • 🦋 Frostspore Butterflies — found at Forest Lake area, any weather
  • 💬 Talk to Naniwa and choose "Mysterious Frostspore Butterflies" to start
  • 🌰 Each catch earns Fashionwave Points toward your reward pass

🥚 Onsen Egg Hunt (16 Days)

A 16-day daily treasure hunt on Onsen Mountain that rewards exclusive winter-themed furniture and rare titles.

📋 Requirements

  • ⭐ DG Level 7 or higher
  • ✅ Completed main story tutorial quests
  • 💬 Speak to the NPC on Onsen Mountain to trigger the event

🔍 How It Works

①

Each day, a new Cracked Egg spawns somewhere on Onsen Mountain

②

Find the egg and take a photo of it

③

Find the nearby Furniture Bubble (snowman-shaped) and pop it

④

Collect the physical egg decoration and furniture reward

💡 Tip: Don't miss a day! The eggs and furniture bubbles are in different locations each day. Complete all 16 days for the grand prize and exclusive titles.

🧊 Frostbitten Skin (Blue Skin)

One of the most sought-after cosmetics of the Winter Frost Season — a pale blue skin unlocked through a 6-day ritual involving the Frostspore Infection.

📋 How to Unlock

①

Trigger the Frostspore Infection buff by standing in cold weather (look for the snowflake icon)

②

Keep the buff active for at least 1 minute

③

Repeat for 6 consecutive days

④

The Frostbitten Skin unlocks automatically after Day 6

💡 Tip: It doesn't matter how long you stand in the cold — just make sure you trigger the buff each day. Missing a day resets your streak!

⛸️ Ice Skating (New Permanent Hobby)

Ice Skating is a brand-new hobby introduced during the Winter Frost Season. Unlike seasonal hobbies, Ice Skating becomes a permanent hobby once unlocked.

🔓 How to Unlock

  • 1. Accept Atara's call (Jan 24+)
  • 2. Visit Onsen Mountain Park
  • 3. Speak with Eric (Ice Skating NPC)
  • 4. Complete daily rink construction tasks
  • 5. Ice Rink opens when construction finishes

⛸️ Details

  • 📅 Available from January 31, 2026
  • 🏔️ Located at Onsen Mountain Ice Rink
  • ♾️ Permanent hobby (stays after event)
  • 👫 Skate with friends!

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

When does the Heartopia Winter Frost Season start and end? ▼

The Winter Frost Season runs from January 24, 2026 at 5:00 AM to March 14, 2026 at 5:00 AM, with daily resets around 6:00 AM server time. The first week (Jan 24–31) is the Prep Phase, and the full Fashionwave event begins January 31.

How do I unlock the Frostbitten Skin (blue skin)? ▼

Trigger the Frostspore Infection buff for 6 consecutive days, keeping the buff active for at least 1 minute each day. The buff is triggered by standing in cold weather during the Winter Frost Season. Missing a day resets your streak.

Where do I catch Frostspore Fish? ▼

All Frostspore Fish are caught exclusively at Old Sea, off the coast of Onsen Mountain. Complete Vanya's introductory quest first — she relocates to the northern beach of Onsen Mountain during the event.

How does the Onsen Egg Hunt work? ▼

The Onsen Egg Hunt is a 16-day daily treasure hunt starting January 31. Each day, find one egg on Onsen Mountain, take a photo of it, then find a nearby furniture bubble. Requires DG Level 7 and completed main story tutorial.

What time is the Snow Concert? ▼

The Snow Concert runs daily at 7:00 PM server time during the first and last weeks of the season. During middle weeks, it shifts to weekends only. Enter the venue between 7:00–7:20 PM to participate.
