🐦 Nest of Hundreds Event Guide

Photograph rare birds and earn star points! Master the photography techniques to maximize your rewards.

📋 Event Overview

🔓 Unlock

DG Level 8

📍 Location

Fishing Village

Near Fountain

💰 Cost

2,000 Gold

(first/week)

🎯 Hobby

Birdwatching

⭐ Star Points System

This event measures your progress in star points, not just number of photos. Higher-quality shots = more stars!

👤

Personal Goal

30 Star Points

👥

Group Goal

150 Star Points

🎁 Rewards: Complete goals to earn Surprise Gacha Pods! Info Cards can also be given to Bailey J. or sold for gold.

📸 Photography Techniques

🎯 Basic Steps

  1. 1. Equip scanner from toolbox
  2. 2. Use binoculars to find birds
  3. 3. Lock onto bird with reticle
  4. 4. Snap the picture!

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • • Getting too close scares birds away
  • • Out-of-focus = "Invalid Card"
  • • Waiting too long = bird flies away

⭐ How to Get Higher Star Ratings

⭐

1 Star

Idle bird, standing still

⭐⭐

2 Stars

Active pose (chirping)

⭐⭐⭐

3+ Stars

Preening, flapping wings

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

5 Stars

Perfect + in flight!

💡 Tip: Listen for birdsong and watch for animations — chirping, preening, and wing-flapping give bonus stars!

✨ Perfect Photographs

📷

At Birdwatching Level 4, you unlock the "Perfect Photographs" feature!

  • • Bird must be precisely centered in frame
  • • Properly zoomed in
  • • Automatically adds +1 star to photo quality!

🎮 Event Strategies

🎯 Quality Strategy

Wait for perfect moments!

  • ✓ Watch for active animations
  • ✓ Listen for birdsong
  • ✓ Center birds for Perfect shots
  • ✓ Fewer photos, more stars each

📸 Quantity Strategy

Spam photos for faster completion!

  • ✓ Take 3 quick shots per bird
  • ✓ Even 1-star shots count
  • ✓ Good when others scare birds
  • ✓ Faster goal completion
💡 Key Insight: You can photograph the same bird multiple times! No need to find new birds for each shot.
