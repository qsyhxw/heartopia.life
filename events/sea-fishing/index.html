🚀 How to Start the Event

1
Reach DG Level 7

Complete quests and earn Contribution Medals to reach Developer's Guild Level 7.

2
Go to Fishing Village

Travel to the Fishing Village and find Bill near the boats.

3
Talk to Bill

Initiate the event by speaking with Bill. Choose the correct boat (boat 1 or 2)!

4
Board & Sail

Once the countdown ends, the boat sails into deep waters with exclusive fish!

💰 Event Costs

  • • First time/week: 2,000 Gold
  • • Additional: Price increases
  • • Join others: FREE!

🎁 Free Town Events

Mentor NPCs host free "Town Events" at specific times. Anyone who has unlocked the event can join for free!

🎣 Fishing Mechanics

🎯 How to Fish

  1. 1. Equip fishing rod from toolbox
  2. 2. Look for fish silhouettes in water
  3. 3. Hold cast button for precise aim
  4. 4. Cast close to fish shadow
  5. 5. Wait for bite, then reel in!

📏 Line Tension Guide

  • White — Safe to reel continuously
  • Yellow — Caution, brief pause needed
  • Red — STOP reeling or line snaps!
✨ Glittering Sea: When the sea glitters during the event, cast your line — you won't need to reel in fish during this special phase!

✨ Golden Fish Shadows

🦈 Catch Sharks!

Golden fish shadows indicate rare fish, including sharks! They appear as large, shiny golden silhouettes.

  • • Nurse Hound Shark available at Fishing Level 6+
  • • Sharks can ONLY be caught during Sea Fishing Events
  • • Reel them in immediately when hooked!

🐟 Shoals (Fish Schools)

Shoals are special phases where many fish gather together — great for catching lots of fish quickly!

🎯 How to Trigger Shoals

  • • Triggered through normal fishing
  • • More players = Higher chance!
  • • Group events trigger shoals more often

🏆 Shoal Achievements

  • • Shoal Caller: Trigger 3 shoals in single event
  • • Shoal's Blessing: Trigger 100 shoals total
