Heartopia Fashionwave & Memory Journal Guide

Finish tasks in a useful order, earn and spend Fashionwave Tokens, complete the Memory Journal, and clear each deadline.

Task orderTokensMemory JournalDeadlines

Quick answer

Finish the limited route before permanent Ocean Cleanup

Claim the Fashionwave Catalog gift and quest rewards, sell accepted items to Azure for tokens, then complete the camera quests that award the Memory Journal. Buy wanted store items by August 22 at 5:59 AM, finish journal editing by August 29 at 5:59 AM, and use Time-Limited Exhibition Passes by August 31 at 5:59 AM. All times use server time.

Recommended route

Complete Fashionwave in This Order

This is a Heartopia.Life workflow, not a required quest chain. It puts expiring rewards first.

  1. 1. Unlock the season

    Reach D.G. Lv.7, finish “The Answer Lies Within,” and enter Whalefall Canyon.

  2. 2. Claim the free gift

    Open the Catalog and claim its Thank-You Gift before spending.

  3. 3. Clear limited quests

    Complete event tasks, then press Claim where required.

  4. 4. Build Collection Points

    Collect listed discoveries and claim milestones and passes.

  5. 5. Budget tokens

    Check Azure’s procurement and reserve tokens for wanted rewards.

  6. 6. Finish the Journal

    Complete photo prompts, claim the book, edit, save, and share only when asked.

Currency route

How to Earn Fashionwave Tokens

Quest rewards

Return to the event panel and claim completed tasks.

Catalog milestones

Collection Point levels can award tokens.

Azure procurement

Compare the live offer before selling fish, food or materials.

Daily targets

Higher-value targets can be random and differ by player.

Sell with a reserve

  • Keep one-off quest items.
  • Keep ingredients for your next recipe.
  • Compare the live token value.
  • Do not copy another player’s procurement list.

Store decision guide

What to Buy First

1. Must-have reward

Reserve tokens for limited cosmetics, vehicles or actions you want.

2. Progress item

Buy an item that opens your current activity.

3. Collection filler

Use spare tokens after checking weekly additions.

4. Gold fallback

Unused tokens convert to Gold at 1:1 by mailbox.

Avoid blind refreshes. Check weekly additions and your token reserve first.

Photo quest

Complete the Memory Journal

The journal comes from camera quests. Opening it is not editing it: enter Edit Mode, add content, save, and recheck the task panel.

  1. 1. Track the quest

    Activate the requested photo subject.

  2. 2. Take the photo

    Keep the subject in frame until it registers.

  3. 3. Claim the journal

    Collect the reward and find the book in your bag.

  4. 4. Enter Edit Mode

    Use the edit control, not view mode.

  5. 5. Add and save

    Add content, save, then reopen.

  6. 6. Share when asked

    Share pages only when a task requests it.

Troubleshooting

Store or Journal Not Working

Store is missing

Use the Catalog shortcut or speak to Azure.

Tokens did not increase

Claim completed tasks or confirm Azure’s offer.

Procurement differs

Use the random offer in your own Store.

Journal is missing

Claim its quest, then inspect your bag.

Edits do not register

Edit, save, reopen, then check the task.

Pass disappeared

Limited passes expire on August 31.
