What “little whale” means

Players use little whale, small whale, and fountain whale for the same Splash Whale photo collection. They are decorative whale figures hidden around Whalefall Canyon, not fish that you catch with a rod.

Appearance conditions

Reach D.G. Member Level 7, enter Whalefall Canyon, and complete Naughty's prerequisite whale quest (shown in some regions as “The Big Whale and the Little Whale”). Then activate the Splash Whale objective. The collection unlocks in sequence using server time; older unlocked whales may still be available for catch-up.

Call of Whales Concert

How to join the Concert

Concert time is tied to the live activity schedule for your server. Check the event panel instead of converting a time posted by a player in another region.

  1. Step 1

    Open the whale event

    Tap the whale icon on the main screen, then open the Fashion Activity panel.

  2. Step 2

    Read your server time

    Select the Concert task and note the next time and destination shown in game.

  3. Step 3

    Track the task

    Keep the Concert objective selected so the current destination marker is easier to follow.

  4. Step 4

    Arrive before it starts

    Enter the marked area a few minutes early and remain there until the performance begins.

Concert not starting?

Check these first

  • Wrong clock: use the server time printed in the event panel, not your local midnight.
  • Wrong destination: the live objective marker is more reliable than a first-week screenshot.
  • Task not selected: reopen Fashion Activity and track the Concert objective.
  • Panel looks stale: change zones or relog, then check the next listed session again.
Quick answer: there is no single worldwide Concert time. Your event panel is the final schedule for your server.

Troubleshooting

If Call of Whales does not appear

The event panel is locked or missing

Confirm your D.G. Member level and main story progress first. Then close and reopen the event panel after the server has refreshed.

The activity is unavailable at your current time

Compare your local clock with server time. The listed end time is 5:59 AM server time, not midnight in every region.

Ocean Cleanup targets or markers look broken

Install the latest game update and reload Whalefall Canyon. The July 15 flash update addressed invisible pollution mist, untargetable Swirling Pollutants, and an incorrect underwater marker icon.

Call of Whales FAQ

When does Call of Whales end?

The Fashionwave season ends on August 22, 2026 at 5:59 AM, using server time.

What are the entry requirements?

Reach D.G. Member Level 7 and complete the main-story requirement shown in the live event panel.

How many Small Whales are there?

There are 16 Small Fountain Whales in the complete sequence. 10 locations were available through July 20, 2026; later whales unlock on following days.

How do I join the Concert?

Open the whale event icon, choose Fashion Activity, track the Concert task, and follow the time and destination shown for your server.

Is Ocean Cleanup time-limited?

No. Ocean Cleanup was introduced as a permanent hobby, while Fashionwave and its timed rewards use the event countdown.

Why did I not receive whale furniture?

First complete Naughty's prerequisite whale quest. Taking the photo then completes only the first step; find and open the separate reward bubble before leaving the route.
