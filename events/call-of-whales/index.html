Event window

July 11 - August 22

Starts July 11 at 6:00 AM and ends August 22 at 5:59 AM, using server time.

Entry requirement

D.G. Member Level 7

Complete the main-story requirement shown in your live event panel before entering the event area.

Main focus

Sea and Fashionwave

Daily season collection

Find the 16 Small Fountain Whales

Quick route: Accept the related task at the Whalefall Canyon camp. One Small Fountain Whale becomes available each day, for 16 total. Photograph the whale, then search the canyon for the reward bubble that appears.
  1. 1. Accept the task

    Talk to the camp NPC and make sure the Small Whale objective is active.

  2. 2. Take the photo

    Move close enough for the target name to appear in Photo Mode.

  3. 3. Collect the bubble

    After the photo, locate the new reward bubble inside Whalefall Canyon.

Earlier whales can remain available, so a missed day does not necessarily block the collection. Check the camp and canyon again before assuming the task expired.

Troubleshooting

If Call of Whales does not appear

The event panel is locked or missing

Confirm your D.G. Member level and main story progress first. Then close and reopen the event panel after the server has refreshed.

The activity is unavailable at your current time

Compare your local clock with server time. The listed end time is 5:59 AM server time, not midnight in every region.

Ocean Cleanup targets or markers look broken

Install the latest game update and reload Whalefall Canyon. The July 15 flash update addressed invisible pollution mist, untargetable Swirling Pollutants, and an incorrect underwater marker icon.

Call of Whales FAQ

When does Call of Whales end?

The Fashionwave season ends on August 22, 2026 at 5:59 AM, using server time.

What are the entry requirements?

Reach D.G. Member Level 7 and complete the main-story requirement shown in the live event panel.

How many Small Whales are there?

There are 16 Small Fountain Whales. Photograph each target, then collect the bubble that appears in the canyon.

Is Ocean Cleanup time-limited?

No. Ocean Cleanup was introduced as a permanent hobby, while Fashionwave and its timed rewards use the event countdown.
