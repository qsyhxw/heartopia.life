Heartopia Bubble Locations: Shell, Gold & Pink

Fixed event route

All 10 Seashell Bubble Locations

Nine points are currently documented as obtainable. Clear the Whalefall furniture route, then finish the five Pearl material packs around Whale Island.

Recorded point 10 · currently blocked

Whalefall Wall / Seashell Tableware Point

A tenth bubble is recorded around Whalefall, near the passage toward Jellyfish Cave, but current reports show a wall preventing collection. Do not spend time trying to clip through it; recheck after an event progression or patch.

One-time fashion route

Four Golden Clothing Bubbles

All four are in Whalefall Canyon. They disappear after collection, so an empty spot can mean that piece is already owned.

Weekly rotating route

Pink Bubble Reset and Search Plan

Pink bubbles are not fixed Call of Whales pickups. Their locations and reward contents rotate each Saturday at 6:00 AM server time. The current recorded rotation runs from July 18 at 6:00 AM through July 25 at 5:59 AM.

Before following a weekly map

  • Confirm the map dates match your server's current rotation.
  • Expect 15 weekly pink bubbles rather than the 10 shell pickups above.
  • Pink rewards can vary; an older reward list is not a guaranteed result.
  • Start a fresh route after Saturday reset even if last week's checklist was complete.

Fast sweep order

  1. 1. Town and Residential Area
    Check plazas, building edges, stairs, and high ledges.
  2. 2. Fishing Village and Whale Mountain
    Scan fountain, coast, umbrella, and cliff routes.
  3. 3. Forest and Flower Field
    Check wooden bridges, river bends, trees, and windmill paths.
  4. 4. Onsen Mountain
    Rotate the camera around ruins, statues, signs, and elevated paths.

Troubleshooting

Bubble missing at the listed spot?

Check whether it was collected

Fixed gold and shell bubbles disappear after pickup. Check recent furniture, clothing, and materials.

Move around the landmark

Several bubbles float vertically or circle rocks. Look above and behind the named object.

Confirm event access

Reach D.G. Member Level 7 and finish the live event prerequisite before searching Whalefall Canyon.

Reload the area

Change zones or relog if an uncollected fixed bubble fails to render.
