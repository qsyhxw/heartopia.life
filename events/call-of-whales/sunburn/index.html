Heartopia Sunburn Skin & Blush Guide

Wait for the sunglasses-sun weather, use the Sand Bath beside the Whalefall Canyon portal, and confirm each valid session before counting it toward the blush or skin tone.

Fast unlock route

Complete One Valid Sunburn Session

1

Check the hourly weather

Look for the sun wearing sunglasses. The English label is reported as Scorching Sun or Scorching Heat depending on the client wording.

2

Go to the canyon portal

Search the nearby beach sand for the Sand Bath interaction. Use the Whalefall Canyon entrance as the stable landmark.

3

Stay for about 60 seconds

Do not leave early. A short timer or temporary state may appear while the interaction is running.

4

Confirm, then stop

Look for the green umbrella-style Sunburned status. Record one valid session and return on another qualifying day.

Evidence strength

What Is Confirmed, and What Is Still Community-Tested?

Consistent across current reports

Special sunglasses-sun weather is required

Players who received credit consistently used the special Scorching Sun / Scorching Heat icon. Ordinary Sunny weather is repeatedly reported not to count.

Consistent across current reports

A valid Sand Bath lasts about one minute

Successful reports wait for the interaction timer and the Sunburned status rather than entering and leaving immediately.

Current reward results

3 sessions unlock blush; 6 unlock skin

The third valid session unlocks Sunburn Blush and the sixth unlocks the Sunburn Skin Tone. The acquisition pop-up may fail to appear, so check a mirror.

Community-tested, not safe to hard-code

Weekdays, consecutive days and repeat attempts

Do not rely on a fixed Monday/Wednesday schedule, and do not assume the days must be consecutive. Current evidence supports one confirmed session per qualifying day; repeating the action on the same day is not a reliable shortcut.

Rewards and permanence

Where the Blush and Skin Tone Appear

After 3 valid sessions

Sunburn Blush

Open a mirror and check face makeup or face-paint options. Some players receive no acquisition screen even though the blush is already unlocked.

After 6 valid sessions

Sunburn Skin Tone

Open the mirror's skin-tone controls after the sixth confirmed session. Current players describe the pink/red tone as a wardrobe customization unlock.

Is it permanent?

Best current answer: the unlocked customization appears permanent, while the method of obtaining it is seasonal. Player results show the blush and skin tone remain selectable in the mirror after the weather ends. Call of Whales is currently scheduled to end on August 22, 2026 at 5:59 AM server time. Complete the unlock before the acquisition window closes; the live event panel is the final authority for your server.

Troubleshooting

Progress Did Not Count?

  1. 1. Recheck the icon.
    It must be the sunglasses-sun weather, not a plain sun.
  2. 2. Wait for the full interaction.
    Leaving before roughly 60 seconds can prevent the status.
  3. 3. Confirm the Sand Bath prompt.
    Sitting or using an emote on nearby sand is not the event interaction.
  4. 4. Check a mirror.
    The reward pop-up can be missed or absent even when the item unlocked.
  5. 5. Do not force extra same-day attempts.
    Wait for another qualifying weather period and record only a confirmed result.

Quick answers

Heartopia Sunburn FAQ

Where is the sunburn location?

On the beach sand beside the Whalefall Canyon portal. Search for the Sand Bath interaction rather than only matching a screenshot angle.

What weather do I need?

The special sun-with-sunglasses icon, called Scorching Sun or Scorching Heat in current reports. Plain Sunny is not enough.

Do the six days need to be consecutive?

No reliable current evidence proves a consecutive-day requirement. Count completed qualifying sessions and avoid guessing from the calendar alone.

Why did I not get the blush pop-up?

Open a mirror after the third confirmed session. Players report the blush can unlock without an acquisition screen.
