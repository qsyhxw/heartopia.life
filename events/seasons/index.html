🌈 Weather-Based Events

Special events triggered by in-game weather! Check your Watch for weather forecasts.

🌈 Rainbow Event

  • ⏰ Starts at 6:00 PM server time
  • ⏳ Lasts a few real-time hours
  • 🛒 Doris appears at rainbow base
  • 🐟 Rare fish spawns (Swordfish, Bluefin Tuna)
  • 🌾 Bonus harvest for gardening

🌠 Meteor Shower

  • ⭐ Collect Starfall Shards
  • 🛒 Doris appears with meteor items
  • 🪑 Exclusive meteor-based furniture
  • 🏆 Achievement: Stardust Collector (60 shards)

🌧️ Rainy Weather

  • 🦋 Special insects spawn
  • 🐦 Rare bird appearances
  • 🛒 Doris may appear
  • 🌾 Good for certain crops

🌨️ Snowfall

  • ❄️ Snow Sculpting hobby available
  • 🛒 Doris appears with winter items
  • ☃️ Winter seasonal content
