Sea Fishing Event

DG Level 7

Deep sea fishing expedition with exclusive rare fish!

📍 How to Start

  • • Talk to Bill near the boats
  • • Location: Fishing Village
  • • Requires Fishing hobby unlocked

⏱️ Gameplay

  • • Players split into two boats
  • • Travel to deep waters
  • • Limited time fishing session

🎯 Goals & Rewards

Personal Goal: Catch 5 fish
Group Goal: Catch 30 fish total

🎁 Reward: Surprise Gacha Pods

💡 Tip: Use Mermaid Perfume or Fish Attractor baits (buy from Bill) to increase your chances of catching rare fish during the event!

Bait the Insects Event

DG Level 8

Catch as many insects as possible in a timed challenge!

📍 How to Start

  • • Find the insect baiting device
  • • Location: Onsen Mountain (near lake)
  • • Requires Insect Catching hobby

⏱️ Gameplay

  • • Phase 1: 3 min at Onsen Mountain
  • • Travel: 1 min to Crater Lake
  • • Phase 2: 3 min at Crater Lake

🎯 Goals

Personal Goal: Catch 10 insects
Group Goal: Catch 50 insects total

⚠️ Warning: This event consumes a lot of stamina! Eat stamina-boosting food beforehand. Naniwa sometimes hosts free Town Events for bug catching.

Nest of Hundreds Event

DG Level 8

Photograph rare birds in a timed bird-watching challenge!

📍 How to Start

  • • Find the bird-calling device
  • • Location: Fishing Village Fountain
  • • Requires Birdwatching hobby

⏱️ Gameplay

  • • Rare birds appear during event
  • • Use binoculars to find birds
  • • Photograph before they fly away

🎯 Goals (Star Points)

Personal Goal: 30 star points
Group Goal: 150 star points total

💡 Tips:

  • • You can photograph the same bird multiple times
  • • Focus on quantity over quality — spam pictures to reach goal faster!
