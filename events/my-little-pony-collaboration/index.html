🌳 Tree of Harmony Quest

Restore the magic to Equestria by helping the Tree of Harmony on Cloud Island (also known as Whale Island).

1

Visit Cloud Island

Travel to Cloud Island to find the wilting Tree of Harmony.

2

Daily Sensing

Use the "Sensing" action daily to collect random Elements of Harmony (Honesty, Generosity, Laughter, Kindness, Loyalty, Magic).

3

Water the Tree

Use collected elements to water the tree. This fills the Energy Progress Bar and unlocks Exhibition Passes!

Heartopia MLP Collab FAQ

When was the Heartopia My Little Pony collab?

The Heartopia x My Little Pony collaboration ran from February 14, 2026 to March 15, 2026. This page keeps the event rewards, car terms, badges, and Cloud Island quest notes together for players checking past MLP content.

How do I get the Harmony Car?

The Harmony Car was tied to collecting the themed vehicles from character pools. If the event returns, check the in-game collection screen for the exact remaining vehicle requirements before spending event currency.

Are MLP badges regular Heartopia badges?

MLP badge searches usually point to collaboration rewards or profile cosmetics. For normal achievement badge rules, use the Heartopia badges guide and the hidden achievements guide.

Where was the Tree of Harmony quest?

The Tree of Harmony quest was on Cloud Island, also called Whale Island by some players. The daily loop used Sensing actions and Elements of Harmony to fill the event progress bar.
