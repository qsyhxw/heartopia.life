Finding the Secret Pond

⚠️ Urban Legend Warning

Extensive community testing and database mining confirms that the "Secret Pond" does not exist in Heartopia. This guide explains the origin of the rumor and where you should actually be fishing.

If you have been wandering the edges of the map relying on third-party guides telling you "how to find the Secret Pond" to catch the ultimate fish, you aren't alone. This has become one of the most widely searched navigational mysteries in Heartopia. Unfortunately, it is entirely based on a massive community misunderstanding.

Location Map & Entry Requirements

Countless players ask for map pins or entry requirements (like holding a specific tool or waiting until midnight) to access this hidden biome.

  • The Truth of the Map: There is no hidden instance or secret portal behind any waterfall. The map of Heartopia is fully revealed once you unlock all the standard fast-travel towers. There are no "invisible" map markers waiting to be discovered.
  • The Origin of the Rumor: Machine-translated guides from early beta testing likely hallucinated the term "Secret Pond" when actually referring to secluded sections of existing rivers, or players intentionally spread fake playground rumors to troll newer fishers.

Exclusive Fish (Legendary Koi)

The main reason players search out this pond is to capture the so-called "Legendary Koi" at midnight. However, just like the pond, this fish's name is highly inaccurate.

  • The Real Target: What players are actually trying to catch is the Butterfly Koi.
  • Real Catching Requirements: The Butterfly Koi is very real, but it is not locked behind a hidden location. It is caught natively in Meadow Lake.
  • Weather Over Location: Instead of searching for a secret pond, you need to wait for specific weather. The Butterfly Koi exclusively spawns during Rainy or Rainbow weather states. That is the actual "secret"!

Surrounding Resources

If you're looking for high-density resource hubs, stop wasting time clipping against map boundaries looking for the Secret Pond. Instead, focus your daily gathering routes on these actual locations:

  • Meadow Lake: As mentioned, this is the prime hub for rare catches including the Wels Catfish and the Butterfly Koi.
  • Forest (Deer Tower): Your best bet for rare bugs and high-tier wood logging spots.
  • The Coast: For all your saltwater fishing compendium needs.

Fact Check Verified: We strongly advise using the official in-game compendium lists to track your missing fish. If a fish is not listed as a silhouette in your journal, it does not exist!
