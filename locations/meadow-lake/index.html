📍Location & Access

🗺️ How to Find Meadow Lake

Meadow Lake is located near Windmill Flower Fields, one of the scenic areas in Heartopia.Life.

Look for the bus station nearby for convenient fast travel access!

🚌 Transportation

Bus station nearby makes Meadow Lake easily accessible from other areas of the map.

⭐ Butterfly Koi - Star Attraction

The Butterfly Koi is Meadow Lake's most sought-after catch!

  • Level Required: Fishing Level 4-5
  • Weather: Rain or Rainbow (also Snow)
  • Time: All day during correct weather
  • Tip: Will NOT appear on sunny days!

💡Tips for Visiting

🌧️ Check the Weather

For Butterfly Koi, you MUST have rain, rainbow, or snow weather. Check the in-game TV for forecasts!

📊 Level Up First

Reach Fishing Level 4-5 before attempting to catch Butterfly Koi.

🍞 Bring Bait

Use Fish Bait or Premium Bait to reduce wait times and increase rare fish chances.

🚌 Use Fast Travel

Take advantage of the nearby bus station for quick access from town.

❓Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Meadow Lake located?

Meadow Lake is located near Windmill Flower Fields and has a convenient bus station nearby for easy access.

What fish can I catch at Meadow Lake?

You can catch Butterfly Koi (Level 4-5, rain/rainbow weather), Horse Mackerel, Trout, Goldfish, Wels Catfish, and European Smelt at Meadow Lake.

How do I catch Butterfly Koi?

Reach Fishing Level 4-5, visit Meadow Lake during rain, rainbow, or snow weather, and fish all day. Butterfly Koi will NOT appear on sunny days.

What else can I do at Meadow Lake?

Besides fishing, you can find Snowman Bubbles during Winter Frost season (swing set near Fox Trough) and complete a Jump Puzzle near the bridge.

Is Meadow Lake good for beginners?

Yes! Meadow Lake has fish for various levels. While Butterfly Koi requires Level 4-5, other species are accessible to beginners. The convenient bus station also makes it easy to reach.
