🏘️ Main Areas & Districts

Heartopia features a seamless open world without loading screens, designed as a cozy fairy-tale town with diverse areas to explore.

🏛️

Central Town

The main social hub of Heartopia, featuring restaurants, furniture stores, clothing stores, and daily activities. Most players gather here for community interactions.

Key Streets: Residential Street, Garden Street, Art Street

🏡

Home Sweet Home

Your personal living space that grows from a small cottage to a mansion. Central location for home building, gardening, decorating, and crafting.

Expansion: Unlock additional plots at D.G. Levels 4, 6, 8, 11, 14, 16

🎣

Fishing Village

Coastal area dedicated to fishing activities, featuring fishing spots, market stalls, and a wharf.

Features: Wharf Market Stall, coastal fishing spots

🌲

Amazing Forest

Dense forest region for exploration, featuring sub-areas like Spirit-Oak Pine Forest and Jump Puzzle Area. Home to rare insects and wildlife.

Sub-areas: Spirit-Oak Pine Forest, Jump Puzzle Area, Forest Lake Bridge

🌸

Flower Field

Beautiful meadow filled with flowers and windmills. Popular spot for photography and wildlife watching.

Wildlife: Foxes, various birds

⛰️

Onsen Mountains

Warm spring-season area with hot springs, mountain lakes, and beautiful scenery. Perfect for fishing, bird photography, and relaxation.

Features: Onsen Mountain Lake (fishing), hot springs, fluorite resources

Winter Event: Ice skating rink, ice/snow slides

📍

Other Notable Locations

  • • Purple Light Beach - Coastal area connected to lighthouse
  • • Lighthouse Area - Scenic viewpoint with bridge access
  • • Giantwood River Bridge - Major river crossing
  • • Rosy River Bridge - Scenic bridge near windmill
  • • Suburbs - Residential area where Albert Jr. roams

🏪 Shops & Stores

Most shops are located in Central Town on Residential Street and Garden Street. Shop inventories refresh daily.

Location: Residential Street, Town Square

Sells: Furniture, building recipes

Inventory changes daily

👗 Dorothee's Clothing Store

Location: Residential Street, Town Square

Sells: Clothing, accessories, customization options

Upstairs: Hair, eyebrows, eyes, beards, face paint

🍳 Massimo's Cooking Store

👥 Important NPC Locations

💰

Albert Jr. - Gold Merchant

Location: Roams around the Suburbs (not fixed)

Function: Sell items for gold (food, furniture, crops, fish)

💡 Tip: Track Albert Jr. using his icon on the in-game map

🌈

Doris - Traveling Merchant

Locations (weather-dependent):

  • • Rain/Snow: Art Street in main town
  • • Rainbow: Base of rainbow (often Onsen Mountain or forest)
  • • Meteor Shower: Near meteor landing sites (Onsen Mountain or beaches)

⚠️ Doris only appears during special weather conditions

📋

Mayor Atara - Daily Quests

Location: Central Town

Function: Provides daily resident requests (5 per day, 50 Contribution Medals)

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What are the main areas in Heartopia?

The main areas include Central Town (social hub), Home Sweet Home (your personal space), Fishing Village, Amazing Forest, Flower Field, and Onsen Mountains. The game features a seamless open world without loading screens.

Where is Albert Jr. located?

Albert Jr., the Gold Merchant, roams around the Suburbs area. You can track him using his icon on the in-game map to find him and sell items for gold.

Where can I find Doris the traveling merchant?

Doris appears during special weather: on Art Street during rain/snow, at the base of rainbows (often in Onsen Mountain or forest), and near meteor landing sites during meteor showers.

What shops are in Central Town?

Central Town features Bob's Furniture Shop, Dorothee's Clothing Store, Massimo's Cooking Store, Ka-Ching's General Store, and more on Residential Street and Garden Street. Most shops refresh their inventory daily.

Where is the best fishing spot?

Onsen Mountain Lake is a popular fishing spot offering various species like Common Whitefish, Ruffe, Tadpole, Mottled Sculpin, and Bluegill at different times and weather conditions.

How do I unlock new areas?

Most areas are accessible from the start. However, Home Plots unlock at specific D.G. Member Levels (4, 6, 8, 11, 14, 16). Some event-specific locations appear during seasonal events.

Are there loading screens between areas?

No! Heartopia features a seamless open world without loading screens, allowing for continuous exploration across the entire map.
