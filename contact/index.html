❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is Heartopia.Life an official site?

No, we are an unofficial fan site. We are not affiliated with XD Entertainment.

Can you fix my game, account, purchase, or missing item?

No. Please use the official support routes at the top of this page. Heartopia.Life cannot view accounts, issue refunds, restore items, or fix the game.

Can I contribute to the guides?

We welcome corrections and suggestions! Please email us with any information you'd like to share.

How often is the content updated?

We update our guides and databases regularly, especially after game patches and updates.
