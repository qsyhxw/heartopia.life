❓ What Is the Inflatable Insect Attractor?

The Inflatable Insect Attractor is a special consumable item in Heartopia that lures rare insects to your location. When placed, it attracts bugs that don't normally spawn in the wild — including the elusive Sulkowsky's Morpho butterfly!

✅ What It Does

  • • Spawns rare/exclusive insects
  • • Attracts Sulkowsky's Morpho
  • • Works in specific locations
  • • Essential for collection completion

⚠️ Important Notes

  • • Single-use — disappears after use
  • • Costs 2,000 gold each
  • • Requires Insect Catching Level 4
  • • Buy from Naniwa only

🛒 How to Get the Inflatable Insect Attractor

Step-by-Step Guide

1
Reach DG Level 6
Complete Resident Requests to earn Contribution Medals
2
Unlock Insect Catching Hobby
Use Hobby Expansion Ticket with Naniwa (near the river garden)
3
Level Up to Insect Catching Level 4
Catch insects to gain experience
4
Buy from Naniwa's Shop
Price: 2,000 Gold or 1 Wishing Star

🎯 How to Use the Inflatable Insect Attractor

  1. 1
    Go to a Good Location
    Best spots: Onsen Mountain, Fishing Village, or areas with existing insect spawn points
  2. 2
    Open Your Inventory
    Find the Inflatable Insect Attractor in your items
  3. 3
    Place the Attractor
    Use the item to deploy it in your location
  4. 4
    Wait for Rare Insects to Spawn
    Sulkowsky's Morpho and other rare bugs will appear!
  5. 5
    Catch with Bubble Net
    Approach carefully and capture the insects!

💡 Pro Tip: Place the attractor in areas that already have insect spawn points for potentially better results! The Flower Field and Forest areas are great choices.

💡 Tips & Tricks

🤫

Stealth Approach

Move slowly toward insects to avoid startling them. They'll flee if you rush!

🎯

Charge Your Bubble

Hold the action button to charge and send a precise bubble from a distance. More accurate!

📍

Best Placement

Place the attractor in areas with existing insect spawn points for better results.

🌈

Rainbow Weather

Many rare insects only spawn during rainbow weather. Check your forecast!
