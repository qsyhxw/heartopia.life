🏡 What is Heartopia?

Heartopia is a multiplayer cozy life simulation game developed by XD Entertainment. Released on January 7, 2026 for PC, iOS, and Android (Steam release on January 16, 2026), it offers a relaxing experience similar to Animal Crossing and Stardew Valley.

Build your dream home, engage in hobbies like fishing, cooking, gardening, and birdwatching, adopt pets, and form connections with other players. Heartopia emphasizes creativity, freedom, and enjoying life's simple moments.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is Heartopia? ▼
Heartopia is a multiplayer cozy life simulation game developed by XD Entertainment. It features home building, hobbies like fishing and cooking, gardening, pet adoption, and social interactions. It's available on PC, iOS, and Android.
How do I redeem codes in Heartopia? ▼
Open your in-game Watch (press TAB on PC or tap the smartphone icon on mobile), go to Settings, click "Redeem Code" at the bottom right, enter your code, and press Confirm. Rewards will be sent to your Mailbox.
Is Heartopia free to play? ▼
Yes, Heartopia is free to play on all platforms (PC, iOS, Android, Steam). It uses a free-to-play model with optional in-game purchases and gacha mechanics for cosmetic items.
What platforms is Heartopia available on? ▼
Heartopia is available on PC (via official website and Steam), iOS (App Store), and Android (Google Play). It launched on January 7, 2026, with Steam release on January 16-17, 2026.
How do I make money fast in Heartopia? ▼
The best way to make money in Heartopia is through cooking. Process raw ingredients into dishes like jam — for example, 4 blueberries (64 gold) can become blueberry jam (170 gold). Farming crops and fishing are also profitable.
What hobbies are available in Heartopia? ▼
Heartopia features 7 hobbies: Fishing, Cooking, Gardening, Birdwatching, Insect Catching, Cat Caring, and Dog Caring. Fishing and Gardening are unlocked in the tutorial; others require DG level progression and Hobby Expansion Tickets.
What is DG Level in Heartopia? ▼
DG (Developer Grade) Level is your main progression system in Heartopia. Increasing your DG level unlocks new features, hobbies, furniture, and story content. Complete daily, weekly, and main quests to earn Contribution Medals for leveling up.
Is Heartopia similar to Animal Crossing? ▼
Yes, Heartopia shares many similarities with Animal Crossing and Stardew Valley. It features cozy life simulation gameplay including home building, gardening, fishing, cooking, and social interactions with characters.
How do I unlock cooking in Heartopia? ▼
To unlock cooking, you need to increase your DG level, obtain a Hobby Expansion Ticket, and give it to the appropriate NPC. Cooking is recommended as your third hobby after Fishing and Gardening due to its excellent profit potential.
