🏡 What is Heartopia?

Heartopia is a multiplayer cozy life simulation game developed by XD Entertainment. The mobile launch was January 7, 2026, and the Steam release was January 16, 2026. Our platforms FAQ tracks PC, Steam, iOS, Android, Switch, PS5, Xbox, and Mac status in one place.

Build your home, try hobbies like fishing, cooking, gardening, and birdwatching, adopt pets, decorate outfits and rooms, and play socially with friends. For a deeper overview, read What is Heartopia?, then use the database hub when you need fish, insects, birds, cats, recipes, items, or material pages.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is Heartopia? ▼
Heartopia is a multiplayer cozy life simulation game developed by XD Entertainment. It features home building, fishing, cooking, gardening, pets, character customization, and social play. Heartopia.Life helps you find codes, platform notes, guides, and database pages quickly.
How do I redeem codes in Heartopia? ▼
Open your in-game Watch (press TAB on PC or tap the smartphone icon on mobile), go to Settings, click "Redeem Code" at the bottom right, enter your code, and press Confirm. Rewards will be sent to your Mailbox.
Is Heartopia free to play? ▼
Yes, Heartopia is free to play on supported PC and mobile platforms, with optional in-game purchases for cosmetic and account items.
What platforms is Heartopia available on? ▼
Heartopia is playable on Windows PC, Steam, iOS, and Android. As of July 7, 2026, Nintendo Switch, PS5, Xbox, and native Mac versions are not officially listed; see the Platforms FAQ for details.
How do I make money fast in Heartopia? ▼
The best way to make money in Heartopia is through cooking. Process raw ingredients into dishes like jam — for example, 4 blueberries (64 gold) can become blueberry jam (170 gold). Farming crops and fishing are also profitable.
What hobbies are available in Heartopia? ▼
Heartopia features 7 hobbies: Fishing, Cooking, Gardening, Birdwatching, Insect Catching, Cat Caring, and Dog Caring. Fishing and Gardening are unlocked in the tutorial; others require DG level progression and Hobby Expansion Tickets.
What is DG Level in Heartopia? ▼
DG (Developer Grade) Level is your main progression system in Heartopia. Increasing your DG level unlocks new features, hobbies, furniture, and story content. Do daily, weekly, and main quests to earn Contribution Medals for leveling up.
Is Heartopia similar to Animal Crossing? ▼
Yes, Heartopia shares many similarities with Animal Crossing and Stardew Valley. It features cozy life simulation gameplay including home building, gardening, fishing, cooking, and social interactions with characters.
