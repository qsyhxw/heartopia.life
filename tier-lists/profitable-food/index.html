Heartopia Best Food to Sell Tier List (2026)

Cooking and selling food to Albert Jr. is widely considered the best way to earn massive income in Heartopia. However, not all meals are created equal. Discover the highest profit margins from early-game Grilled Mushrooms to the endgame 12,640G Strawberry Jam.

🍳 How Cooking Profit Works

The profitability of a dish is not just about the recipe itself; it is determined by three crucial multipliers:

🌟 Star Quality

The biggest multiplier. A 5-star dish often sells for substantially more than its 1-star counterpart. Your goal is always 4 or 5 stars.

🍅 Ingredient Tier

Using silver or gold-star farmed produce significantly increases the odds of cooking a higher-star meal.

👩‍🍳 Cooking Level

A higher Cooking Hobby level raises your baseline quality, decreasing failures and boosting 5-star procs.

🏆 The Most Profitable Cooking Recipes

We have categorized the recipes based on their maximum 5-star selling price and the effort/time required to gather their ingredients.

S

Endgame Millionaires

Highest profit margins. Requires high-level farming or dedicated foraging routes.

Strawberry Jam

The undisputed king of profit. Since strawberries can be grown in massive batches on your home plot, ingredient setup costs are kept to the absolute minimum.

5-Star Sell Price 12,640 G
Effort High (Farming)

Black Truffle Cream Pasta

Extremely profitable, but Black Truffles cannot be farmed. They must be manually foraged on Forest Island (nodes respawn ~15 mins).

5-Star Sell Price 7,200 G
Effort High (Foraging)

Grass Cake

An insane ROI because its main ingredient (Weeds) is basically free. Note: Exclusive to players who unlocked it during the Pleasant Goat event.

5-Star Sell Price ~5,500 G
Effort Medium

Great for established farms before unlocking endgame crops.

Grape Jam

Highly profitable, but requires 4 Grapes per jar. Grape vines take considerable time to grow, making mass production slower than strawberries.

Estimate Value High Return

Corn Soup

An excellent transitionary food. Corn is easy to scale up significantly in the mid-game.

Estimate Value Solid Profit

Beginner & Easy Income

"Lazy" profit with zero crop management required.

Grilled Mushrooms

The ultimate beginner food. A Level 1 recipe using Mushrooms scattered everywhere in the starting zones.

Onsen Egg

Pasteurised Eggs can be bought directly from Massimo for 100G with no farming required, making it the easiest trick to batch-cook for effortless daily profit.

🚀 The "Speedrun" Progression Strategy

If your goal is to buy all the expensive house blueprints and cosmetics, follow this path:

  1. 1
    Days 1-7: The Forager

    Forage mushrooms relentlessly in the wild and sell Grilled Mushrooms.

  2. 2
    Days 7-20: The Bathhouse Cook

    Unlock Massimo's egg supply and mass-cook Onsen Eggs at the bathhouse.

  3. 3
    Day 20+: The Truffle & Jam Empire

    Transition your farm to growing berries. Exclusively craft Strawberry Jam while foraging for Black Truffles in your downtime.
