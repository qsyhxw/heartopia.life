Heartopia Butterfly Koi Location

Rare

Quick answer: catch Butterfly Koi at Meadow Lake during Rain or Rainbow weather. It appears all day and requires Fishing Level 5.

Location Meadow Lake
Time All Day
Weather 🌧️ Rain / Rainbow
Level Req Fishing Lv. 5
Price Not Listed Sell value varies by star rating.
💡

Fast check: If the weather is Sunny, skip this fish and return on Rain or Rainbow weather. Use attractors only after the right weather is active.

📍 Exact Location

Head to Meadow Lake. The best spot is the wooden dock near the Windmill and the Bus Station. Fish there during Rain or Rainbow weather; Butterfly Koi is available all day once you meet the Fishing Level 5 requirement.
