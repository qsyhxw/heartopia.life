💡 Fishing Tips

✅ Maximize Profits

  • • Check weather daily — 🌈 Rainbow = rare fish!
  • • Farm 5★ fish at Level 6+ for max Gold
  • • Night fishing for European Lobster is profitable
  • • Sea Fishing Event (DG7+) for bonus catches
  • • Use bait to reduce waiting time

⚠️ Fishing Mechanics

  • • Watch line tension — release when flashing red!
  • • Upgrade your rod for better catches
  • • Some fish require Mermaid Attractor (Tilapia, Edible Frog)
  • • Level 10 unlocks Pearl of Tides rod (+20% luck)
