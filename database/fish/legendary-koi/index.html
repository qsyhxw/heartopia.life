The Legendary Koi represents the ultimate achievement for anglers in Heartopia.Life. This is a "special occasion catch" rather than a consistent farming source due to its exclusive spawn conditions.

🎣How to Catch

1️⃣ Unlock Fishing Hobby

Complete Vanya's quest (cat rescue) and obtain a Hobby Expansion Ticket at Heritage Association Level 2.

2️⃣ Find Secret Pond

Discover the Secret Pond location. This exclusive spot is where the Legendary Koi spawns.

3️⃣ Fish at Midnight

Visit the Secret Pond during midnight hours. The Legendary Koi only appears at this specific time.

4️⃣ Use Proper Technique

Cast at visible fish shadows, manage line tension carefully (White=Safe, Yellow=Slow, Red=STOP), and reel in your prize!
