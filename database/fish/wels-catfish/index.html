The Wels Catfish is one of the most mechanically demanding aquatic catches in Heartopia. Successfully baiting and reeling in this massive fish is considered a badge of honor for anyone pursuing their Fishing Hobby. Because it requires highly specific catching conditions, many players struggle to find it. This database entry covers exactly what you need to prepare.

Top Fishing Spots & Conditions

You cannot simply cast your line into the ocean or local rivers. The Wels Catfish is highly localized and only emerges during specific environmental states:

  • Location: The only known habitat is Meadow Lake (sometimes referred to as Grassland Lake).
  • Time of Day: Plan your fishing trips for the Evening and Night (typically 6:00 PM – 6:00 AM server time). They absolutely will not bite during morning or noon hours.
  • Weather: It is strictly active during Sunny or Rainbow weather. Do not waste your bait trying to fish for it while it is raining.

Bait & Tension Mechanics

Meeting the environmental conditions is only half the battle. Here is what you need regarding your gear and execution:

  • Level Requirement: You must attain a minimum of Fishing Hobby Level 10. If your rank is lower, the Wels Catfish will never spawn in the minigame instance.
  • Using Bait: Because of its rarity, it is highly recommended to cast premium bait to draw them towards your hook. Without bait to filter the spawns, you may waste hours dealing with common trash fish.
  • Advanced Reeling: The Catfish is exceptionally strong. Watch the tension line closely—when it turns from yellow to blinking red, you must give the line slack instantly or it will snap. Let it run for a moment before reeling again.

Rarity and Economy Info

The monumental effort to land a Wels Catfish pays out significantly when it's time to cash out:

  • Raw Value: The current market value ladder is 610 / 915 / 1,220 / 2,440 / 4,880 depending on star rating.
  • Recipes & Bundles: Most players hunt the Wels Catfish to complete high-tier collection challenges or cook five-star culinary recipes. As a central ingredient, the resultant meals often provide potent buffs or fetch astronomical prices.
