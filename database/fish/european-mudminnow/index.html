The European Mudminnow is a highly sought-after rare fish in Heartopia. Successfully catching one is a milestone for players leveling their Fishing Hobby, but it requires specific preparation. This guide covers its exact spawning location, the fishing level needed, and the bait you should bring.

Best Location & Weather Requirements

Unlike common river fish, the European Mudminnow favors still, murky waters. You can exclusively fish for this species in specific bodies of water on the Heartopia Map.

  • Primary Location: Head to the Suburban Lake. Fish off the deeper wooden docks to increase your bite rate for rare fishes.
  • Weather & Time: The Mudminnow is generally not restricted by daytime, but some community members report a slightly higher spawn rate during rainy weather or overcast afternoons.

Required Bait & Rod

You cannot catch the European Mudminnow right out of the gate as a beginner. It has a hard level requirement gating its capture:

  • Level Requirement: You must reach Fishing Hobby Level 8 before the European Mudminnow will begin appearing in your fishing minigame. Ensure you are consistently catching easier fish or completing daily fishing requests to grind your level.
  • Bait: Standard earthworms might attract them eventually, but using higher-tier baits (such as Crafted Lures or Premium Bug Bait) will drastically filter out common fish and speed up your catching process. Check our Fishing Spots & Bait Guide for a full breakdown.

Sell Price & Associated Recipes

Once caught, you have a few options for your prized European Mudminnow. You can sell it directly for a comfortable profit, or integrate it into advanced culinary dishes.

  • Sell Value: The raw fish holds a significant standalone value to NPC merchants compared to standard catches. Keep an eye on market days for a markup.
  • Cooking: Utilizing it in the kitchen typically yields much higher returns. Check out our Recipe Prices & Profit Guide to see how it compares to other premium seafood dishes when processed.
  • Collection: Don't forget to donate your first catch to complete your in-game collector's log or aquarium display in your home!
