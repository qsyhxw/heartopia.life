Heartopia Sardine Location

Sardine is an early sea fish in Heartopia. The current fish table lists it at Ocean fishing spots, with Fishing Level 1 and sell prices from 50G to 400G.

Quick Answer

Go to an Ocean fishing spot and fish there first. Sardine is listed as a Level 1 sea fish, so it is one of the earliest ocean catches to check when a recipe, request, or collection entry asks for sardines.

Best first step: make sure you are at sea water, not a lake or river. If you keep catching River or Lake fish, move to an Ocean spot.

If Sardine Is Not Appearing

  1. Check the water type: Sardine is listed under sea fish, so start at Ocean water.
  2. Do a short route reset: leave the spot, return, and cast at fresh fish shadows.
  3. Compare nearby sea fish: Sea Bass and Horse Mackerel can appear in the same early sea category, so keep a few casts in the same area.
  4. Check your quest text: if a request says Whale Sea or another named area, follow that request wording instead of the generic Ocean row.

FAQ

Where is Sardine in Heartopia?

The current fish table lists Sardine at Ocean fishing spots.

Is Sardine a river fish?

No. Sardine is listed in the sea fish section, so use Ocean spots rather than river spots.

What is the best related fish to check next?

If you are already fishing sea spots, check Seahorse at Whale Sea next, then move to higher-level rare fish when your fishing level increases.
