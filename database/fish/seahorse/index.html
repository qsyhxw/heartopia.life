Heartopia Seahorse Location

Seahorse is a Level 2 sea fish in Heartopia. The current fish table lists it at Whale Sea, with sell prices of 100G, 150G, 200G, 400G, and 800G.

Quick Answer

Go to Whale Sea and fish sea spots there. Seahorse is listed as Fishing Level 2, so check it early when you are collecting Whale Sea entries or filling sea fish requests.

Best first step: use a Whale Sea spot first. Generic Ocean spots are better for Sardine, while Seahorse is tied to Whale Sea in the current fish table.

If Seahorse Is Not Appearing

  1. Check the named area: Seahorse is listed at Whale Sea, not generic Ocean.
  2. Fish several shadows: Horse Mackerel and other early sea fish may appear first in the same route.
  3. Avoid the wrong water type: lake and river spots have different fish pools.
  4. Follow quest wording: if a request points to a specific dock or sea zone, use that location first.

FAQ

Where is Seahorse in Heartopia?

The current fish table lists Seahorse at Whale Sea.

Is Seahorse an Ocean fish?

It is a sea fish, but the listed location is Whale Sea. Use Whale Sea before trying generic Ocean routes.

What should I fish next after Seahorse?

For early sea collection, check Sardine at Ocean. For a weather-gated lake target, check Butterfly Koi at Meadow Lake during Rain or Rainbow weather.
