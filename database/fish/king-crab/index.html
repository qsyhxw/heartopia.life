Where to Find King Crab

This fish is listed in Whale Sea. Start by reaching the correct sea area before checking its weather and time window.

  1. 1. Reach Whale Sea. Use the location shown in the fish database rather than a different sea area.
  2. 2. Check Fishing Level 8. The fish will not be available until this listed requirement is met.
  3. 3. Match the schedule. Wait for Rainbow weather and fish during Day, Dawn, Dusk.
  4. 4. Watch for a Large shadow. This helps separate the target from other catches at the same location.
