Tilapia Location Quick Answer

For tilapia Heartopia searches: Tilapia is listed for any river, including river routes shown from River Bank fishing guidance. It requires Mermaid Attractor, so normal river fishing may not show it even when your level is high enough.

Best first step: reach Fishing Level 3, choose a river spot, activate Mermaid Attractor, then fish while the blue special shadows are active.

If Tilapia Is Not Appearing

  1. Check the water type: Tilapia is a river target. Lake Mermaid Attractor fish are different.
  2. Check your level: reach Fishing Level 3 before expecting it to appear.
  3. Use Mermaid Attractor: normal river fishing can keep showing regular river fish.
  4. Watch the active window: fish while the attractor effect and special shadows are still present.

FAQ

Where to find Tilapia in Heartopia?

Find Tilapia in any river after using Mermaid Attractor. It is listed as a Fishing Level 3 river fish.

Does Tilapia need special weather?

No. The current tracker data lists Tilapia as any weather and any time.

Is Tilapia a lake fish or river fish?

Tilapia is a river fish. Mermaid Attractor has different fish by water type: river gives Tilapia, lake gives Edible Frog, and ocean gives Rabbit Fish.
