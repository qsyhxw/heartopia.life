🦈 Smooth Hammerhead - Heartopia.Life

The Smooth Hammerhead is one of the rarest and most sought-after fish in Heartopia.Life. This legendary 5-star fish can only be caught in the Old Sea during Rainbow weather conditions.

📋 Catch Conditions

📍 Location

Old Sea

One of several ocean fishing zones in Heartopia.Life. Make sure you're in the correct sea area!

🎣 Fishing Level

Level 10 Required

You must reach Fishing Level 10 before the Smooth Hammerhead will appear.

🌈 Weather Condition

Rainbow Weather ONLY

Rainbow weather is the rarest condition and considered a "jackpot" for rare fish spawns.

🕐 Time of Day

12 AM - 6 AM

6 PM - 12 AM

Nighttime hours only. Does not appear during daytime.

⚠️ Extremely Rare!

The combination of Rainbow weather + nighttime + Level 10 requirement makes the Smooth Hammerhead one of the hardest fish to catch in the game. Be patient and persistent!

🎣 How to Catch Smooth Hammerhead

🔓 Step 1: Unlock Fishing Hobby

  1. 1. Find Vanya, the Fishing Mentor
  2. 2. Complete her quest (cat rescue)
  3. 3. Unlock the Fishing Hobby
  4. 4. Level up to Fishing Level 10 by catching fish regularly

🌊 Step 2: Get to Old Sea

Navigate to the Old Sea fishing location. This is one of several ocean zones in the game.

💡 Tip: Check your map to find the Old Sea marker.

🌈 Step 3: Wait for Rainbow Weather

Rainbow weather is extremely rare. You'll need to check the in-game weather regularly.

💡 Tip: Some players recommend checking during in-game events or special occasions when rare weather is more likely.

🎣 Step 4: Fish During Nighttime

1️⃣ Cast Your Line

Aim for visible fish shadows in the water. Holding the cast button allows precise aiming and reduces wait time.

2️⃣ Wait for a Bite

Use Fish Bait (purchased from Vanya) to reduce waiting time. Mermaid Perfume can also accelerate fish spawns.

3️⃣ Reel In Carefully

Watch the line tension! White = Safe, Yellow = Slow Down, Red = STOP. Breaking the line loses the fish!

4️⃣ Land Your Catch

Successfully manage the tension mechanic to land the Smooth Hammerhead. Higher Fish Quality skill increases star rating chances!

💡 Pro Fishing Tips

📊

Upgrade Fish Quality

Invest skill points in "Fish Quality" to increase your chances of catching higher-star fish, which sell for significantly more gold.

🔧

Maintain Your Rod

Upgrade "Rod Maintenance" to improve success rates when reeling in difficult fish like the Hammerhead.

🎯

Aim for Shadows

Always cast directly at visible fish shadows. This dramatically reduces wait time and increases your catch rate.

🧪

Use Mermaid Perfume

This special item accelerates fish spawns, giving you more chances to encounter rare species during limited Rainbow weather windows.

🍞

Stock Up on Bait

Fish Bait from Vanya's shop reduces wait times between bites. Essential for maximizing rare weather opportunities!

⏰

Be Patient

Rainbow weather is rare. Set up notifications or check regularly. When it appears, focus all your fishing time on the Old Sea!

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I catch the Smooth Hammerhead in Heartopia.Life?

The Smooth Hammerhead can be caught in the Old Sea location. You need Fishing Level 10 and must fish during Rainbow weather between 12 AM-6 AM or 6 PM-12 AM.

What level do I need to catch Smooth Hammerhead?

You need to reach Fishing Level 10 to catch the Smooth Hammerhead. It is one of the highest-level fish in the game.

When does the Smooth Hammerhead appear?

The Smooth Hammerhead only appears during Rainbow weather, which is the rarest weather condition. It can be caught during nighttime hours: 12 AM to 6 AM and 6 PM to 12 AM.

How much does the Smooth Hammerhead sell for?

The exact sell price varies by star quality. As a 5-star rarity fish, it's expected to be one of the highest-value catches, potentially comparable to other rare fish like Swordfish (600G) or higher.

What's the best strategy for catching this fish?

Reach Fishing Level 10, upgrade your Fish Quality and Rod Maintenance skills, stock up on Fish Bait and Mermaid Perfume, then wait for Rainbow weather. When it appears, spend all your time fishing in the Old Sea during nighttime hours (12 AM-6 AM or 6 PM-12 AM).
