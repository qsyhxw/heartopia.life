❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most profitable recipe?

Strawberry Jam at 5★ sells for 12,640 gold - the highest in the game. For beginners, Grilled Mushrooms (1,440g at 5★) is best due to free foraged ingredients.

How does star quality affect prices?

Star quality dramatically multiplies the base price: 2★ = 1.5x, 3★ = 2x, 4★ = 4x, 5★ = 8x the 1-star price.

Where do I sell cooked dishes?

Sell to Albert Jr., the merchant NPC in Town Square.

How do I unlock cooking?

Reach DG Level 5, get a Hobby Expansion Ticket, and visit Massimo in the town center to unlock Cooking.
