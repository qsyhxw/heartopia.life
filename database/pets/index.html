❤️ Care & Training

🐱 Cat Care

  • ✅ Feed — Boosts affection (favorite food = more!)
  • ✅ Pet — Increases affection over time
  • ✅ Play — Chase toys for high affection
  • ✅ Bathe — Keeps clean and happy

🐕 Dog Care + Training

  • ✅ Feed — Boosts affection
  • ✅ Pet — Increases affection
  • ✅ Play — Fetch for high affection
  • ✅ Walk — Dogs need walks!
  • ✅ Train — Teach tricks and commands

🎓 Dog Training Commands

Seek Treasure Shake Head Give Paw + More at higher levels
💡 Tips:
  • • Affection levels are permanent — they won't decrease!
  • • Discover favorite foods for bigger affection boosts
  • • Level up Dog Caring hobby to unlock more tricks
  • • Auto-Feeders (higher levels) keep pets fed automatically

🍽️ Pet Favorite Foods Discovery

🔍 Important Discovery System: Unlike wild animals with fixed favorite foods, each cat and dog has unique, randomized food preferences that you must discover through experimentation! Feeding their favorites provides the biggest affection boosts.

🐱 Cat Food Preferences

  • • Each cat has individual favorite foods
  • • Test different cooked dishes and ingredients
  • • Watch for positive reactions and affection gains
  • • "Sensitive" trait cats are extra picky
  • • Favorites influence "Cat Moments" generation

🐕 Dog Food Preferences

  • • Each dog has unique food preferences
  • • Experiment with various food types
  • • Dog food and snacks from inventory work
  • • Higher affection = better training results
  • • Keep hunger bar filled for happy dogs

🧪 How to Discover Favorite Foods

Step 1: Prepare

Cook various dishes or gather ingredients from hobbies

Step 2: Test

Feed different foods and observe affection increases

Step 3: Track

Note which foods give the biggest boosts

💡 Pro Tips for Food Discovery:
  • • Start with simple, easy-to-cook items before testing expensive dishes
  • • Keep a notebook (in-game or real) to track which foods each pet prefers
  • • Cooked dishes generally provide better affection than raw ingredients
  • • Some pets may prefer sweet foods, others savory - experiment widely!
  • • Higher cooking level unlocks more food options to test
  • • Daily feeding with favorites = faster affection progression
