📋Corn Overview

Gardening Level 6

🌾 Growing Details

12-Hour Growth Time

Can be grown in any season. Perfect as an overnight crop!

💡 Quick Tip

Corn's 12-hour growth time makes it ideal for planting before bed. Harvest when you wake up for efficient farming!

🌱How to Grow Corn

1️⃣ Unlock Gardening Hobby

Complete the tutorial and reach Developer's Guild Level 2 to unlock the Gardening hobby.

2️⃣ Reach Level 6

Level up your Gardening hobby to Level 6 by planting and harvesting other crops.

3️⃣ Buy Corn Seeds

Visit Blanc at the Gardening Store (use map to track). Purchase Corn seeds for 170G each.

4️⃣ Plant & Water

Plant seeds in your garden plots. Water regularly to trigger bountiful harvests!

5️⃣ Harvest After 12 Hours

Wait 12 hours, then harvest your Corn. Use fertilizer/growth boosters for faster growth!

❓Frequently Asked Questions

How do I unlock Corn in Heartopia.Life?

Reach Gardening Level 6, then purchase Corn seeds from Blanc at the Gardening Store for 170 Gold.

How long does Corn take to grow?

Corn takes 12 hours to grow, making it perfect as an overnight crop. Plant before bed, harvest in the morning!

How much does Corn sell for?

Raw corn sells for 510 Gold. However, cooking it into Corn Soup (5-star) can sell for 10,720 Gold!

What can I make with Corn?

Corn is used to make Corn Soup (2 Corn + 1 Milk + 1 Butter). Unlock the recipe at Cooking Level 5 from Massimo. Corn is also a favorite food for Pandas!

Is Corn profitable?

Yes! Corn has a 200% ROI (340G profit per seed). When cooked into 5-star Corn Soup, profitability increases dramatically to 10,720G per dish.
