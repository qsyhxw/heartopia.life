Plant Corn before bed (12hr), harvest in morning. Then plant Strawberry (6hr), harvest in evening. Repeat daily for maximum Gold!

💡 Farming Tips

✅ Maximize Profits

  • • Cook, don't sell raw! — Cooked dishes give 2-3x more Gold
  • • Use 1★ ingredients for cooking — Higher ★ may sell for more raw
  • • Use Fertilizer — Increases harvest quality
  • • Use Growth Booster — Reduces growth time
  • • Water regularly — Skipping delays growth!

🏆 Best Crops by Situation

  • • Active play: Tomato (15 min) or Pineapple (30 min)
  • • Overnight farming: Corn (12 hr) or Lettuce (8 hr)
  • • Best ROI: Potato (Lv1) or Avocado (Lv13)
  • • For recipes: Wheat (baking) or Strawberry (jam)
