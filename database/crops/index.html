Avoid These Mistakes

  • Do not use a growth time as a promised profit calculation.
  • Plan longer crops only when you will be away long enough.
  • Check current in-game details after a game update before spending heavily.

Crops FAQ

How many crops are listed in Heartopia?
This database currently lists 17 crop entries.

What is the quickest crop growth time?
Lemon Verbena, Romaine Lettuce, Tomato, and White Radish are listed at 15 minutes.

What takes the longest to grow?
Avocado is listed at 13 hours.

Does this page show sell prices?
No. It focuses on fields that can be checked consistently: crop name, seed price, and growth time.
