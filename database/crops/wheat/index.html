Heartopia Wheat

Wheat is a medium-window grain crop. It is useful when you want a 4-hour planting cycle and a steady ingredient supply for cooking plans.

Seed price
95 G
Growth time
4h
Type
Grain
Window
Medium

How to Get Wheat

  1. Open the map and find Blanc or the gardening shop.
  2. Check the seed list for Wheat seeds.
  3. Buy enough seeds for your available planters.
  4. Plant, water, and return after about 4h.

If the seed is not visible in your shop yet, keep progressing your gardening path and recheck later.

Best Time to Plant

Wheat fits a same-day return: plant it before a questing block, school break, work session, or evening routine when you can check back in about 4 hours.

Planner tip: Choose Wheat when 15-minute crops feel too hands-on but overnight crops are too long.

Uses and Planning Notes

Crop collection
Harvest it once and mark it in My Progress.
Recipe prep
Use Wheat when a recipe asks for a grain-style ingredient. Check the recipe database before planting a large batch.
Seed budget
95 G per planter before any extra items.

Common Mistakes

  • Buying seeds before checking how many empty planters you have.
  • Starting Wheat when its 4h growth time does not match your next login.
  • Using old sell-price or profit posts as fixed values.
