🍓 Jams (Best Early Profit!)

Jams are unlocked automatically when you unlock Cooking. Use 4 fruits to make jam — often more profitable than selling raw fruits. Check recipe sell prices before selling high-star ingredients raw.

Recipe Ingredients Level Sell Price Profit Tip
🍎 Apple Jam 4x Apple 1 180G Free apples from trees!
🫐 Blueberry Jam 4x Blueberry 1 170-255G Found in House Plots
🍓 Strawberry Jam 4x Strawberry 1 ~180G Grow in garden
🌈 Mixed Jam ⭐ 4x Mixed Fruits 1 320G (3★) Good profit route

💡 Pro Tip: 4 Blueberries sell for 64G raw, but one Blueberry Jam sells for 170G+ (1★). That's 2.5x profit!

💡 Cooking Tips

✅ Maximize Profits

  • • Make Jams early! — Often stronger than selling raw fruit; confirm the current value on the recipe price list.
  • • Mushroom Stew = 500G — A useful mushroom route when you can forage the ingredients cheaply.
  • • Use 1★ ingredients — Higher star ingredients may sell for more raw
  • • Buy recipes from Massimo — New recipes at each cooking level
  • • Don't burn! — Watch the QTE minigame

⚠️ Cooking Mechanics

  • • QTE Minigame — Press button at the right time to avoid burning
  • • Higher Cooking Level = Higher quality dishes (more stars)
  • • Energy from food — Dishes restore energy when eaten
  • • Gift dishes to NPCs — Many love cooked food!

Heartopia Recipe FAQ

Where do I check Heartopia recipe sell prices?

Use the recipe price list for dish values and star-quality multipliers. If you want to include ingredient costs, open the recipe calculator.

Is Grass Cake the same as Green Roll Cake?

Many players use Grass Cake as the search term for Green Roll Cake. Start with the Green Roll Cake guide, then use the Grass Cake recipe page if you are following event wording.

How do I make Black Truffle Pie?

Black Truffle Pie uses 2x Black Truffle, 1x Wheat, and 1x Egg. The rare step is usually finding the truffles, so use the Black Truffle location guide before cooking.
