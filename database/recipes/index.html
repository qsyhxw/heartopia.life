🍓 Jams (Best Early Profit!)

Jams are unlocked automatically when you unlock Cooking. Use 4 fruits to make jam — much more profitable than selling raw fruits!

Recipe Ingredients Level Sell Price Profit Tip
🍎 Apple Jam 4x Apple 1 180G Free apples from trees!
🫐 Blueberry Jam 4x Blueberry 1 170-255G Found in House Plots
🍓 Strawberry Jam 4x Strawberry 1 ~180G Grow in garden
🌈 Mixed Jam ⭐ 4x Mixed Fruits 1 320G (3★) Best profit!

💡 Pro Tip: 4 Blueberries sell for 64G raw, but one Blueberry Jam sells for 170G+ (1★). That's 2.5x profit!

💡 Cooking Tips

✅ Maximize Profits

  • • Make Jams early! — Best early-game profit (2.5x raw fruit value)
  • • Mushroom Stew = 500G — Highest selling soup
  • • Use 1★ ingredients — Higher star ingredients may sell for more raw
  • • Buy recipes from Massimo — New recipes at each cooking level
  • • Don't burn! — Watch the QTE minigame

⚠️ Cooking Mechanics

  • • QTE Minigame — Press button at the right time to avoid burning
  • • Higher Cooking Level = Higher quality dishes (more stars)
  • • Energy from food — Dishes restore energy when eaten
  • • Gift dishes to NPCs — Many love cooked food!
