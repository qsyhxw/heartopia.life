Heartopia Chocolate Sauce Recipe and Unlock Steps

Chocolate Sauce uses 4 Cacao Beans per batch. Community recipe guides identify it as a Mixed Jam variant. Cook Mixed Jam first, then use four beans in its ingredient slots to learn the sauce.

Unlock and cook Chocolate Sauce · If you cannot get Cacao Beans · Plan your harvest · Check values before selling

Unlock and cook Chocolate Sauce

  1. Unlock Cooking with Massimo and learn Mixed Jam.
  2. Prepare Mixed Jam once before trying its variants.
  3. Collect four Cacao Beans. Some English guides call them Cocoa Beans.
  4. At the stove, choose the Mixed Jam base and fill the relevant ingredient slots with four beans. Complete the cooking actions.
  5. Check the result and recipe book for Chocolate Sauce before making a larger batch.

The unlock route is documented by TH.GL’s hidden recipe guide ↗ and AllThingsHow’s recipe guide ↗. These are community instructions.

If you cannot get Cacao Beans

Check Blanc’s Gardening Store for Cacao Tree Seeds and read the unlock condition shown on your account. The recipe’s Cooking Level 1 label does not guarantee access to its farming ingredient. Grow and harvest the tree before attempting the recipe.

If the sauce appears in your recipe list but you have no beans, work on ingredient access first. Ordinary fruit produces a different jam variant. The shop inventory includes Cacao Tree Seeds. Gardening Store inventory ↗

Check values before selling

Heartodex lists 1,400 Gold and 80 energy for the first quality tier. TH.GL independently lists four Cocoa Beans at a stove. Other recipe sites show substantially different sale and energy values, so check your current item tooltip before making a profit plan. Ingredient costs also matter.
