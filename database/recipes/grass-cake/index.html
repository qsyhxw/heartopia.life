Heartopia Grass Cake Recipe Guide: Pleasant Goat Event & 5,500G Profit

The Grass Cake (青草蛋糕) is a limited-time collaboration dish introduced during the "Pleasant Goat and Big Big Wolf" crossover event. Known for its shockingly high return on investment, the Grass Cake is considered a top-tier endgame dish for players looking to farm massive amounts of Gold.

⚠️

Disambiguation Notice

Many non-Asian players searching for "Grass Cake" are actually looking for the Green Roll Cake (which requires green sugar during rainbow weather). The genuine event Grass Cake on this page has different ingredients and heavily outperforms the Roll Cake in profit.

📖 How to Unlock the Grass Cake Recipe

HIGH RISK: EXCLUSIVE EVENT REWARD

This recipe was distributed as an exclusive login reward during the Pleasant Goat and Big Big Wolf Collaboration Event, which took place from February 14, 2026, to March 16, 2026. If you missed this window, there is currently no way for new players to obtain it. Watch out for future updates or cash shop rotations.

If you logged into Heartopia during this window, you automatically received the recipe sheet in your mailbox. Once learned from your backpack, the recipe becomes permanently available at your home stove.

🌾 Grass Cake Ingredients & Where to Find Them

Required Materials

To bake one Grass Cake, you will need the following 4 ingredients:

  • 🌾 1x Wheat
  • 🥛 1x Milk
  • 🍵 1x Matcha Powder
  • 🌿 1x Weed

How to Gather the Materials

The materials require a mix of Farming and shopping from town NPC vendors:

  1. Wheat: Purchase Wheat Seeds from Blanc and grow them on your home farming plot.
  2. Milk: Available for direct purchase from the cooking vendor, Massimo.
  3. Matcha Powder: Also purchased directly from Massimo.
  4. Weed: This is a byproduct of farming. Whenever you harvest your fully grown crops (like Wheat), you have a random chance to pull out Weeds simultaneously. Do not throw them away!

💰 Sell Price & Insane Profit Margins

The main reason the Grass Cake is so highly sought after is its astronomical sell price. While the ingredient setup costs time and a little gold, the net return is staggering once your Cooking Hobby level is high enough to produce multi-star variations.

Star Rating Sell Price
⭐ 1-Star 690 Gold
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5-Star ~5,500 Gold
💡 Strategic Tip: Because weeds are a random drop from crop harvesting, we highly recommend dedicating a portion of your farm to fast-growing low-level crops exclusively to farm the Weeds needed for massive Grass Cake cooking sessions.
