Heartopia Goliath Beetle Location

The Goliath Beetle appears at Deer Tower in Rainbow weather, during Dusk and Night. You need Insect Level 12.

Location
Deer Tower
Weather
Rainbow
Time

Level

Best Time to Search

Do not start the Deer Tower route until both requirements line up: Rainbow weather and the late-day Dusk or Night window. This avoids spending a whole route while the beetle cannot appear.

Before leaving
Confirm Insect Level 12.
At Deer Tower
Make a complete loop through the accessible area.
Keep checking
Stay until the Dusk or Night window ends.

Common Misses

  • Searching at Day or Dawn instead of Dusk or Night.
  • Waiting at Deer Tower during Sunny or Rainy weather.
  • Arriving before Insect Level 12 is available.

Pair This Route

Deer Tower is also the listed area for Sunset Morpho, though its time window is earlier. Check the full insect database before setting out.
