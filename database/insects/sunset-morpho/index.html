Plan the Deer Tower Route

Sunset Morpho shares Deer Tower with other late-game catches, but its own window is early. Arrive at Dawn or during Day on a Rainbow-weather day, then leave the area only after you have checked the full route.

Why It May Be Missing

  • Rainbow weather is required.
  • Do not wait for Dusk or Night; those are outside its listed schedule.
  • Make sure you are at Deer Tower, not Amethyst Beach.
