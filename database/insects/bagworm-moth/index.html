How to Catch Bagworm Moths

Note: Bagworm Moths are notoriously solitary buggy creatures. Expert players note that often only one spawns in the designated area at a time.

The Bagworm Moth is a high-level, elusive insect in Heartopia. Because of its strict spawning parameters and highly fragile alert state, players frequently struggle to track these down for compendium completion and rare resource gathering. Follow this database entry to find the exact tree types and weather conditions needed.

Best Locations & Tree Types

Unlike common butterflies that flutter aimlessly across town, the Bagworm Moth is mostly stationary and very location-dependent:

  • Primary Spawn Location: You must travel to the Forest biome, specifically navigating around the Deer Tower landmark. They almost never venture far from this specific quadrant.
  • Specific Perches: Instead of hiding high in the canopy, keep your camera pointed downwards. They frequently hang from low tree branches. Additionally, they have a unique quirk where they can spawn hanging off man-made objects such as hanging light lamps and star garlands decorating the forest!

Time & Weather Conditions

You can spend hours staring at star garlands, but if your watch reads the wrong time, you will inevitably turn up empty-handed:

  • Time of Day: They are nocturnal variants. You will only start seeing them appear during Dusk/Evening and throughout the Night.
  • Weather Rules: Check the sky. They prefer dry, calm conditions and will only spawn if the weather is classified as Sunny or experiencing a Rainbow. You will not find them during rainstorms.

Required Tools & Traps

Even if you find one hanging peacefully, you will need the correct character progression stats to bag it successfully.

  • Hobby Requirement: It is locked behind Insect Catching Level 10. Lower leveled players won't be able to secure the prompt even if they find the model.
  • Stealth Approach: Bagworm Moths are extremely skittish. You must enter "sneak mode" to approach them. If you run up to their tree or miss your initial net swing, they will instantly vanish and you'll have to wait for the area to reload and spawn a new one. Utilize our Insect Catching Tools Guide to ensure your net is upgraded.
