📍Where & When to Catch

1. Required Location

Fishing Village Wharf

This insect is exclusive to the wharf area. It flutters strictly around the wooden docks of the Fishing Village.

2. Required Weather

🌧️ Rain OR 🌈 Rainbow

Tip: It will NOT spawn on sunny days. Use your watch to check the weather forecast!

3. Time of Day

12:00 AM - 6:00 PM

It's a daytime to early evening insect. Best caught during rainy afternoons!

🦋How to Catch

1

Reach Level 4

You must be at least Insect Catching Level 4 to see and catch this butterfly. If you are lower level, it won't spawn.

2

Equip Bubble Net

Equip your Bubble Net tool from your inventory.

3

Use Stealth Mode

Hold the action button to charge your net and move slowly. If you run, you will scare it away!

❓FAQ

Where exactly is the Fishing Village Wharf?

The Wharf is the wooden dock area in the Fishing Village where you can also catch fish. It's located along the coast.

Is the Beautiful Leopard rare?

Yes, it is considered a rare insect because it requires both a specific location and specific weather conditions (Rain/Rainbow) to appear.

What can I do with it?

You can sell it to Albert Jr. for Gold (around 360g for 4-star quality) or display it in a Vivarium in your house.
