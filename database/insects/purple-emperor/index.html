📅Day 12 Mission Guide

Mission Requirement

Catch a Purple Emperor with at least ★★ (2-Star) Rarity.

⚠️ Warning: If you catch a 1-star version, the quest will NOT complete. Keep catching until you find a 2-star or higher! Rarity is random.

📍Where to Find

1. Flower Field

The best place to look! Check specifically around the windmill area and the dense flower patches.

2. Whale Mountain

Look around the grassy areas on the mountain slopes. They often flutter near the path leading up.

3. Purple Beach (Rare)

Occasionally spotted near the vegetation bordering the beach.

🦋How to Catch

Step 1: Check the weather via your smartwatch. It must be Sunny or Rainbow. If it's raining, sleep or wait.

Step 2: Ensure it is past 12:00 PM (Noon). They do not spawn in the morning.

Step 3: Look for bright purple wings. They are quite visible against the green grass.

Step 4: Use a charged bubble net shot if it's moving fast.

❓FAQ

I caught one but the quest didn't finish?

Check the star rating in your inventory. The mission requires a 2-Star or higher rarity. If you caught a 1-Star, you need to catch another one.

Can I catch it in the morning?

No. It only spawns from 12:00 PM (Noon) to 12:00 AM (Midnight).
