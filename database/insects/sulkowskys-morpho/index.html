🦋 Heartopia Sulkowsky's Morpho Location

Sulkowsky's Morpho is the rare attractor butterfly many players search as Sulkowski Morpho, Sulkowskyi Morpho, or Blue Morpho. It is best handled as an Inflatable Insect Attractor catch, not a normal flower-route butterfly.

Inflatable Insect Attractor Any Time Any Weather Level 4 Attractor Unlock
🫧

Method

Inflatable Insect Attractor

📍

Best Areas

Onsen Mountain / Fishing Village

🕐

Time

All Day

🌦️

Weather

Sunny, Rainy, or Rainbow

Quick Answer

To catch Sulkowsky's Morpho in Heartopia, buy an Inflatable Insect Attractor from Naniwa at River Garden, then place it at Onsen Mountain, Fishing Village, or another active insect spawn area. The insect-catching guide lists it as an attractor butterfly that can appear all day in sunny, rainy, or rainbow weather.

Need the item?

Buy from Naniwa for 2,000 Gold or 1 Wishing Star.

Need the level?

Use the attractor after Insect Catching Level 4.

Need the net?

Keep the Bubble Net ready before placing the item.

  1. 1

    Buy the Inflatable Insect Attractor

  2. 2

    Go to a high-activity insect route

    Start with Onsen Mountain or Fishing Village before testing other areas.

  3. 3

    Place the attractor and watch the first wave

    The attractor lures rare insects. Sulkowsky's Morpho is one of the key targets.

  4. 4

    Keep your net equipped before placing the item so you do not lose a rare spawn.

Common Mistakes

Searching normal flower routes

Morpho Butterfly and Blue Morpho Butterfly use flower routes. Sulkowsky's Morpho is handled through the attractor.

Using the item too early

Make sure Insect Catching Level 4 and the Bubble Net are ready before spending the attractor.

Standing in a quiet area

If you get weak results, move to Onsen Mountain, Fishing Village, or another active insect area.

Expecting every attractor to hit

The item lures rare insects, so you may need more than one attempt to get this exact butterfly.

After You Catch It

Check the star rating before selling. The site's insect-catching page lists a 5-star Sulkowsky's Morpho sell value of 720 Gold, so higher-quality catches can help offset the attractor cost.

If you are filling the insect collection, mark this one separately from Morpho Butterfly and Blue Morpho Butterfly because the route and item requirement are different.
