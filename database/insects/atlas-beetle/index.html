Heartopia Atlas Beetle Location

Catch the Atlas Beetle at Spirit-Oak Pine Forest during Rainbow weather. It can appear in the Day, Dusk, and Night windows.

Location
Spirit-Oak Pine Forest
Weather
Rainbow
Time
Day, Dusk, Night
Level
11

Quick Catch Route

  1. Reach Insect Level 11 before starting the search.
  2. Travel to Spirit-Oak Pine Forest when the weather changes to Rainbow.
  3. Check tree trunks carefully while moving through the forest.
  4. Keep checking through Day, Dusk, or Night until you find it.

Why It Is Not Appearing

  • Wrong weather: Atlas Beetle is limited to Rainbow weather.
  • Wrong area: use Spirit-Oak Pine Forest rather than another wooded location.
  • Level locked: it requires Insect Level 11.
  • Missed trunks: inspect the tree-trunk spots as you circle the area.

Rainbow Forest Targets

A Rainbow-weather trip to Spirit-Oak Pine Forest can also be useful for the Golden Stag Beetle. Use the filters in the insect database to compare the current forest schedule.

Atlas Beetle FAQ

Where is the Atlas Beetle in Heartopia?
It appears in Spirit-Oak Pine Forest.

What weather does Atlas Beetle need?
Rainbow weather.

Can I catch Atlas Beetle at night?
Yes. Its listed schedule includes Day, Dusk, and Night.
