Conditions to Check First

This target has two valid weather choices, but only late-day hours. Start at Suburban Lake during Dusk or Night, then check whether the weather is Sunny or Rainbow. Rainy weather does not match this entry.

Requirement

Troubleshooting

  • Use Suburban Lake, not a different lake area.
  • Wait for Dusk or Night; Day and Dawn are outside the schedule.
  • Rainbow and Sunny work; Rainy does not.
