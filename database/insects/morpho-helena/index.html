Heartopia Morpho Helena Location

Find Morpho Helena at Amethyst Beach in Rainbow weather during Day or Dawn.

Location

Level

Beach Search Checklist

  1. Bring your Insect Level to 10.
  2. Go to Amethyst Beach once Rainbow weather begins.
  3. Search during Dawn or Day, before the Dusk window starts.
  4. Use a full pass through the beach before leaving.

If You Cannot Find It

  • Rainbow is required; Sunny and Rainy conditions do not match this entry.
  • Morpho Helena is tied to Amethyst Beach, not Deer Tower.
  • The time window ends after Day, so return at the next Dawn.
