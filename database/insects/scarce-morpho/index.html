Crater Lake Catch Route

  1. Reach Insect Level 13.
  2. Head to Crater Lake in Rainbow weather.
  3. Search the full area during Day or Dusk.
  4. Leave Night and Dawn for another target; they are outside this schedule.

Why It Is Not Appearing

  • Scarce Morpho requires Rainbow weather.
  • It is tied to Crater Lake.
  • Its listed window is Day or Dusk only.
  • It unlocks at Insect Level 13.

High-Level Morpho Collection

Scarce Morpho is a later collection target. For the Level 14 butterfly in a different area, see Glasswing Butterfly; use the insect database to compare all Morphos.
