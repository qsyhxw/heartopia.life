Heartopia Rajah Brooke's Birdwing Location

Rajah Brooke's Birdwing is a level 3 butterfly entry. The current insects table lists it at Player Home, during 6PM-12PM, in sunny or rainbow weather.

Quick Answer

Check your Player Home route in sunny or rainbow weather. The current table lists the time window as 6PM-12PM; use that window for your route, and follow your in-game entry if it displays a different time.

Best route: sweep home garden edges, open lawn paths, and any nearby attractor-style insect spots before leaving for Flower Field butterflies.

Catch Checklist

  1. Check the forecast and wait for sunny or rainbow weather.
  2. Start at Player Home instead of Flower Field or Forest.
  3. Use the 6PM-12PM table window for your route timing.
  4. Equip the Bubble Net before walking through garden edges.
  5. If you only see common home insects, finish a full home loop before changing zones.
