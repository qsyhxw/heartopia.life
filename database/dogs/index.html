❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How many dog breeds are in Heartopia?

There are 11 dog breeds: Corgi, Shiba Inu, Beagle, Rural Dog, Golden Retriever, Labrador, Poodle, Rottweiler, Shepherd Dog, Sled Dog, and Dalmatian.

Why can't I adopt a dog?

You must adopt a cat first! Cat Caring must be unlocked before Dog Caring becomes available.

Can I train my dog?

Yes! Dogs can learn tricks like Seek Treasure, Shake Head, and Give Paw. More commands unlock at higher Dog Caring levels.

What's the difference between dogs and cats?

Dogs can be trained and walked, and have the exclusive Lone Wolf trait. Cats cannot be trained or walked.

How many dogs can I have?

Similar to cats, you can adopt more dogs as your Dog Caring level increases. Check your hobby level for current capacity.
