📋Sled Dog Overview

The Sled Dog is one of the 11 dog breeds available for adoption in Heartopia.Life. To adopt a Sled Dog, you must first unlock the pet care system by reaching DG Level 12 and completing specific hobby requirements.

💡 Important Note

The Adoption Center displays 3 random dogs daily. If a Sled Dog isn't available today, check back tomorrow as the selection refreshes every day!

🔓How to Unlock & Adopt

Follow these steps to adopt your Sled Dog:

1️⃣ Reach DG Level 12

Complete daily Resident Requests and earn contribution medals to level up your Developer's Guild membership. This typically takes several days of consistent gameplay.

2️⃣ Unlock Cat Caring Hobby First

Visit Mrs. Joan at the Adoption Center (middle of town). You MUST unlock the Cat Caring hobby before Dog Caring. This requires 1 Hobby Expansion Ticket.

3️⃣ Unlock Dog Caring Hobby

After completing the Cat Caring tutorial, speak to Mrs. Joan again. Use another Hobby Expansion Ticket to unlock the Dog Caring hobby.

4️⃣ Visit the Adoption Center

Check the 3 available dogs each day. The selection rotates daily, so if no Sled Dog is available, return the next day.

5️⃣ Adopt Your Sled Dog

Select the Sled Dog and pay 500 Gold for a physical examination. Name your new pet, and it will be sent to your house!

🐾Caring for Your Sled Dog

Once adopted, your Sled Dog requires daily care to build affection and unlock rewards:

🍖 Feeding

Feed your dog daily. Dogs eat Dog Food, some mushrooms, and certain cooked meals. Monitor hunger levels via the Pet app.

🎾 Playing

Play with your dog to increase bond levels and keep energy high. Check energy levels in the Pet app.

🎓 Training

Train your dog to improve skills and strengthen your bond.

🛁 Bathing

Keep your dog clean with regular baths to maintain happiness.

🚶 Going Out

Take your dog outside for walks and exploration.

⚠️ Note on Favorite Foods

Specific favorite foods for Sled Dogs have not been confirmed in available sources. General dog foods like Dog Food and mushrooms are known to work. We'll update this guide as more information becomes available.

❓Frequently Asked Questions

How do I unlock the Sled Dog in Heartopia.Life?

Reach DG Level 12, unlock Cat Caring hobby first, then unlock Dog Caring hobby. Visit the Adoption Center daily to find a Sled Dog (selection rotates daily).

How much does it cost to adopt a Sled Dog?

Adopting any dog costs 500 Gold for a physical examination at the Adoption Center.

Where do I find Mrs. Joan?

Mrs. Joan is located at the Adoption Center in the middle of town. She's the pet care mentor who helps you unlock Cat Caring and Dog Caring hobbies.

Can I adopt multiple Sled Dogs?

Yes! You can adopt up to 3 dogs total (any breed combination) by leveling up your Dog Caring hobby to Level 1 and Level 4.

What do Sled Dogs eat?

Dogs eat Dog Food, some mushrooms, and certain cooked meals. Specific favorite foods for Sled Dogs are not yet confirmed, but general dog foods work well.
