Hawfinch in Heartopia

Hawfinch is an Onsen Mountain bird target. The current birds database lists it at Onsen Mountain Crater Lake, available all day in normal sunny, rainy, or rainbow weather.

Quick Answer

Go to Onsen Mountain Crater Lake and look for Hawfinch with the Bird Scanner. It is listed as an all-day bird in sunny, rainy, or rainbow weather.

Best use: If you are already routing Onsen Mountain, Hawfinch is a flexible target because it does not require a morning-only or night-only window.

Photo Route Checklist

  1. Travel to: Onsen Mountain Crater Lake.
  2. Check conditions: Any normal sunny, rainy, or rainbow weather works according to the current database.
  3. Equip: Bird Scanner before you approach the lake edge.
  4. Move slowly: Do not sprint directly at the bird.
  5. Wait for action: Better poses can produce better Info Cards than idle shots.

FAQ

Where is Hawfinch?

The current Heartopia.Life birds database lists Hawfinch at Onsen Mountain Crater Lake.

Does Hawfinch need special weather?

It is listed for sunny, rainy, and rainbow weather, so it is more flexible than birds with a single weather gate.

Why am I not seeing it?

Recheck that you are at Onsen Mountain Crater Lake, equip the Bird Scanner, move slowly, and sweep the lake area instead of staying in one camera angle.
