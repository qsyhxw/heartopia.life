Heartopia European Robin Location

If you are searching for "Robin" in Heartopia, the bird table entry is European Robin. It is listed in the Central Area, available all day in sunny, rainy, or rainbow weather.

Quick Answer

Search the Central Area with your Bird Scanner. European Robin is one of the easiest bird entries to route because it does not require a specific time window or single weather type.

Good first sweep: Central Area also includes Eurasian Magpie and House Sparrow, so it is a useful starting point for filling early Info Cards.

Central Area Route

  1. Start in Central Area and equip the Bird Scanner before moving through open paths.
  2. Make a slow loop around trees, small open spaces, and nearby town edges.
  3. Keep the camera steady when the bird enters frame; sudden movement can lose the shot.
  4. If you only see Magpie or House Sparrow, finish the loop and return after a short area reset.
  5. Save higher quality cards for collection progress before selling duplicates.
