Quick Birdwatching Route

Search the Spirit-Oak Pine Forest during Day or Dusk once Birdwatching Level 12 is unlocked. All regular weather types work.

  1. Confirm Birdwatching Level 12.
  2. Travel to Spirit-Oak Pine Forest.
  3. Match the listed weather and time.
  4. Photograph the bird and mark it in your collection.

If You Cannot Find It

Dawn and Night are outside the Snowy Owl schedule. Check the forest route during the correct two time periods.
