Green Peafowl Birdwatching Guide

Fact Check: The Green Peafowl is available during all weather conditions and times. Stop waiting for sunny mornings!

In Heartopia, Birdwatching stands as one of the six major progression hobbies. Out of the numerous species available to document, the Green Peafowl—with its striking bronze-green plumage—is highly sought after because of its symbolic value and high-yield information cards. Here is how you can consistently find and catalog it.

Natural Habitat & Regions

If you're aiming to complete your bird compendium, you will need to head to specific zones:

  • The Nest of Hundreds: This is the guaranteed spawn location for the Green Peafowl. The large roaming area provides several perching spots, primarily slightly elevated areas where their plumage stands out against the terrain.
  • No Weather Locks: Unlike insect catching or deep-sea fishing, you don't need to stress over the server's weather forecast. You can track them down during Sunny, Rainy, or Rainbow weather conditions. Furthermore, they are active at Dawn, Morning, Afternoon, Dusk, AND Night.

Photographic Interaction Tips

Unlike bugs or fish, you do not capture birds with a net physically. Instead, you interact with them via the Birdwatching Mechanics:

  • Using the Scanner: You must equip your camera scanner/binoculars. When you spot the Green Peafowl, aim your reticle and wait for the scanning meter to load fully without spooking the bird.
  • Generating Info Cards: Once a successful scan is complete, the game yields a "Bird Information Card". The Green Peafowl card provides excellent market value.
  • Economic Benefit: After gathering a surplus of these cards, you can trade them to specific NPCs for premium rewards, exclusive birding outfits, or simply hawk them on the market for an influx of gold to fund your house expansions.
