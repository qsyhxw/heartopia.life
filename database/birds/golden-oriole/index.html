How to Find the Golden Oriole

The Golden Oriole (Eurasian Golden Oriole) is a bright yellow bird that can be found along the residential areas (Home Lots) and the banks of the Rosy River. The current bird table lists it as an all-day bird in sunny, rainy, or rainbow weather.

Quick Route

Start at Home Lots, follow the Rosy River edge, and keep the Bird Scanner ready before moving into open grass or tree-lined areas.

Catching Tips

  • Move Slowly: The Golden Oriole is described as "easily spooked." Approach cautiously to get a good photograph or observation.
  • Look for Yellow: Its bright yellow plumage makes it stand out against green trees or grass.
  • Rainbow Hunting: It is one of the birds that can appear during Rainbow Weather, making it a reliable find during mixed conditions.
