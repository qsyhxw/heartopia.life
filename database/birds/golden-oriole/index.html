How to Find the Golden Oriole

The Golden Oriole (Eurasian Golden Oriole) is a bright yellow bird that can be found relatively easily once you meet the level requirement. It inhabits the residential areas (Home Lots) and the banks of the Rosy River.

⚠️ Requirement Warning

You must have reached Bird Watching Level 3 to spot this bird. If you are a lower level, it will not spawn for you.

Catching Tips

  • Move Slowly: The Golden Oriole is described as "easily spooked." Approach cautiously to get a good photograph or observation.
  • Look for Yellow: Its bright yellow plumage makes it stand out against green trees or grass.
  • Rainbow Hunting: It is one of the birds that can appear during Rainbow Weather, making it a reliable find during mixed conditions.
