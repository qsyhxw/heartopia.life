Winter Mandarin Duck in Heartopia

The current winter bird guide does not list a separate Winter Mandarin Duck entry. If your search is about a winter duck-like bird, start with the confirmed winter bird route around Suburban Lakes.

Quick Answer

Check the Winter Birds guide first. The listed duck-like winter entries are Winter Mallard, Winter Eurasian Wigeon, and Winter Smew, with the shared route pointing to Suburban Lakes during Winter Frost Season.
