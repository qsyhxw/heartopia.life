Quick Birdwatching Route

Visit the Forest Jump Puzzle at Dawn or Night after reaching Birdwatching Level 14. Any regular weather works.

  1. Confirm Birdwatching Level 14.
  2. Travel to Forest Jump Puzzle.
  3. Match the listed weather and time.
  4. Photograph the bird and mark it in your collection.

If You Cannot Find It

Day and Dusk do not match its schedule. Check the jump-puzzle area rather than a general forest route.
