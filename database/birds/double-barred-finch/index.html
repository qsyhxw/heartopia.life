Double-Barred Finch is a Fishing Village bird. The current birds table lists it at Fishing Village Lighthouse, available all day in sunny, rainy, or rainbow weather.

Quick Answer

Go to Fishing Village Lighthouse and sweep the lighthouse approach with the Bird Scanner. Double-Barred Finch is flexible because it is listed as all day and works across normal sunny, rainy, and rainbow weather.

Route use: Pair this bird with Przevalski's Parrotbill at Fishing Village Square and Cinnamon Ground Dove at East Pier.

Fishing Village Route

  1. Travel to Fishing Village and move toward the Lighthouse landmark.
  2. Equip the Bird Scanner before entering the lighthouse approach area.
  3. Walk the outer edge slowly; do not sprint into the bird's space.
  4. If the bird is not in frame, circle back through the lighthouse path and nearby open ground.
  5. Wait for a chirp, forage, or short movement before taking the shot for a better card.

FAQ

Where do I find Double-Barred Finch?

The birds database lists Double-Barred Finch at Fishing Village Lighthouse.

Does Double-Barred Finch need rainy weather?

No special single weather is listed. Sunny, rainy, and rainbow weather all work.

Why am I only finding other Fishing Village birds?

Recheck the exact sub-area. Double-Barred Finch is tied to the Lighthouse, while the other village birds use the Square or East Pier.
