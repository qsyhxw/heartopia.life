Search route

Where to Find Dolphins

  1. 1. Open the live event route.
    Reach the Call of Whales entry requirement and travel to Whalefall Canyon.
  2. 2. Complete the dolphin introduction.
    Follow the dolphin investigation or Rory prompt before attempting to feed the group.
  3. 3. Search the canyon event area.
    Use the active quest guidance or animal marker. The official announcement confirms Whalefall Canyon but does not publish a fixed coordinate.
  4. 4. Approach and use the live interaction prompt.
    Feed or interact when the prompt appears, then check group familiarity in the event interface.

No weather requirement confirmed

The official visit notice identifies the area and dates, not a Sunny, Rainy, or Rainbow-only spawn. Do not wait for a specific weather cycle unless the live game prompt says otherwise.

Interaction progression

Dolphin Familiarity & Life Fragments

Step 1

Feed and interact

Use the interaction shown in the live event. The game may control available feeds or daily opportunities, so follow the on-screen prompt rather than a fixed food list.

Step 2

Raise group familiarity

Progress belongs to the visiting dolphin group. Check the event panel after interactions to confirm that familiarity increased.

Step 3

Reach Lv.4 first

Familiarity Level 4 unlocks Life Fragments. Dolphins may also visit and leave gifts at your Seamount underwater home.

After the visit: the official notice says Life Fragments may still be received after the dolphins leave. Unlocking the feature before the deadline is therefore the safest priority.

Troubleshooting

Why Dolphin Interaction Is Not Working

No feed or interaction prompt

Return to the live Call of Whales quest and finish the dolphin investigation or Rory introduction. Reload Whalefall Canyon after the step completes.

Dolphins are not visible

Confirm the server date, event access, and current quest state. Re-enter the canyon and follow the active marker rather than waiting for different weather.

Familiarity did not increase

Check that the introduction is complete and that the interaction was accepted by the event panel. Daily or prompt-based limits may apply.

No Seamount visit or gift

Visits are associated with the Seamount underwater home and familiarity progression; they should not be treated as a guaranteed result after every interaction.

Quick answers

Dolphin FAQ

Where are Dolphins?

In the Whalefall Canyon event area during Call of Whales.

When is the Dolphin event?

The visit began July 18 and ends August 22, 2026 at 05:59 server time.

What is the best familiarity target?

Level 4, because it unlocks the Life Fragment feature.

Do Dolphins stay permanently?

No. They leave Whale Island when Call of Whales ends, although Life Fragments may still be received afterward.
