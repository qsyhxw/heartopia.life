🦌 Heartopia Deer Guide: Sika Deer Trough & Favorite Food

Sika Deer is the first wildlife animal most players meet through Animal Care. This page gives the deer trough route, favorite foods, reward use, and fixes for common feeding issues.

Quick Answer

The Heartopia deer is the Sika Deer. Its trough is in the forest area east of Forest Lake, between the Deer Tower and Spirit Oak Pine Forest. Use Lettuce, House Salad, or Tree Branches at the trough after Animal Care is unlocked.

Where to Find the Deer Trough

Region
Forest

Use Forest Lake as the nearest landmark.

Route
Deer Tower path

Check the stretch toward Spirit Oak Pine Forest.

  1. 1 Progress until Animal Care appears in your Town Guide flow.
  2. 2 Talk to Annie when the wildlife tutorial asks you to inspect or use a trough.
  3. 3 Go east of Forest Lake and look between the Deer Tower and Spirit Oak Pine Forest.
  4. 4 After the trough is discovered, use the Animals app on your watch to track it again.

Sika Deer Favorite Food

Food Type Best Use
🥬 Lettuce Crop Easy daily deer feed once your farm can spare it.
🥗 House Salad Cooked dish Good when you want stronger bond progress and have ingredients ready.
🌿 Tree Branches Forage/material Useful early option if you are short on crops or cooked food.

If the animal UI shows a different prompt after an update, follow the in-game prompt first. Food lists can change when animal systems are adjusted.

Daily Feeding Routine

📍

Check the trough

Use the watch app after discovery so you do not waste time searching the forest route again.

🥬

Add one favorite

Start with Lettuce or Tree Branches, then use House Salad when you can cook it comfortably.

🎁

Return later

Bond increases over repeated visits, and gifts may appear near the trough at higher bond.

Rewards and Use

Deer are part of the wildlife friendship loop. They are useful for learning how troughs, favorite food, animal bond, and wildlife gifts work before you chase harder animals such as Panda, Fox, or Sea Otter.

What deer can do

  • Teach the basic trough feeding loop
  • Raise wildlife bond through repeated feeding
  • Help with animal care progress and related collection goals
  • Leave gifts near the trough as bond improves

What deer cannot do

  • Move into your home like a cat or dog
  • Use a pet bowl or pet bed
  • Replace the trough system
  • Appear reliably before Animal Care is unlocked

Troubleshooting

The deer is not appearing at the trough.

Confirm Animal Care is unlocked, check the correct forest trough, and make sure food is placed in the trough. If you just fed it, wait for the next available feeding cycle.

The favorite food does not seem to work.

Try Lettuce first because it is the easiest to confirm. Then test House Salad or Tree Branches. If the game displays a specific item hint, use that item.

I found the deer once but lost the location.

Open the Animals app on your watch and check discovered troughs. If it is not listed, repeat the route east of Forest Lake between Deer Tower and Spirit Oak Pine Forest.
