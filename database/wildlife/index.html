🌿 What is Wildlife?

In Heartopia, many players refer to Wild Animals as “wildlife”: animals that can be interacted with in the open world via Animal Troughs. Community guides consistently describe a loop of finding an animal’s area, placing or using a trough, feeding preferred items, and increasing “bond” over time.

Reality check: Most public guides for wildlife are community-written (not official patch notes). Treat specifics (exact locations, favorite foods, reward thresholds) as “best-known” rather than guaranteed.

🔓 Unlock & Tracking (What We Can Confirm)

  • Unlock timing: Community sources agree that wildlife interaction becomes available after main story progression and reaching a required milestone in DG/Developer progression that unlocks an Animal Care section in the Town Guide.
  • Getting started: Multiple sources mention speaking with an NPC named Annie to begin the Animal Care introduction and complete your first trough tutorial (often described as the Sika Deer trough in the forest).
  • Tracking: After discovering a trough, sources report you can track it using the Animals app on your watch. A manual map marker is also commonly recommended for fast daily return trips.
Why we keep this general: specific “unlock level” requirements differ between sources, so we avoid presenting a single number as confirmed.

🍎 Feeding & Bond Tips

Community guides generally agree on these patterns:

  • Each animal family has 3 “favorite foods” (item names can vary between guides).
  • Feeding favorites is commonly reported to increase bond faster than random items.
  • Bond progression is commonly described as improving visit frequency and increasing the chance of animals leaving small gifts at the trough.
  • Some guides claim weather preferences (e.g., Sunny or Wet) that can boost bond, but this is not consistently documented in official notes.
  • If you want reliable results, use the in-game UI: once an animal is discovered, prefer the game’s own tracking/details over external lists.
