🌿 What is Wildlife in Heartopia?

In Heartopia, Wild Animals (commonly called "wildlife") are creatures you can interact with throughout the open world using Animal Troughs. Unlike pets (cats and dogs), wild animals cannot be adopted but can be befriended through regular feeding.

🎯 Core Gameplay Loop

  1. Discover an animal's habitat area
  2. Find or unlock the Animal Trough
  3. Feed the animal their favorite foods
  4. Increase bond level over time
  5. Receive rewards and gifts

🎁 Bond Rewards

  • • Animals visit more frequently
  • • Higher chance of leaving gifts
  • • Unlock special interactions
  • • Gallery completion progress
⚠️ Data Accuracy Note: Most wildlife information comes from community guides rather than official patch notes. Treat specific details (exact locations, favorite foods, reward thresholds) as "community-reported" and verify in-game when possible.

🔓 How to Unlock Wildlife System

1

Progress Through Main Story

Wildlife interaction becomes available after reaching a certain milestone in the main story and DG (Developer Grade) progression.

2

Unlock Animal Care in Town Guide

The Animal Care section appears in your Town Guide once you meet the requirements.

3

Talk to NPC Annie

According to community reports, speak with Annie to begin the Animal Care introduction and complete your first trough tutorial (typically the Sika Deer trough in the forest).

4

Use Animals App for Tracking

After discovering a trough, track it using the Animals app on your in-game watch. Setting manual map markers is also recommended for quick daily visits.

📝 Note: Specific unlock level requirements vary between community sources. We avoid stating exact numbers to prevent misinformation. Check your in-game Town Guide for current requirements.

🍎 Feeding & Bond Mechanics

📈 How Bond Works

  • • Each animal family has 3 favorite foods
  • • Feeding favorites increases bond faster than random items
  • • Higher bond = more frequent visits + better gift chances
  • • Bond progress is tracked in the Animals app
  • • Weather bonuses confirmed: Feeding during favorite weather grants bonus bond points

💡 Pro Tips

  • • Visit troughs daily for consistent bond growth
  • • Set map markers for quick navigation
  • • Use in-game UI to verify current favorite foods
  • • Wait for favorite weather when known (Bunnies: sunny, Capybaras/Ferrets: rainy)
  • • Check weather forecast in-game before feeding

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I unlock Wildlife in Heartopia?

Wildlife interaction becomes available after progressing through the main story and reaching a milestone that unlocks the Animal Care section in the Town Guide. Speak with NPC Annie to begin the tutorial with the Sika Deer trough.

How many wild animals are in Heartopia?

Community sources report approximately 8 wild animal families: Panda, Capybara, Fox, Bunny, Sea Otter, Sika Deer, Ferret, and Alpaca. This number may change with game updates.

What are Animal Troughs?

Animal Troughs are feeding stations placed in specific locations where wild animals appear. You can feed animals their favorite foods to increase bond levels and receive rewards like gifts and special interactions.

Can I adopt wild animals as pets?

No, wild animals cannot be adopted. Unlike cats and dogs (which can become pets), wildlife can only be befriended through the trough feeding system. You can increase your bond with them but they remain wild.

How do I track discovered animals?

Use the Animals app on your in-game watch to track discovered troughs and animals. You can also set manual map markers for quick daily visits to each trough location.
