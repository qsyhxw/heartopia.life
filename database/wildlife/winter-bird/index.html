🐦 Winter Birds Guide

A guide to finding the rare Winter Bird Variants. These chilly friends wear special outfits and are only available during the Winter Frost Season!

📅 Jan 31 - Mar 13 📍 Suburban Lakes Limited Time
🐧

1 Where to Find Them

All "Winter" designated birds share the same spawn behavior. You don't need to check different biomes!

Region Suburbs
Specific Spot Suburban Lakes

Tip: Look for them on the water surface or waddling directly next to the lake edges in the Suburbs area. They are easily spotted by their hats and scarves!
