📋 Step-by-Step Unlock Guide

  1. Reach DG Level 12 - Complete quests and activities
  2. Unlock 5 hobbies: Cooking, Gardening, Fishing, Insect Catching, Birdwatching

  4. Visit Mrs. Joan at Pet Supplies shop (Garden Street, Central Square)

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How many cat breeds are in Heartopia?

There are 14 cat breeds in Heartopia, including 2 rare breeds (Panda Cat and Golden Spotted Cat).

What level do I need to adopt cats?

You need DG Level 12, plus 5 hobbies unlocked and a Hobby Expansion Ticket.

Are rare cats stronger than common cats?

No! Rare cats are purely cosmetic. All cats have the same stats and can have any personality traits.

How do I get a Panda Cat or Golden Spotted Cat?

Check Mrs. Joan's Pet Supplies shop daily. Rare breeds have a lower spawn rate but will appear eventually.

Can I change my cat's personality traits?

No, personality traits are permanent and assigned when the cat is generated. Choose wisely before adopting!
