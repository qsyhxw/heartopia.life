🗺️Where to Find Black Truffles

📍 Exact Location

Forest Island (Northeast)

  • • Located in the northeastern part of the map
  • • North of the East Sea
  • • Accessible from game start

👀 How to Identify

Small Black Dirt Mounds

  • • Appear as subtle dirt mounds on ground
  • • Often near trees, bushes, and logs
  • • Move slowly to spot them easier

💡 Quick Tip

Black Truffles blend into the environment! Slow down when exploring Forest Island and check around trees and bushes carefully.

🔍How to Collect Black Truffles

1️⃣ No Tools Required

Simply interact with the dirt mounds when you see the hand icon. No pickaxe or special tools needed!

2️⃣ Direct Interaction

Walk up to the black dirt mound and press the interact button to dig up the truffle.

3️⃣ Instant Collection

Truffles are added to your inventory immediately after collection.

❓Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I find Black Truffles in Heartopia?

Black Truffles are exclusively found on Forest Island, located in the northeastern part of the map. Look for small dirt mounds near trees, bushes, and logs.

How often do Black Truffles respawn?

Black Truffles respawn approximately every 15 minutes. There are multiple spawn points on Forest Island, but only 4 truffles appear at any given time.

Should I sell Black Truffles raw or cook them?

Always cook them! Raw truffles sell for 99 Gold, but Black Truffle Pie (5-star) sells for 6,640 Gold - 67x more profitable!

What's the most profitable Black Truffle recipe?

Black Truffle Pie is more efficient long-term (requires 1 Wheat), while Black Truffle Cream Pasta has higher sell price (7,200G) but needs 2 Wheat. For best gold per hour, choose Pie!

Do I need tools to collect Black Truffles?

No! You can collect Black Truffles by simply interacting with the dirt mounds. No pickaxe or special tools are required.
