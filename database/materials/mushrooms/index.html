🍄 Heartopia Mushroom Locations

For mushrooms, check Fishing Village for Shiitake routes, Onsen Mountain for Oyster routes, and Forest Island for Black Truffle.

Quick Facts

Shiitake MushroomsFishing Village route
Oyster MushroomsOnsen Mountain route
Black TruffleForest Island route
Best useCooking and recipe profit

Main Mushroom Routes

Use Fishing Village when you need Shiitake Mushrooms and Onsen Mountain when you need Oyster Mushrooms. Bizarre variants can appear as rarer versions, so do not rush to sell them.

Black Truffle

Black Truffle works like a high-value cooking material and has a separate page because its location, recipes and profit path are more specific.

Cooking Uses

Before selling raw mushrooms, compare recipe value through cooking pages. Simple mushroom dishes can be useful early, while truffle recipes can be stronger later.
