LEGENDARY

🌳 1. Roaming Oak Timber

The rarest wood in Heartopia! Roaming Oak Timber can only be obtained by helping Oak-Oak, a magical walking tree that appears daily in different locations.

📊 Daily Limit

3

per day

🔓 Unlock Requirement

DG Level 6

"Astralis in Forest" quest

⏰ Reset Time

6:00 AM

Server time

🔓 How to Unlock Oak-Oak

  1. 1. Reach D.G. Member Level 6 by completing Daily Resident Requests
  2. 2. Check your mailbox for a mysterious letter from Andrew
  3. 3. Travel to the northeastern forest (Spiritual Oak Forest)
  4. 4. Complete the "Astralis in Forest" quest
  5. 5. Meet and free Oak-Oak from tangled roots!

📍 Finding Oak-Oak Daily

Oak-Oak moves to a new random location every day after reset. Here's how to find him:

  • • Check residential areas — Often spawns near player home plots
  • • Look for tangled roots — Oak-Oak gets trapped by roots each morning
  • • Use your scooter — Circle the suburbs quickly
  • • Join Discord — Official #oak-oak-location channel has daily coordinates
  • • Ask neighbors — Other players share the same spawn location
🔨 Crafting Uses: Oak Tree Swing (15 timber), Oak Garden Arch, Oak Planter Box (4 timber), Hydrangea Bushes, Premium outdoor furniture
LEGENDARY

💎 2. Flawless Fluorite

A glowing light-blue crystal used for high-end furniture and lighting. Like Roaming Oak, this material spawns at a single random location daily.

📊 Daily Limit

3

per day

🔓 Unlock Requirement

DG Level 6+

"Astralis in Onsen Mountain" quest

🛠️ Tool Required

Pickaxe

Any tier works

🔓 How to Unlock Flawless Fluorite

  1. 1. Complete the "Astralis in Forest" quest first (Oak-Oak quest)
  2. 2. Follow the story to meet Eric at Onsen Mountain
  3. 3. Complete the "Astralis in Onsen Mountain" quest
  4. 4. You'll mine your first Flawless Fluorite during the quest!

📍 Finding Flawless Fluorite Daily

After the quest, the node spawns in other players' front yards (not your own!):

  • • Look for glowing blue crystals in a tan rock formation
  • • Check suburban ringroad — Ride around player houses
  • • Watch for a mole — A mole with glasses pops out when you break the node!
  • • Join Discord — Daily Fluorite location channel available
  • • Node wiggles when you get close
🔨 Crafting Uses: Lighting furniture (lamps, ceiling lights), Research Room upgrades, Luxury furniture, Premium decorations
EPIC

🪵 3. Rare Timber (Giant Trees)

Rare Timber comes from Giant Trees — massive trees found along the suburban roads. Unlike daily-limited materials, Giant Trees respawn every 2 hours!

🌲 Total Trees

8

in Suburbs area

⏰ Respawn Time

2 Hours

after harvesting

🔓 Unlock

Available

from game start

🌰 Black Walnut

Rarest of the three

Location: Northeast plains, near Plots 4-5

Used for: Luxury furniture

🌲 Red Pine

Medium rarity

Location: Near White Beech, between Plots 4-5

Used for: Garden furniture

🌳 White Beech

Most common of rare trees

Location: Northeast plains cluster

Used for: Light-colored furniture

📍 Giant Tree Locations

8 Giant Trees form an X-shaped pattern around the suburbs:

  • • Near Plot 01, 03, 04, 08, 12 — Along suburban roads
  • • Northeast plains — 5 trees in a cluster (Black Walnut, Red Pine, White Beech)
  • • How to spot: Much taller than normal trees, thick trunks
  • • Tip: Drive the suburban loop road to find them all
⚠️ Note: Rare Timber is NOT guaranteed every harvest — sometimes you'll only get regular wood. Keep trying!

🍄 4. Black Truffle & Rare Mushrooms

Black Truffle is the most valuable mushroom in Heartopia, found only on Forest Island. Unlike other mushrooms, you dig truffles from dirt mounds by hand!

🥇 Black Truffle

📍 Location: Forest Island (northeast of map)

🔍 Appearance: Small dark dirt mounds

🛠️ Tool: None! Dig by hand

⏰ Respawn: ~10-20 minutes

💰 Sell Price: 99 Gold each

⚡ Energy: +25 when eaten

📊 Total Spawns: ~14 on Forest Island

🍳 Recipes: Black Truffle Pie, Black Truffle Cream (high-value cooking!)

🍄 Matsutake (Ultra Rare)

Location: Spirit Oak Pine Forest / Jump Puzzle area

Status: Extremely rare, many players never find one

Note: May require higher DG level to spawn

🌈 Bizarre Mushrooms

Types: Red, Green, Blue colored variants

Status: Collection items only (can't eat)

Note: Found in same areas as regular mushrooms

Mushroom Location Notes
Shiitake Fishing Village Near trees/signs, very common
Oyster Onsen Mountain Near rock formations
Penny Bun Forest Near pine trees/fallen logs
Button Flower Field Near fences, easy to miss
⭐ Black Truffle Forest Island Dig dark mounds, 99g each!

Some rare materials only appear during special weather events or limited-time events.

  • 📍 Location: Onsen Mountain
  • 🌧️ Weather: Meteor Shower only
  • 📊 Total Nodes: 9 locations
  • 🎖️ Badge: Collect 60 for "Stardust Collector"
  • 🎁 Uses: Special furniture & emotes

🌧️ Doris Shop Items

  • 📍 Location: Art Street (rain), Rainbow base
  • 🌧️ Weather: Rain, Snow, Rainbow, Meteor
  • 🛒 Sells: Exclusive recipes & furniture
  • 🌈 Rainbow Tip: Appears at 6 PM after rain
