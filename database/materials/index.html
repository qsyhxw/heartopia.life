Quick Answer

Rare Timber: check large, thick trees along the outer roads around residential plots. It is a separate item from Roaming Oak Timber, so confirm the exact material name in your recipe.

Fluorite: follow the Onsen Mountain and Fluorite Mine route, then check nearby Crater Lake paths when your crafting list asks for mineral materials.

Mushrooms: use Fishing Village for Shiitake routes, Onsen Mountain for Oyster routes, and the Black Truffle page for the high-value Forest Island item.

Highland Poppy: use the existing Highland Poppy guide and flower crossbreeding guide for flower-material routing.

Keep, Cook, or Sell?

Keep First

Roaming Oak Timber, rare timber variants, Fluorite and special flower materials are safer to keep until your workbench and house plans are stable.

Cook First

Regular mushrooms and Black Truffle often become more useful inside recipes than as raw materials.

Sell Extras

Only sell extras after you have checked recipe profit, crafting needs and current request boards.

FAQ

What does codestopia mushroom mean?

Most players using that wording are looking for Heartopia mushroom locations, code rewards, or cooking ingredients. Start with the mushroom page, then check codes if you are hunting material rewards.

Where should I start each day?

Check Roaming Oak Timber first, then run the Fluorite Mine route, then gather mushrooms or Black Truffle depending on your cooking plan.

Is Black Truffle part of the mushroom route?

Yes for cooking and material planning, but it has a different Forest Island route and a dedicated page because players search for it separately.
