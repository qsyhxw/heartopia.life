🌳 Heartopia Roaming Oak Timber

Roaming Oak Timber and Rare Timber are separate materials. Follow Roaming Oak routes for this item, check home-lot clues, and keep any code-reward timber until your crafting list is clear.

Quick Facts

Best search targetRoaming Oak / Roaming Oak Timber
RouteRoaming Oak, home plot check, forest path
Best useCrafting, building and request prep
Keep or sellKeep first; sell only extras

How to Get It

Check Roaming Oak related routes after reset, then confirm whether any active redeem code includes Roaming Oak Timber. Because it is tied to rare crafting needs, treat every piece as a keeper until you know your next build.

Daily Route

Start from your home plot, check Roaming Oak clues, then continue through forest gathering paths before doing normal wood routes. This keeps rare timber from being forgotten during daily chores.

Use Notes

Use it for workbench and building needs before selling. If you are planning a house rebuild, keep a reserve so a later recipe does not stop your progress.
