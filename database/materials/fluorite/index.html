💎 Heartopia Fluorite Location

For Fluorite, use the Onsen Mountain and Fluorite Mine route first, then sweep the nearby Crater Lake path before you leave the area.

Quick Facts

Material typeMineral
Best routeFluorite Mine / Onsen Mountain
Nearby checkCrater Lake path
Best useCrafting and special material requests

Where to Look

Start at Onsen Mountain and follow the Fluorite Mine route. If your route passes Crater Lake, check nearby mineral spots before moving to another region.

Fluorite vs Flawless Fluorite

Regular Fluorite is useful for common crafting needs. Flawless Fluorite is safer to keep because higher-tier requests and special crafting checks can ask for rarer mineral variants.

Route Tip

Pair Fluorite with mushroom and animal-trough routes around Onsen Mountain so the trip covers more than one material type.
