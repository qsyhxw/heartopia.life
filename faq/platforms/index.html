🎮 Is Heartopia on Nintendo Switch?

Everything you need to know about Heartopia platforms: Switch, Steam, iOS, Android, cross-play, and console release plans!

⚡ Quick Answer

No, Heartopia is NOT on Nintendo Switch. It's currently available on Steam, iOS, and Android. Console release is possible in the future but not announced. Mac players can check the Mac download status page.

🕹️ Will Heartopia come to PlayStation or Xbox? ▼

⚠️ No Announced Plans

There are currently no official plans for PlayStation or Xbox releases.

Reason: The game currently lacks controller support, which would be required for console versions.

🎛️ Does Heartopia support controllers? ▼

❌ No Controller Support (Yet)

Heartopia currently does not support game controllers. The game is designed for mouse/keyboard on PC and touch controls on mobile.
