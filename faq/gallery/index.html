❓ How to View Gallery in Heartopia?

Frequently asked questions about gallery, photo album, Drawing Board, and everything related to viewing and saving your Heartopia creations!

⚡ Quick Answer

To view gallery in Heartopia: Open your Smartwatch → Select "Album" for photos, OR interact with the Drawing Board for custom designs.

📱 How do I view the gallery in Heartopia? ▼

There are two galleries in Heartopia:

📷 Photo Album

  1. 1. Open your Smartwatch
  2. 2. Select "Album"
  3. 3. Browse your photos!

🎨 Design Gallery

  1. 1. Find your Drawing Board
  2. 2. Interact with it
  3. 3. Click the image icon
📍 Where is the photo album in Heartopia? ▼

The photo album is located in your Smartwatch:

  1. 1 Look for the watch icon in the top-right corner of your screen
  2. 2 Open the watch menu
  3. 3 Select "Album" to view all your photos
📸 How do I take photos in Heartopia? ▼
  1. 1 Open your Quick Tools Menu
  2. 2 Select the Camera tool
  3. 3 Use the grid to align your shot
  4. 4 Press the shutter button to capture
💡 Tip: Take at least one photo daily to advance your album progress!
💾 How do I download photos to my PC? ▼
  1. 1 Open your Photo Album via Smartwatch
  2. 2 Select the photo you want to download
  3. 3 Click "Download" or "Save to System"
  4. 4 Find the photo in your Downloads folder
⚠️ Note: Photos are encrypted in game files. You MUST use the in-game download feature!
🎨 How do I unlock the Drawing Board? ▼

💰

🏪
Ka Ching
Where to buy
  1. 1. Reach Developer Level 14
  2. 2. Visit Dorothee at the Clothing Store
  3. 3. Accept "Jean Morin's Letter" quest
  4. 4. Follow the quest to Ka Ching
  5. 5. Purchase the Drawing Board for 2,000 Gold
🖌️ What is Brushstroke Whimsy quest? ▼

Brushstroke Whimsy is the quest that unlocks the painting system.

❓ Stuck on "View Gallery"?

Many players get confused here! Here's the solution:

  1. 1. Place the Drawing Board on the ground
  2. 2. Interact with the Drawing Board
  3. 3. Click the gallery/image icon
  4. 4. Quest complete! ✅
🐦 How do I use the Bird Scanner for photos? ▼

The Bird Scanner is a special camera for birdwatching:

  1. 1 Open Tools Button (bottom-right)
  2. 2 Equip the Bird Scanner
  3. 3 Use the slider to zoom in/out
  4. 4 Capture birds to earn money!
👗 Can I create custom clothes with my designs? ▼

Yes! After unlocking the Drawing Board, you can create custom clothing:

Requirements:

  • • Tailoring Ticket: 80 Moonlight Crystals
  • • A completed custom design
  • • Compatible clothing item
❌ Why can't I find the Gallery option? ▼

Common reasons and solutions:

Drawing Board not placed

You must place the Drawing Board on the ground, not access it from inventory.

Not at DG Level 14

Check your Developer Level in the profile menu.

Quest not started

Visit Dorothee first to start "Jean Morin's Letter" quest.
