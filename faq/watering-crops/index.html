🌱 How to Water Crops in Heartopia?

Frequently asked questions about watering crops, gardening, planting, and everything you need to know to grow the best harvest!

⚡ Quick Answer

To water crops: Equip your Watering Can from Tools → Tap on planted crops → At Gardening Level 3, hold to water multiple plants!

💧 How do I water crops in Heartopia? ▼
  1. 1 Open your Tools Menu
  2. 2 Equip the Watering Can 🪴
  3. 3 Tap/Click on your planted crops
  4. 4 Look for the sparkle effect = watered!
💡 Pro Tip: At Gardening Level 3, hold down the watering button to water multiple plants at once!
📍 Where do I get the watering can? ▼
👩‍🌾
Visit Blanc
Gardening Shop on Garden Street

When you start the Gardening Hobby, Blanc provides you with a free Watering Can. Talk to her to unlock gardening and get your tools!

🌾 Do crops need water to grow? ▼
⚠️
Without Water
Crops still grow, but lower quality
✨
With Water
Higher quality = more gold!

Bottom line: Always water your crops for better quality and higher profits!

🔄 How do I water multiple plants at once? ▼
🎯
Requires: Gardening Level 3
Unlocks multi-water ability
  1. 1. Reach Gardening Level 3
  2. 2. Equip your Watering Can
  3. 3. Hold down the watering button (don't tap)
  4. 4. Watch multiple plants get watered simultaneously!
👀 How do I know if crops are watered? ▼

Look for these visual cues:

  • ✨ Sparkle effect on the plant
  • 🟤 Darker soil (appears moist)
🐛 Bug Fix: If watering doesn't register, pick up the planter with your crop and place it again to reset the "wet" status.
⏱️ How long do crops take to grow? ▼
Crop Grow Time
🍅 Tomato ~15 minutes
🥬 Lettuce ~8 hours
🥔 Potato Varies by level
🌾 Wheat Unlocks later
💡 Tip: Use Growth Booster from Blanc to speed up growth!
📦 Where do I get plant boxes? ▼

Get plant boxes (planters) from Blanc's Gardening Shop:

  • 📍 Location: Garden Street
  • 📦 Initial limit: few planters
  • 📈 More unlocked via Agronomy Expert skill

🧴 Fertilizer

Improves crop quality

Buy from Blanc

⚡ Growth Booster

Reduces grow time

Buy from Blanc

🌸 How do I water flowers for crossbreeding? ▼

Flowers work differently from crops:

  1. 1 Plant flowers in bare soil (not planters)
  2. 2 Plant them close together
  3. 3 Water consistently to encourage crossbreeding
  4. 4 Friends can also water your flowers!
🌈 Result: Crossbreeding creates new flower colors!
🌿 How do I remove weeds? ▼

Weeds appear around your crops over time:

  • 👆 Tap/Click on weeds to remove them
  • ✨ Removing weeds improves crop quality
  • 📈 Also gives Gardening Proficiency Points
