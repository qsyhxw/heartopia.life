🏠 How to Buy Plot in Heartopia?

Everything about land expansion, plot costs, DG level requirements, and step-by-step instructions to grow your home!

⚡ Quick Answer

To buy a plot: Open Watch → D.G. Member's Guild app → Plots tab (4 squares) → Select plot with shaking lock → Pay Gold!

🏠 How do I buy a plot in Heartopia? ▼
  1. 1 Open your Watch Menu ⌚
  2. 2 Go to D.G. Member's Guild app
  3. 3 Tap the Plots tab (four squares icon) 🔲
  4. 4 Find a plot with a shaking lock 🔓
  5. 5 Tap it and pay the Gold cost 💰
💡 Tip: A stationary lock = you don't meet the DG level requirement yet. A shaking lock = ready to purchase!

🏅 How do I get Contribution Medals? ▼

Contribution Medals level up your DG rank:

  1. 1 Find NPCs with quest icons around town
  2. 2 Complete their Resident Requests
  3. 3 Earn Contribution Medals as rewards
⚠️ Daily Limit: Max 5 requests per day. They reset daily!
