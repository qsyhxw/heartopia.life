💇 How to Change Hair in Heartopia?

Frequently asked questions about hairstyles, Salon, hair color, and everything you need to know to customize your character's appearance!

⚡ Quick Answer

To change hair: Go to Dorothee's Clothing Store → Head upstairs to the Salon → Interact with a mirror → Choose hairstyle & color!

✂️ How do I change my hair in Heartopia? ▼
  1. 1 Go to Dorothee's Clothing Store in town center
  2. 2 Head upstairs to the second floor
  3. 3 Find the Salon area with mirrors
  4. 4 Interact with a mirror 🪞
  5. 5 Choose your hairstyle and click Purchase
💰 Cost: Hairstyles cost around 2,000 Gold to equip!
📍 Where is the Salon in Heartopia? ▼
👗
Dorothee's Clothing Store
2nd Floor - Town Center

Look for:

  • • Two chairs with mirrors 🪞
  • • Mirror icon appears when you approach
  • • Interact to open customization menu
🎨 How do I change hair color? ▼
  1. 1 Open the Salon menu (interact with mirror)
  2. 2 Select the hairstyle you want
  3. 3 Click the paint dropper icon 🎨 on the left
  4. 4 Choose your new hair color
  5. 5 Click Purchase to apply!
💡 Note: Some hairstyles offer more color options than others, including gradients!
⏰ When can I change my hair? ▼

⚠️ Not Available Immediately!

You can't change your hair right at the start of the game.

Requirements:

  • • Play for about ~1 hour
  • • Receive a letter from Dorothee
  • • The letter confirms styling services are available

👗 How do I change clothes? ▼

Clothes are changed separately from hair:

🏠 At Home
Use your Wardrobe furniture
📱 On the Go
Open Watch → Dress-Up feature
❓ Do I need my own mirror at home? ▼

✅ No! The Salon is always available

You don't need to craft or buy your own mirror furniture. Just visit Dorothee's Clothing Store anytime to change your appearance!

📋 Hair Change Quick Reference

Step What to Do
1. Location Dorothee's Clothing Store → 2nd Floor
2. Interact Approach mirror → Tap mirror icon
3. Choose Style Browse hairstyles → Select one
4. Choose Color Click paint dropper → Pick color
5. Apply Click Purchase → Done!
