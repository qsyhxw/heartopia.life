💇 How to Change Hair in Heartopia

Go to Dorothee's Clothing Store, use the upstairs Salon mirror, then choose a hairstyle, hair color, and other appearance settings.

⚡ Quick Answer

To change hair in Heartopia: Go to Dorothee's Clothing Store in town center → head upstairs to the Salon → interact with a mirror → choose hairstyle or hair color → click Purchase to apply.

Exact Steps: Change Hair or Hair Color

Question Answer
How to change hair in Heartopia? Dorothee's Clothing Store → upstairs Salon → mirror → choose hairstyle → check cost → Purchase.
How to change hair color? Open the same Salon mirror, select a hairstyle, use the paint dropper or color picker, then confirm.
Where is the Salon? Second floor of Dorothee's Clothing Store in town center.
Cost / requirement Basic styles are commonly shown around 2,000 Gold; some styles require DG progress, Wishing Stars, gacha, or events.
✂️ How do I change my hair in Heartopia? ▼
  1. 1 Go to Dorothee's Clothing Store in town center
  2. 2 Head upstairs to the second floor
  3. 3 Find the Salon area with mirrors
  4. 4 Interact with a mirror 🪞
  5. 5 Choose your hairstyle and click Purchase
💰 Cost: Basic hairstyle changes are commonly shown around 2,000 Gold, while premium or event styles can use Wishing Stars, gacha, or event rewards. Check the price shown in the Salon before confirming.
📍 Where is the Salon in Heartopia? ▼
👗
Dorothee's Clothing Store
2nd Floor - Town Center

Look for:

  • • Two chairs with mirrors 🪞
  • • Mirror icon appears when you approach
  • • Interact to open customization menu
🎨 How do I change hair color? ▼
  1. 1 Open the Salon menu (interact with mirror)
  2. 2 Select the hairstyle you want
  3. 3 Click the paint dropper icon 🎨 on the left
  4. 4 Choose your new hair color
  5. 5 Click Purchase to apply!
💡 Note: Some hairstyles offer more color options than others, including gradients!
⏰ When can I change my hair? ▼

⚠️ Not Available Immediately!

You usually cannot change your hair right at the start of the game.

Requirements:

  • • Progress through the early tutorial, often about ~1 hour
  • • Receive a letter from Dorothee
  • • The letter confirms styling services are available

❓ Do I need my own mirror at home? ▼

✅ No! The Salon is always available

You don't need to craft or buy your own mirror furniture. Just visit Dorothee's Clothing Store anytime to change your appearance!
