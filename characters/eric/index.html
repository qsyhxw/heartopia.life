Eric Character Profile

Eric is a recurring NPC in Heartopia. Unlike traditional farming simulator characters who run shops behind counters, Eric is an active town resident who is heavily involved in various storytelling quests and seasonal events.

⚠️

Stop Looking for a Fixed Schedule

Many guides online try to provide a "24-hour daily route" for Eric. This is incorrect and will waste your time. Eric features a dynamic wandering AI instead of standing in one specific spot. The only reliable way to find him is to use the Smartwatch tracking method or check his specific quest locations!

📍 Known Locations & How to Find Him

Scripted Quest Locations

Depending on your current story progress or active seasonal events, Eric is scripted to appear at the following specific areas:

  • 🏕️
    Adventure Park

    Eric can be found here specifically during the "Astralis in Onsen Mountain" questline.

  • ⛸️
    Onsen Mountain North Park

    He oversees or participates in the ice skating event located in the snowy northern park.

⌚

The Smartwatch Tracking Method

Because of his wandering AI, trying to memorize a route is inefficient. The absolute best way to find Eric is to track him digitally:

  1. Open your in-game map using your Smartwatch.
  2. If you have an active quest with Eric, look for a glowing exclamation mark (!) on the map.
  3. Select his portrait from the NPC tracker menu to enable a real-time directional waypoint on your mini-map. Just follow the arrows!

🤝 Friendship, Gifts & Quests

Daily Gifting System

Like most residents in Heartopia, you can build a relationship with Eric by talking to him and giving him daily gifts. Increasing your friendship level unlocks unique dialogue, mail rewards, and potentially exclusive blueprints.

  • Daily Limit 1 Gift Per Day
  • Loved Gifts +15 to +20 points
  • Liked Gifts +5 to +10 points
  • Disliked Gifts (Trash, Weeds) Negative Points
🕵️ Community Bounty: Have you discovered Eric's exact favorite gift? Let us know so we can update the wiki!

Notable Quests

If you are aiming for 100% completion in Heartopia, expect to interact heavily with Eric during these main quests and seasonal features:

  • Gossip Special Security Blanket Tip: You must acquire the musical instruments from NPC Annie first before Eric will talk to you.
  • Astralis in Onsen Mountain
  • Enjoy Your Visit
  • Winter Prep (Event) During this event, Eric will actually call your device to progress the story.
