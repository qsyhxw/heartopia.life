�?/span> Frequently Asked Questions

How many characters are in Heartopia?

There are 15+ named NPCs including hobby mentors, shopkeepers, story characters, and event-specific characters. More may be added in future updates.

How do I increase friendship with NPCs?

Give gifts daily (one per NPC, best in morning hours), choose positive dialogue options, complete their quests, and give birthday gifts for 3x bonus!

When are NPCs available?

Most NPCs follow daily schedules. Shopkeepers are at their stores 6 AM - 12 PM. Some move around in afternoons. Most sleep 10 PM - 6 AM.

What are the benefits of Best Friend status?

Best Friend unlocks 30% shop discounts, exclusive quests, rare items, hobby expansion tickets, and advanced techniques from hobby mentors!
