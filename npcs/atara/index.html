📍 Where to Find Atara

🏔️ Primary Location: Onsen Mountain Igloo

Atara is a story NPC who can be found inside the igloo on Onsen Mountain in Heartopia. Unlike roaming villagers, she stays in a fixed location within the igloo structure.

To reach her, travel to Onsen Mountain and look for the igloo. Enter the igloo to find Atara inside. She plays a key role in both the Astralis storyline and the Winter Frost Season event.

💡 Tip: You need D.G. Level 7 and completed main story tutorial quests to interact with Atara for the Onsen Egg Promise quest chain. Make sure you've met these requirements before visiting.

🗺️ How to Get There

1

Travel to Onsen Mountain

Open your map (M key) and head to the Onsen Mountain area.

2

Find the Igloo

Look for the igloo structure on the mountain. It's a distinctive building that stands out in the snowy landscape.

3

Enter and Talk to Atara

Go inside the igloo to find Atara. Interact with her to trigger dialogue and quest events.

📖 Onsen Egg Promise Quest Guide

🥚 About the Onsen Egg Promise

The Onsen Egg Promise is a quest chain available during the Winter Frost Season event. Atara is a key NPC in the trigger chain — you must speak to her inside the igloo to unlock the 16-day egg hunt. This quest involves multiple NPCs and requires specific prerequisites.

📋 Prerequisites

🔓 D.G. Level 7

You must reach D.G. Membership Level 7 before this quest becomes available

📖 Tutorial Complete

All main story tutorial quests must be finished first

🔗 Quest Trigger Chain (Step by Step)

1

Speak to Erik

Find and talk to Erik first. He will point you toward Atara and the Onsen Mountain area.

2

Interact with Atara in the Igloo

Travel to Onsen Mountain, enter the igloo, and speak to Atara. This interaction is required to advance the quest chain.

3

Find Naughty

After speaking to Atara, locate Naughty to officially start the 16-day egg hunt event.

4

Begin the 16-Day Egg Hunt

Once all three NPCs have been spoken to, the Onsen Egg Promise egg hunt begins. Collect eggs over 16 days across various locations.

💡 Day 7 Egg: One of the eggs in the hunt can be found near Atara's location at Onsen Mountain. Check around the igloo area on Day 7 of the event.

❄️ Atara in the Winter Frost Season

Atara plays a significant role during the Winter Frost Season event in Heartopia. This seasonal event introduces the Onsen Egg Promise quest chain and various winter-themed activities around Onsen Mountain.

❄️ Atara's Event Roles

🥚 Egg Hunt Trigger

Atara is the second NPC in the Onsen Egg Promise trigger chain. Speaking to her inside the igloo is required to unlock the 16-day egg hunt.

⭐ Astralis Storyline

Atara is a key character in the Astralis storyline that takes place around Onsen Mountain. Her dialogue reveals important lore during the winter event.

💡 Event Tip: During the Winter Frost Season, make sure to visit Atara early in the event period. The 16-day egg hunt has a time limit, so starting the trigger chain as soon as possible gives you the most time to collect all eggs.
