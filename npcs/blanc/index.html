Quick Answer

If you searched blanc heartopia, you probably need the Gardening Shop. Blanc is the gardening mentor and shop NPC. Use your in-game map to locate his face icon, then visit him for seeds, plant boxes, fertilizer, and gardening progression.

Blanc Location

Blanc is associated with the Gardening Shop. If you are in the early tutorial and cannot find him, open the map and hover NPC face icons until Blanc appears.

Tip: Player reports often describe Blanc as the NPC to visit for tomato seeds and garden plots. Put garden plots outside or in a place where planting is allowed before trying to grow crops.

Gardening Shop

Blanc's shop supports the gardening loop. Visit him when you need supplies for crops, flower growing, or garden upgrades.

Common shop needs

  • • Seeds
  • • Plant boxes or garden plots
  • • Fertilizer
  • • Growth and planting supplies

Progression note

More seed choices may appear as your Gardening passion level increases. If an item is missing, continue the gardening tutorial and raise the hobby level.

Blanc Gift Tips

Blanc's exact favorite gift list is not stable enough to publish as a fixed table. Use the Friendship Journal first, then test garden-themed items if you want to build friendship.

Try first

Flowers, crops, fruit, garden dishes.

Neutral fallback

Home-cooked meals and common liked items.

Avoid

Trash, rotten food, and items the journal marks as disliked.

Quests and Related Guides

Blanc is mainly important for starting and maintaining gardening. If a quest asks you to plant crops, buy seeds, or place a garden plot, check Blanc's shop before searching elsewhere.

Blanc FAQ

Where is Blanc in Heartopia?▼

Blanc is the Gardening Mentor. Look for him at the Gardening Shop / Gardening Store area, or track him from the in-game map NPC icons.

What does Blanc sell?▼

Blanc sells gardening supplies such as seeds, plant boxes, fertilizer, and related farming items. Some options depend on your Gardening progress.

How do I unlock gardening with Blanc?▼

Follow the early tutorial route until the game points you toward Blanc. He is the NPC tied to the gardening hobby and first planting steps.

What gifts should I give Blanc?▼

Use the Friendship Journal as the final check. Garden-themed items such as flowers, crops, fruit, and cooked garden dishes are sensible first tests.
