📍 Where to Find Dorothee

🏪 Primary Location: Morin's Clothing Store

Dorothee runs Morin's Clothing Store on Residential Street in the Town Square area. She's a permanent shopkeeper NPC — you'll always find her at her store during business hours.

The store has two floors: the ground floor is the clothing shop with dressing rooms in the back left corner, and the second floor houses the hair salon (unlocked via quest).

💡 Tip: If you can't find the store, open your map (M key) and look for the clothing icon in the Town Square area. The store is on Residential Street — you can also use the Track feature on Dorothee's portrait.

🏠 Store Layout

👗 Ground Floor — Clothing Shop

Browse and buy clothes. Dressing rooms in the back left corner for trying on outfits.

✂️ Second Floor — Hair Salon

Head upstairs to find styling chairs and mirrors. Change hair, eyes, and skin tone here.

📖 Dorothee's Quest Guide

Dorothee is involved in several key quests that unlock Heartopia's entire appearance customization system. Complete these in order to access clothing, hair, and outfit features.

👗 Quest 1: My New Clothes

This early-game quest unlocks the ability to buy and change clothes. It's triggered by a flyer you receive during the tutorial.

1

Receive the clothing flyer during early story progression

2

Take the flyer to Dorothee's clothing shop in Town Square

3

Help Dorothee unpack her new stock

✓

Reward: Clothing shop unlocked — you can now buy and change clothes

✂️ Quest 2: Dorothee's Resident Request

Completing Dorothee's Resident Request unlocks the hair salon on the second floor, allowing you to change hair, eyes, and skin tone.

✓

Reward: Hair salon unlocked — change hairstyle, hair color, eye color, and skin tone on the 2nd floor

👔 Quest 3: Custom Closets

Later in the story, Dorothee teaches you about Custom Closets — the outfit preset system that lets you save and switch between outfits.

✓

Reward: Custom Closet feature unlocked — save outfit presets for different occasions

💡 Outfit Presets: To fully unlock Outfit Presets, you also need 800 Clothing Collection Points. You earn these by collecting clothing items — buy from Dorothee, find Prize Bubbles, and complete quests.

✂️ Hair Salon & Appearance Changes

After completing Dorothee's Resident Request, the second floor of her store becomes a full hair salon where you can customize your character's appearance.

💇 What You Can Change

💇 Hairstyles

Browse and purchase new hairstyles. Some cost Gold, others Wishing Stars. Free options available too.

🎨 Hair Color

Change your hair color from a wide palette of options at the salon mirrors.

👁️ Eye Color

Customize your eye color and style to match your look.

🧑 Skin Tone

Adjust your character's skin tone at any time from the salon.

💡 How to Access: Head to Dorothee's store, go up the staircase to the second floor, and find the mirrors behind the styling chairs. Interact with a mirror to open the appearance menu.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Dorothee in Heartopia?

Dorothee runs the clothing shop (Morin's Clothing Store) on Residential Street in the Town Square area. She sells tops, bottoms, full suits, shoes, hats, and accessories.

How do I unlock clothing customization?

Complete the "My New Clothes" quest. You'll receive a flyer early in the game — take it to Dorothee's shop and help her unpack. This unlocks the ability to buy and change clothes.

How do I change my hair and eyes in Heartopia?

Complete Dorothee's Resident Request quest. This unlocks the hair salon on the second floor of her store. Head upstairs, find the mirrors behind the styling chairs, and you can change hairstyle, hair color, eye color, and skin tone.

What does Dorothee sell?

Everything clothing-related: tops, bottoms, full suits, socks, shoes, hats, and accessories including face decor, earrings, backpacks, bags, and umbrellas. Her stock rotates daily, so check back often.
