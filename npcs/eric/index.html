📍 Where to Find Eric

🏔️ Primary Location: Onsen Mountain Park

During the Winter Frost event (January 31 - March 13, 2026), Eric can be found at Onsen Mountain Park where he's coordinating the winter festival setup.

He's often near the ice water pumps and construction areas for the winter activities.

📅 Best Times to Visit

✅ Morning (6 AM - 12 PM)

Best time for gifting - NPCs are most receptive

⚡ Afternoon (12 PM - 6 PM)

Good for quest turn-ins and conversations

📋 Eric's Quests

🧊 Ice Maker Setup

Winter Frost

Help Eric set up the ice water pumps for the winter festival. This is one of the first tasks in the Winter Frost event chain.

Reward: Winter materials, Event progress

📝 Resident Feedback Collection

Winter Frost

Collect 9 stamps of approval from various residents to help Eric with the festival planning.

Reward: Event progress, NPC interactions

🥚 Onsen Egg Promise

Winter Frost

Eric plans a special winter gift for the park. This quest leads to an egg hunt organized by Naughty!

Reward: Access to Egg Hunt event

✨ Astralis in Onsen Mountain

Story Quest

A special story quest that involves the mystery of Astralis on the mountain.

Reward: 🎵 Recorder (Music Instrument)
