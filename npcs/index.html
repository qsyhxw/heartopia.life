⚠️ Warning

Giving disliked items damages relationships! Always check NPC preferences before gifting.

💡 Best Gift Sources

  • 🍳 Home-cooked meals — most NPCs appreciate food
  • 🌸 Freshly grown flowers — great for many residents
  • 🐟 Caught fish — Vanya especially loves these
  • 🥚 Animal products — easy to get, widely liked

💕 Friendship System

Daily Gifting Rules

  • 📅 1 gift per NPC per day — resets at midnight in-game
  • ⏰ Best time: 6 AM - 12 PM (morning) when NPCs are most receptive
  • 🔄 Variety matters: Rotate between 3-4 favorite gifts to avoid diminishing returns
  • 🎂 Birthdays & Events: Triple friendship gains with perfect gifts!
