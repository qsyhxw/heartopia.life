📍 Where to Find Will

🗼 Primary Location: Lighthouse Area

Will is a resident NPC who can be found near the Lighthouse in Heartopia. He's one of the town's villagers who roams around the coastal zone near the lighthouse.

Like other Heartopia NPCs, Will may move around during different times of day. Use your map (M key) to track his current position.

💡 Tip: If you can't find Will, open your map and look for his icon near the Lighthouse. You can click on his portrait and select "Track" to see an astral guide leading you to him.

📅 Best Times to Visit

✅ Morning (6 AM – 12 PM)

Best time for gifting — NPCs are most receptive in the morning

⚡ Afternoon (12 PM – 6 PM)

Good for conversations and quest interactions

🔍 How to Find Will (Step by Step)

1

Open Your Map

Press M on keyboard or tap the map icon on mobile to open the full map view.

2

Look Near the Lighthouse

Will's icon should appear in the Lighthouse area on the coast. NPC icons show their current real-time position.

3

Use the Track Feature

Click Will's portrait on the map and select "Track". An astral guide (glowing trail) will lead you directly to him.

4

Talk to Will

Approach Will and interact. You can chat, give gifts, or check available quests.

⚠️ Can't find Will? He may not be unlocked yet. Will's unlock condition appears to be tied to main story progression. Keep completing story quests and raising your DG Membership level.
