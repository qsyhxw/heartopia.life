✨ Doris - The Secret Weather Merchant

Doris is Heartopia's most elusive traveling merchant, appearing only during special weather events. She sells exclusive items you can't find anywhere else, including the coveted Roll Cake recipe!

Weather-Dependent

📍Where to Find Doris

Doris's location changes based on the weather. Check your in-game watch for weather forecasts!

🌧️

Rain & Snow

📍 Location:

Art Street (Main Town)

⏰ When:

During active rain or snowfall

💡 Tip: Art Street is in the main town area. Look for Doris near the street shops when it's raining or snowing!
🌈

Rainbow

📍 Location:

At the rainbow's base

🗺️ Common Areas:

Onsen Mountain, Forest regions

💡 Tip: Rainbows appear after rain clears. Look for the rainbow in the sky and head to its base - Doris will be there!
☄️

Meteor Shower

📍 Location:

Near meteor landing sites

⏰ Time:

6 PM - 12 AM (Midnight)

⭐ Special Currency: During meteor showers, Doris accepts Star Fragments instead of Gold or Wishing Stars. Collect them from meteor impact areas!
💡 Tip: Onsen Mountain is the most common meteor landing area. Head there when you see meteors falling!

❓Frequently Asked Questions

How often does Doris appear?

Doris appears whenever the specific weather events occur (rain, snow, rainbow, or meteor shower). Weather is dynamic in Heartopia, so check your watch regularly for forecasts!

What if I miss a meteor shower?

Meteor showers occur periodically in the game. If you miss one, keep checking your weather forecast - another will come! Save your Star Fragments for the next event.

Is the Roll Cake recipe worth 50 Wishing Stars?

Absolutely! It's the only way to get this recipe, and Roll Cakes restore 35 Energy, making them excellent for exploration. Plus, you can make colorful variants with Colored Sugars.

Can I find Doris in the same spot every time?

Doris's exact location within an area may vary slightly, but she'll always be in the general area for that weather type (Art Street for rain/snow, rainbow base for rainbows, meteor sites for meteor showers).

What do I do with Star Fragments?

Star Fragments are collected from meteor impact areas during meteor showers. Save them to trade with Doris for exclusive Meteorite Furniture during the same event!
