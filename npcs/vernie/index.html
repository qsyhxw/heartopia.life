📍 Where to Find Vernie

🌸 Primary Location: Flower Fields

Vernie is a story NPC who can be found in the Flower Fields area of Heartopia. She plays a key role in the Astralis storyline and is your gateway to unlocking the music system.

Unlike regular villagers, Vernie appears primarily during the Astralis in Flower Field quest. You'll meet her as part of the main story progression.

💡 Tip: If you can't find Vernie, make sure you've progressed far enough in the main story. She appears when the Astralis in Flower Field quest becomes available. Check your quest log for any Astralis-related objectives.

🗺️ How to Get to Flower Fields

🚌 By Bus

Use the bus system to travel to the Flower Fields stop on your map

🗺️ By Map

Press M to open the map and look for the Flower Fields area — follow the quest marker

📜 Astralis in Flower Field Quest Guide

The Astralis in Flower Field quest is a main story quest where you meet Vernie and receive your first musical instrument. This quest is essential for unlocking the entire music system in Heartopia.

📋 Quest Steps

1

Travel to Flower Fields

Head to the Flower Fields area when the quest becomes available in your quest log.

2

Meet Vernie

Find and talk to Vernie in the Flower Fields. She will give you your first instrument — a Lute.

3

Find Two Teenagers

Vernie asks you to find two teenagers nearby. Start with the passionate girl first.

4

Play Music for the Passionate Girl

Hold the instrument from your backpack and play any random tune for her to continue the quest.

5

Complete the Quest

Find the second teenager and finish the remaining objectives. The Instrument and Music Score menu will unlock upon completion.

✅ Quest Reward: Completing this quest gives you the Lute instrument and unlocks the full music system. After finishing, Ka Ching will call you with a follow-up quest called "New Song to Summon the Astralis".

🎵 Music System Unlock

Vernie is the key to unlocking Heartopia's music system. Here's what opens up after meeting her:

🎸

Lute (First Instrument)

Your starter instrument, gifted directly by Vernie during the quest. Can be played anywhere.

🎼

Music Score Menu

Unlocks the Instrument and Music Score menu, letting you collect and play music scores.

📞

Ka Ching Follow-Up

After the quest, Ka Ching calls with "New Song to Summon the Astralis" — providing music score numbers.

🎹

More Instruments

After unlocking music, buy additional instruments from Annie using gold.

💡 Tip: If you accidentally sell your Lute, you can buy another one from Annie. There's no separate quest required — just visit her shop and purchase it with gold.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Vernie in Heartopia?

Vernie can be found in the Flower Fields area. She appears during the Astralis in Flower Field quest as part of the main story progression.

What does Vernie give you in Heartopia?

Vernie gives you your first instrument — a Lute — during the Astralis in Flower Field quest. This unlocks the Instrument and Music Score menu in your game.

How do I unlock the music system in Heartopia?

Complete the Astralis in Flower Field quest by meeting Vernie. She gives you a Lute, which unlocks the music system. After that, Ka Ching will call with a follow-up quest called "New Song to Summon the Astralis."

What are Vernie's favorite gifts?

Vernie's specific gift preferences have not yet been documented by the community. She appears primarily as a story NPC. Try flowers or music-related items as thematic choices.

I lost my Lute — can I get another one?

Yes! If you sell or lose your Lute, visit Annie and buy another one with gold. No extra quest is needed.
