🏪 Vanya's Fishing Shop

Located on Garden Street North. Open 24/7 (Interact with shelves).

Item Cost Note
Fishing Rod Repair Kit 200 G Essential!
Common Bait 50 G Reduces wait time
Fish Attractor 500 G Attracts specific fish
Aquarium (Small) 1,000 G Furniture

📜 Key Quests

  • • Fishing Beginner: The tutorial quest. She gives you a broken rod and asks you to repair it. Unlocks the Fishing Skill.
  • • Cat on the Roof: A daily quest where you must feed a stray cat fish.
  • • Winter Events: In Winter, she moves to Old Sea/Snow Village for special Frostspore fish events.

Fun Fact: The "Vanya Bear" snow sculpture (Level 5 Snow Sculpting) is named after her!
