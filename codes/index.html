📱 How to Redeem Heartopia Codes

  1. 1
    Launch Heartopia

    Open the game on PC, iOS, or Android.

  2. 2
    Open Watch Menu

    Press TAB on PC, or tap the watch icon (top-right) on mobile.

  3. 3
    Go to Settings

    Click the gear icon ⚙️ to open Settings.

  4. 4
    Click "Redeem Code"

    In the General tab, find the "Redeem Code" button (bottom-right).

  5. 5
    Enter Code & Redeem

    Type or paste the code, then click "Redeem". Codes are case-insensitive.

  6. ✓
    Collect from Mailbox

    Rewards are sent to your in-game Mailbox (in front of your house).
