📱 How to Redeem Heartopia Codes

  1. 1
    Launch Heartopia

    Open the game on PC, iOS, or Android.

  2. 2
    Open Watch Menu

    Press TAB on PC, or tap the watch icon (top-right) on mobile.

  3. 3
    Go to Settings

    Click the gear icon ⚙️ to open Settings.

  4. 4
    Click "Redeem Code"

    In the General tab, find the "Redeem Code" button (bottom-right).

  5. 5
    Enter Code & Redeem

    Type or paste the code, then click "Redeem". Codes are case-insensitive.

  6. ✓
    Collect from Mailbox

    Rewards are sent to your in-game Mailbox (in front of your house).

❓ Heartopia Codes FAQ

How often are Heartopia codes updated?

We refresh this page whenever new redeem codes appear or older codes stop working. The current list was checked on July 8, 2026.

Why does a Heartopia code say invalid?

The code may have expired, already been claimed on your account, been typed with an extra space, or not be active for your platform or region yet. Copy the code exactly, restart the game, and then try the next code in the list.

Where do Heartopia code rewards go?

After redemption, rewards are usually delivered to the in-game mailbox near your house. If nothing appears immediately, reopen the mailbox after changing areas or restarting the game.

Can I submit a working Heartopia code?

Yes. Use the code submission form on this page and include the reward, source, platform, and claim result if you have them. New submissions are checked before they are added to the active list.
